## What S02 gets right

### Livia is finally a person

The tea, two almonds, sacrificial-love joke, accurate observation, sponsor
liability, and access grant coexist. Livia is funny, tired, proud of Mara,
professionally acquisitive, and genuinely hurt by the refusal. Care is not a
decorative prelude to coercion; the apparent gift is the mechanism that binds
Mara to overnight labor and exposure.

### Epistemology becomes plot

The interface distinguishes pulse evidence, observer annotation, model-assisted
completion, human acceptance, and consent withdrawal. “Raw” data turns out to
contain interpretation. This is the project's theory dramatized through a
screen, a click, and institutional consequences instead of a seminar speech.

### Prayer causes an action

The brief address to Christ neither tranquilizes Mara nor tells her the answer.
It interrupts her preferred argumentative prayer and lets her notice the
participant-controls link. The practice changes attention; changed attention
creates a choice; the choice stops collection. That is excellent instructional
causality.

### Jonah becomes accountable and desirable

He helped build the compromised annotation layer, reported the defect
anonymously, and still let Mara enter. He acknowledges the withdrawal and loses
access. On the walk he does not rebrand those facts as heroic restraint. Mara's
anger does not cancel attraction; it gives it specificity. His immediate stop
when she places a palm on his chest is hotter than an abstract consent speech.

### The intimacy has a story function

The crooked first contact, laugh, tightening hand, small astonished sound, and
wanted stop use selective physical detail. The stopping rule grows from the
same epistemic failure as the plot: when either says “raw,” the other stops
annotating and asks. Breakfast supplies a concrete HFN. The blank-source door
event then reopens the thriller without collapsing ambiguity into the
paranormal.

## S02 questions for human readers

These are appraisal questions, not reasons to regenerate:

1. **Miriam arrives underintroduced.** A reader can infer that she is the
   neutral observer, but the story has not made her memorable before she pays a
   cost. One unobtrusive identifier earlier may be warranted.
2. **The administrative chain is highly efficient.** Participant control,
   witness signature, operator acknowledgment, and access suspension all do
   exact plot work. Ask whether this feels satisfyingly causal or visibly
   engineered.
3. **Confession to kiss is fast.** Jonah offers the anonymous report and Mara
   authors the kiss, which makes the transition defensible. Readers should say
   whether anger has acquired erotic edges or accountability has been rewarded
   too quickly.
4. **“Raw” is almost suspiciously elegant.** It is earned by the telemetry
   argument and works in dialogue, but the final repetition at the mystery hook
   may feel like a crafted thematic click. Test it; do not pre-emptively blunt
   it.

## Scaffolding judgment

The atom boundary is the first scaffold change that produced a qualitative
jump. Its success should be stated precisely:

1. Verbalized Sampling and local high-temperature draws explored causal
   possibilities.
2. Best-of-eight found a useful institutional mechanism.
3. The harness froze sparse observable atoms and their provenance.
4. Donor wording, scores, model identity, probabilities, and future beats were
   removed from the novelist packet.
5. A stronger novelist owned the sentences.
6. Atom realization and literary quality were checked separately.

This is not yet a fully automated base-to-frontier production pipeline. The
successful writer boundary is honestly recorded as
`codex-agent-manual-boundary.v1`, not a provider call. The architecture is
validated; the provider-neutral novelist/critic adapter and automatic bounded
repair acceptance still need to be wired before claiming reproducible
end-to-end automation.
