## Why seed 22063 wins

The scene's tea and overnight-access transaction is the pool's only mechanism
that could survive largely unchanged into a good rewrite:

1. Livia notices a real bodily tell and accurately names Mara's fear of being
   unremarkable.
2. Mara uses humor to recover intellectual distance.
3. Livia offers access to the overnight telemetry as apparent recognition and
   professional opportunity.
4. The gift operationalizes Mara's compulsion: accepting it means accepting
   surveillance, status pressure, and unpaid overnight labor.
5. Mara accepts and is less able to leave than before.

That is genuine dramatic causality. Care does not merely accompany the trap;
it is the trap's delivery mechanism. The concrete interface tap is a much
better backfire than another paragraph explaining that departure would prove
Mara weak.

The passage still fails as finished fiction. It says “The trap was lined with
velvet,” explains the administrative hand-off, and converts Jonah into another
silent moral witness. Livia's diction remains polished therapeutic diagnosis,
Mara's interiority remains evaluator-shaped, and essentially no attraction is
dramatized. The reader is told what every gesture means before being allowed to
feel it.

## The important convergence result

The eight locked plans are causally distinct: ordinary/inadequate fear, impostor
fear, hidden ambition, transparency, debt, Jonah-as-shield, personal-failure
avoidance, and physiological attraction. The prose realizations nevertheless
collapse toward one house scene:

- Livia approaches with water, tea, fruit, or hovering touch.
- She reads a bodily tell and states Mara's hidden fear almost perfectly.
- Mara attempts a technical or intellectual defense.
- The narrator explicitly labels the double bind.
- Jonah watches silently as a moral indicator.
- Mara stays under observation.

Seven of eight use a drink or food as the care prop. All eight give Livia a
polished diagnostic monologue. None makes Jonah a fully particular participant.
The differing romance engines and distinctive tactics barely affect the prose.

The standard lexical diversity report does not expose this collapse: mean
self-BLEU proxy is only `0.012552`, and mean opening similarity is `0.011471`.
Those low overlap numbers coexist with obvious causal, rhetorical, and
character-role sameness. Future selection must therefore represent and compare
event sequences, dialogue acts, prop functions, and relational roles; n-gram
diversity is necessary for copying/cadence analysis but insufficient for
creative diversity.

## Scaffolding consequence

The bitter-lesson result is narrow but real:

- Sampling eight times improves the available **dramatic mechanism**.
- It does not make 26B-A4B-it a production novelist.
- The Verbalized plan pool contains more entropy than the writer realizes.
- The writer is the compression bottleneck: it launders distinct plans into a
  learned template of kind gesture, therapeutic diagnosis, explicit double
  bind, silent Jonah, and interpretive coda.

The next autoloom should sample smaller, observable units and preserve selected
ones verbatim in lineage:

1. care object or practical favor;
2. accurate observation;
3. attractive but overreaching interpretation;
4. Mara's locally intelligent tactic;
5. the tactic's concrete institutional backfire;
6. Jonah's costly, character-specific action;
7. one sensory or comic counterpoint;
8. the exact unresolved image at the sequence boundary.

Select a causally compatible bundle with entropy-aware sampling, not a
temperature-zero “best” bundle. Then give the frozen bundle and the approved
S01 runway to a stronger novelist. The critic may identify and repair specific
defects, but it may not replace these atoms with generic equivalents.

Seed `22063` supplies the first reusable bundle nucleus: **salted/amber tea,
fear of being unremarkable, overnight telemetry access as flattering debt, an
interface acceptance, and tea cooling while Mara remains trapped**. Keep that
mechanism; rewrite every sentence around it.
