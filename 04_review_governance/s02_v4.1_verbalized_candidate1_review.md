## What the sample establishes

- **Plan repair worked.** Mara's refusal creates the exit condition; Miriam
  protects it; the story keeps ordinary explanations available for the final
  record anomaly.
- **Macro pacing worked.** Sequence one ends with fewer choices, sequence two
  produces Mara's refusal, and sequence three attempts the protected exit,
  walk, stopping kiss, and hook.
- **Transport worked.** There was no server deadlock, prompt leakage, or
  competing 31B client.  Later sequence prefills benefited from longest-common-
  prefix reuse.
- **Prose realization failed.** The model repeatedly states the scene's method,
  tells the reader what an action means, and substitutes generic romance
  language for character-specific heat.

## Controller evidence

The raw doorway segment was only 35 words above its 1,400-word ceiling.  The
old controller nevertheless launched a 2,800-token whole-sequence compression.
That response produced only a short partial replay and then required an endpoint
rescue.  The rescue was intentionally stopped after the literary rejection;
the failed call is preserved and no candidate was committed.

The shared controller now has a small-overrun tail-surgery path and an explicit
sequence checkpoint.  Future expensive arms should stop after sequence one
unless the passage passes both mechanics and an adversarial literary preflight.

## Required realization changes

1. Remove evaluator-shaped phrases and moral conclusions from novelist-visible
   beat text.  Supply observable action coordinates, not interpretive labels.
2. Reject sequence one when it explains its causal mechanism, flattens Livia,
   or crosses the batch cadence threshold; do not spend two more dense calls.
3. Give prayer a concrete provenance and embodied execution.  Do not ask the
   model to manufacture generic “Christian practice” from a rubric slot.
4. Make the romance diagnostic adversarial: identify the hottest specific
   action and the most generic heat sentence.  If only the latter exists, reject.
5. Keep v3 Verbalized planning.  Its diversity and causal repair are not the
   source of this failure; the shared realization context is.

## v4.2 sequence-local ablation

The next checkpoint removed full-scene beats and continuity, future-event
previews, evaluator-shaped beat wording, and nonlocal plan fields.  It produced
960 words with the correct endpoint, zero packet leakage, and zero exact
four-gram overlap with the control language.  It nevertheless worsened to 15
cadence-family hits, or 15.63 per 1,000 words.  The prose still announces that
“the kindness was an ambush,” explains the trap's two branches, and uses Jonah
as a silent moral indicator.

This falsifies the narrow hypothesis that prompt-label leakage was the primary
cause of the house style.  Sequence-local projection is still the correct
control architecture, but the next quality experiment must change the prose
distribution itself: bounded positive exemplars, stronger author/persona
conditioning, or a different writer checkpoint.  No further 31B-it candidate
should be generated from the current anonymous-profile realization prior.

## v4.3 26B-A4B-it writer-prior ablation

The same sequence-local packet and the same locked *Moral Mirror* plan were
then realized by the intended 26B-A4B instruction checkpoint.  This is a clean
writer-prior comparison: seed 22011, temperature 0.9, top-p 0.95, min-p 0.02,
one raw-completion call, and no repair.  The checkpoint is preserved under
`s02-compute-v4.3-a4b` with an honest `generator` model role.

The 26B model was fast and mechanically tidy: 1,005 words in one natural
completion, no packet marker leakage, no control four-gram overlap, and a
concrete care object (salted almonds).  Its dramatic and literary performance
was worse:

- It violates the locked endpoint by ending the session and putting Mara alone
  in the hallway.  Sequence one required Mara to remain inside with the session
  still underway.
- Livia states the entire diagnosis and double bind aloud.  Her manipulation is
  explicit coercive-therapy dialogue, not socially plausible mixed care and
  appetite.
- Jonah again functions as a silent moral lamp.  His weight shift becomes “a
  closing of a circuit” without creating a specific relational consequence.
- The narrator repeatedly explains the implication, the social cost, Mara's
  maneuver, and the final meaning after the action has already supplied them.
- Cadence-family density rises again: 18 hits in 1,005 words, or 17.91 per
  1,000.  This includes four not-X-but-Y constructions, three uses each of
  “merely,” “simply,” and “sudden,” two “felt like” constructions, and an
  interpretive “Mara realized” coda.
