# Autoloom prompt findings v3

Date: 2026-08-03  
Status: active research record

## V12 falsification

V12 demonstrated that trajectory sampling itself works: fixed stochastic seeds produced materially different approaches, stage outputs were durably resumable, and seeded quality–distance selection did not collapse to deterministic top-k. It did not demonstrate a good intimacy generator.

The first four transaction probes exposed the controlling defect. Three branches remained in a forensic scar-inspection attractor. The fourth reached explicit intercourse, but did so through anatomical checklist dialogue and generic completion phrases. Therefore the observed failure was not absence of entropy or simply missing length. Entropy was being spent inside the wrong conditional distribution.

The V12 transaction gate also confounded explicit vocabulary with successful erotic transaction. It could reject the cooler but causally active branches while admitting generic mechanical sex. A primary-agent close read overrode the stochastic approach selection and preserved both decisions as separate artifacts.

## V13 mutation

V13 changes one declared axis: stage-local prompt conditioning.

Before transaction prose, the 31B base model samples a `TransactionProgram` containing:

- offer;
- counteroffer;
- consent turn;
- body logic;
- sensory asymmetry;
- disclosure;
- plot payment;
- ending state.

These records are learned from content-neutral back-translations of prompt-eligible intimacy scenes, then conditioned on the selected approach and its causal program. Three records per approach are selected stochastically, without replacement, using a seeded quality × semantic-distance distribution. The same base model realizes the selected record as prose. No instruction compiler rewrites it.

The prose prompt places the local transaction record immediately before `MANUSCRIPT`, then ends on the exact accumulated manuscript. Raw spicy prose remains earlier in the apprenticeship archive, and the contemporary bridge remains nearest the target. Control syntax never follows the manuscript boundary.

Length is allocated across the stochastic trajectory rather than repaired after a monolithic draw:

- approach: 180–280 words;
- transaction: 520–700 words;
- aftermath: 160–260 words.

The transaction diagnostic now requires visible agency, plot payment, at least three sensory channels, and physical progression in addition to embodied contact and explicitness. It penalizes newly observed generic completion phrases. A failure at a fixed seed is terminal evidence, preventing deterministic retry loops.

## Prediction and falsifier

Prediction: compared with V12, V13 transaction branches will vary in initiating action, bodily geometry, discovery, and resulting decision; at least one branch will make explicit intimacy causally inseparable from the drive conflict without falling into scar cataloguing or generic climax prose.

Falsifier: if the transaction pool still converges on inspection, confession-as-therapy, or interchangeable intercourse despite distinct valid transaction programs, the remaining attractor is in the prose apprenticeship/runway rather than the latent program layer. The next mutation should then replace the contemporary project bridge or change the nearest source-pair topology, not add more control fields.

## V14–V16 topology result

The latent-program parser initially rejected useful base-model surface variation. Replaying the preserved raw draws through a broader parser recovered distinct transaction programs without another model call. This established an important separation: schema conformance was a harness property, not evidence that the underlying samples lacked diversity.

V16 then changed the nearest-example topology. The strongest mode-matched source scene was placed after the contemporary bridge, immediately before the compact transaction memo and manuscript continuation. This increased causal intimacy: bodily action began to expose or alter the drive conflict rather than merely ornamenting it. It also exposed a competing conditioning effect. The nearest source can dominate cadence and scene mechanics, while the nearest bridge can propagate the project's explanatory house style. More context is therefore useful only when its order and local function are controlled.

## V17 sampling-topology result

Word-band failure was partly a stop-topology bug, not simply weak length obedience. Approach and transaction prompts had taught the base model a scene-ending sentinel, encouraging it to terminate locally complete fragments after roughly 100–150 words. V17 removed learned end markers from intermediate stages and allocated separate stochastic budgets:

- approach: 160–230 words / 300 tokens;
- transaction: 420–560 words / 780 tokens;
- aftermath: 100–170 words / 300 tokens.

The resulting transaction pool produced eight complete in-band checkpoints from nine usable draws. This is the strongest operational result of the campaign: the model could obey the band when the prompt represented the requested text as a middle trajectory rather than a self-contained scene.

The result also falsifies the idea that temperature-zero selection caused convergence. Prose was sampled at nonzero temperature; stage selection used a seeded quality-by-semantic-distance distribution without replacement. Reproducibility governed selection lineage, not token generation.

## V18–V19 evaluator correction and finalist

The first transaction selector undercounted valid branches because its lexical completion heuristic treated ordinary uses of “came” as orgasm while missing less formulaic completion language. V18/V19 separated anatomical progression, completed intimacy, agency, plot payment, and motif saturation, and stored the exact evidence spans behind each signal. Replaying the preserved V17 candidates off-model yielded two genuinely eligible transaction branches rather than regenerating prose.

Three sampled aftermath branches produced one eligible ending. The selected raw finalist is 974 words and passes every hard gate except the explicit heat floor: it dramatizes intercourse and clitoral touch, then skips directly to “Afterward.” The gap is real, not a detector artifact.

A five-defect direct Codex edit produced a mechanically compliant counterfactual. Because its prose was written by Codex rather than generated by the Gemma scaffold, it is excluded from promotion, ranking, human finalist packages, and every claim about model capability. Its sole legitimate use is to turn the five observed failures into explicit prompt/scaffold tests. Only a fresh Gemma generation can establish that the scaffold learned the repair.

## Current conclusion

The most important prompt variables observed so far are, in order:

1. **Manuscript boundary:** end on actual prose, never control syntax.
2. **Trajectory semantics:** tell the base model it is writing the middle of an unfolding sequence when a local stop is unwanted.
3. **Nearest exemplar:** place the mode-matched spicy scene nearest the target when heat mechanics matter.
4. **Causal latent:** sample an offer/counteroffer/consent/body/disclosure/payment program before prose, without an instruction compiler laundering it.
5. **Entropy budget:** sample multiple latents and multiple realizations; use seeded stochastic diversity selection, not greedy top-k.
6. **Evaluator evidence:** store exact evidence spans and keep eligibility separate from literary quality.

The remaining weakness is prose taste. The base loom now transfers causal intimacy more reliably, but it still produces abstract relationship summaries, blurred blocking, and generic erotic compression. The next research comparison must be between fresh Gemma outputs from controlled prompt mutations. The direct counterfactual must not appear in a blind manuscript comparison.

## V21–V22 scaffold-only correction

Review of the direct counterfactual exposed an evidentiary mistake: a Codex-written repair cannot demonstrate a generation capability. The counterfactual was therefore marked non-scaffold, barred from promotion and human packages, and retained only as a defect specification.

Those defects were then projected back into a single prompt mutation. V21 kept the checkpoint, source archive, nearest-example topology, approaches, transaction latents, seed policy, and sampler fixed. The transaction prompt alone changed:

- restored the sampled `body_logic`, `sensory_asymmetry`, and `ending_state` fields that the source-nearest branch had accidentally omitted;
- required concrete enacted conflict rather than relationship-policy summary;
- required legible positions and changes of initiative;
- required completed intimacy rather than an `Afterward` jump;
- required the sampled ending state to become a visible choice.

Fresh Gemma generation produced six complete transaction checkpoints from nine terminal attempts. An initial stage diagnostic reported one eligible sample, but trace review found another evaluator false positive: “Esther released his thigh” and “they came back together” were counted as sexual completion. V22 corrected the evidence matcher and replayed the untouched V21 prose without inference. Final result: **0/6 eligible raw transactions**.

This is a negative prompt result. Restoring latent fields improved plot-payment density and yielded an 846-word assembled scene with clean anti-copy results, but did not reliably produce physically lucid or actually completed intimacy. The selected fallback contains malformed geometry (“felt him open wider around her scream”), source-motif overtransfer (salt/scars/heat patterns), and generic procedural action. It must not be promoted or direct-edited into a claimed scaffold success.

## V23–V25 retrieved-apprenticeship result

V23 introduced a local literary-scene index and placed eight retrieved scenes before the existing target-near bridge/source packet. Source provenance is descriptive data only; generated prose is admitted by the ordinary anti-copy index. Calibration and holdout partitions remain separate solely to preserve experimental validity.

The first retrieval method incorrectly inherited book-level tags such as `embodied-intimacy` onto every scene. It therefore selected prestigious relationship passages that were not actually erotic. The first Gemma draw became compressed, bloodless, and ended with hallucinated paratext. This falsified topical/book-level retrieval as a sufficient apprenticeship selector.

V24 changed the retrieval geometry rather than editing prose:

- scene windows narrowed from 1,500 to roughly 950 words;
- intimacy-action and explicit-intimacy densities became scene-level features;
- book facets no longer impersonated scene properties;
- the prompt targeted one author, D. H. Lawrence, rather than enforcing author diversity;
- explicit embodied intimacy and a minimum transaction-heat score became retrieval constraints;
- the transaction generation ceiling rose from 780 to 1,050 tokens because the former left almost no sentence-boundary margin at the 420-word floor;
- paratext leakage became a mechanical hard failure.

Three fresh Gemma draws from the same 13,777-token prompt were 537, 548, and 547 words. All were anti-copy-clean. All three were markedly hotter and more physically explicit than the prior prompt distribution while retaining consent moves and the drive/security plot. The third draw joined intercourse to the compromised-house problem and ended in mutual completion plus a plan to remove private papers that night. Its remaining literary defects are generic erotic diction, occasional awkward anatomy, over-explicit thematic dialogue, and some dominance gestures insufficiently prepared by the preceding movement.

Trace review caught two more mechanical undercounts: the evaluator missed `his seed burst` / `their release` as completion and ignored courier, service, compromised house, papers, and removal tonight as plot payment. V25 repaired those evidence families and replayed the byte-identical V24 prose, including the retrieved scenes in the replay anti-copy index. Result: **1/3 eligible**, selecting `transaction.approach.943038.1.3` with zero inference calls during re-evaluation.

This is the first positive evidence that curated raw-prose apprenticeship changes the base model's erotic distribution in the intended direction. It is not yet evidence of superlative prose. The next controlled mutation should back-translate each selected source scene into its transaction graph, reject mismatched scenes through a model-assisted local reranker, and compare raw-only versus graph→raw pairs using the same three seeds. The V24 raw texts—not any direct Codex rewrite—are the only manuscript evidence for this result.
