# Base Autoloom Prompt Findings v2

Date: 2026-08-03

## Decision

Do not promote any current manuscript to human review. The best current recipe
establishes that a naturalistic prose runway improves explicit realization, but
none of the tested recipes jointly satisfies scene length, closure, prose
quality, embodied clarity, and causal consequence.

The next production scaffold is staged stochastic continuation:

1. Sample twelve 180–280-word approach trajectories from one cache-stable
   apprenticeship prefix; retain four.
2. Sample embodied transactions from each retained approach; retain two.
3. Sample aftermaths from each retained transaction; retain one or more
   Pareto-eligible complete scenes.
4. Apply local mechanical, anti-copy, cadence, motif-saturation, and literary
   checks at every boundary. Use an instruction/frontier model only to judge or
   perform a bounded repair after raw scenes exist.

This is inference-time search over coherent manuscript trajectories. It is not
temperature-zero compilation and not whole-scene instruction-model laundering.

## Causal inferences

- Temperature is necessary for breadth but insufficient for length or mode
  compliance. The observed stop distribution contains distinct failure modes:
  immediate learned delimiter, natural short close, usable close, and cap
  exhaustion.
- Finished-scene demonstrations alter the stopping distribution. Raw spicy
  prose alters heat realization. Neither guarantees a good manuscript.
- Detailed causal plans dominate broad heat labels. Detailed but independent
  causal and intimacy plans can collide and get averaged into abstraction.
- Factorizing causal and intimacy proposal distributions is useful only when
  both remain anchored to the same target objects, people, and decision.
- Long context is not automatically better. The 11.7K prompt was worse than
  the 4.4K naturalistic runway because its many graph/control blocks formed a
  bureaucratic textual attractor.
- A base model is most steerable at the manuscript boundary. Natural prose
  prefill moved explicit realization more than repeated metadata did.
- Ordinary n-gram repetition checks miss semantic motif loops. Future
  preflight must expose cadence families and motif saturation before expensive
  continuation or editing.

## Falsifier for staged search

Reject staged continuation as the next architecture if a twelve-seed approach
pool fails to produce at least four trajectories that are simultaneously
target-anchored, non-generic, non-repetitive, and causally open; or if surviving
transactions remain less coherent than the best whole-scene naturalistic
runway draw after blind review.
