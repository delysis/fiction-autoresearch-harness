## What changed the quality frontier

The best-of-eight 26B-A4B pool found one useful mechanism: Livia turns accurate
recognition into overnight telemetry access, Mara accepts the flattering grant,
and an administrative click makes departure costlier. The local prose remained
unusable. Its best draw scored 7.82 cadence-family hits per 1,000 words and
still explained the trap, flattened Jonah, and supplied no material heat.

The harness therefore preserved six local occurrences in an immutable atom
bundle and removed donor wording, plan labels, probabilities, scores, future
beats, and raw model identity from the novelist packet. The stronger novelist
owned every sentence. The resulting three sequence rates are 0.0, 2.28, and
0.0 cadence-family hits per 1,000 words.

Two independent Codex readers accepted S02 for human appraisal. Both found
that the continuation crossed the literary floor by a wide margin and was
decisively stronger than bootstrap finalist B. Their shared findings:

- Livia is funny, caring, perceptive, acquisitive, wounded, and professionally
  exposed rather than a coercion theorem.
- Mara's pride, hunger, humor, prayer, anger, desire, and technical competence
  all cause choices.
- Jonah built part of the compromised system, reported it anonymously, let
  Mara enter anyway, pays with his access, apologizes without self-exoneration,
  and visibly wants more when the kiss stops.
- Prayer supplies one act of attention rather than instant peace or a sermon.
- The interface cleanly distinguishes physiological evidence, assisted model
  completion, human acceptance, observer interpretation, and withdrawal.
- “Raw” becomes a character-specific stopping-and-asking practice rather than
  a detachable chastity maxim.
- The blank-source 1:17 door event preserves clock error, ordinary human action,
  system corruption, and a stranger remainder.

## Remaining human-appraisal questions

The administrative control chain is unusually efficient: participant control,
witness, operator acknowledgment, and suspension each deliver a required
consequence. It is causal and plausible within Fulcrum, but readers should be
asked whether it feels engineered. Miriam is still the least individualized
major character. The confession-to-kiss transition is fast, although Mara
authors it and Jonah offers the anonymous report before contact.

These are reader questions, not grounds for another generation pass.
