## Why B won

Candidate B received two independent accept decisions. Its decisive advantage
is causal rather than cosmetic: Mara repeats the same `ARRIVAL` target while
altering posture, breathing, knee position, and gaze. Livia's read changes
with the cue channel, so the scene demonstrates that accuracy does not settle
mechanism. At the same time, attraction remains active and the method itself
becomes part of what Mara desires.

The candidate also gives the cleanest bridge into S02. Jonah enables the
recording apparatus while publicly resisting performance; Miriam converts an
improvised control into an institutional record; and the sealed wrist stream
creates a concrete object over which access, interpretation, and care can be
bargained four nights later. Livia remains funny, accurate, attentive, and
acquisitive rather than collapsing into either villain or therapist.

## Bounded v2 repairs

The raw winner remains preserved. Four local repairs created v2:

1. Jonah's implication is shown through his handling of the contacts and
   recording rather than explained by the narrator.
2. Mara's prayer during the control removes an alibi and makes her admit that
   she wants more than method; it does not suppress desire or supply an answer.
3. Miriam's `IMPROVISED CONTROL` notation shows institutional appropriation as
   an act instead of a gloss.
4. A delayed post-contact pressure mark replaces a coincidence that leaned too
   hard toward a psychic explanation. The cuff is open, Mara denies touching
   it, Jonah offers sensor rebound, and Miriam leaves that explanation possible
   but incomplete.

An independent acceptance read found all four changes genuinely narrow and
recommended no further generative rewrite.
