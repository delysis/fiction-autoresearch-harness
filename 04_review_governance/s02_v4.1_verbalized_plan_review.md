# S02 v4.1 Verbalized-plan review

Status: the compact-v2 sampling calls succeeded. Both ended naturally, all 16
strategy objects parsed, all 16 were unique after deduplication, and eight were
selected with no causal-near-duplicate rejection. The first apparent failure
was a scaffold bug: the full-story validator required each variable plan to
repeat the invariant walk-home and doorway-kiss ending. That invariant now
enters the deterministic program view without altering the sampled JSON.

The resulting `locked_plan_set.v1.json` is a valid audit artifact, but it is not
approved for prose generation. Diversity selection admitted several plans with
weak dramatic engines. A v2 plan set must pass quality screening before the
quality-diversity selection.

## Critical ranking of the 16 sampled plans

### Strongest raw material

- **The Moral Mirror — B+.** The flattering label, Mara's refusal to confirm
  Livia's narrative, and Miriam's loss of neutrality form one causal engine.
  Jonah's public contradiction can be a real cost if the prose makes his
  institutional standing concrete. The recurring phrase is ambiguous enough
  to retain ordinary explanations.
- **The Vulnerability Gambit — B.** Honest inadequacy can plausibly make Mara
  less useful to Livia while deepening intimacy. It needs a less therapeutic
  institutional aftermath and a cost for Jonah beyond admitting superiority.
- **The Social Paradox — B.** Becoming deliberately uninteresting is an unusual
  action rather than a slogan. Miriam giving up seniority is material. The
  heart-rate correlation needs an ordinary measurement explanation and the
  menial-role mechanics need to fit the actual Fulcrum setting.
- **The Irony Collapse — B-.** “Understood is not loved” is emotionally fertile,
  and an ugly honest reaction could be active rather than serene. The mirror
  image is gothic shorthand, Miriam as “bad cop” is vague, and Jonah merely
  dropping irony is not yet a cost.

### Promising only after causal repair

- **The Debt of Kindness — B-.** Genuine empathy used to create obligation is
  the best Livia premise in the pool. A technical alarm is a cheap bailout;
  Jonah's intuition is not evidence; the impossible recording is too blatant.
- **The Calibration Error — B-.** Cue noise directly serves the story's
  epistemic mechanism, and attraction complicates Mara's control. The “social
  contradiction,” Miriam's insult, and Jonah's anonymity remain placeholders.
- **The Logic of Silence — C+.** Refusing the channel is clean and active, and
  Miriam burning a bridge is material. “Reacting to a word Mara only thought”
  confirms telepathy instead of preserving competing explanations.
- **The Sensory Breach — C+.** It is the only plan that foregrounds heat as a
  causal force. Changing rooms, losing composure, a felt tension “from afar,”
  and an unexplained scent are atmosphere rather than a sufficient event chain.
- **The Protocol Breach — C.** Surveillance versus intimacy is usable, and
  Jonah's unauthorized monitoring creates necessary friction. The security
  override and untouched console are mechanical conveniences.
- **The Vulnerability Gamble — C.** Livia's steady pulse is a good observable
  remainder. The rest tends toward group-therapy shorthand, and every cost is
  phrased as a warning or admission.

### Reject for this run

- **The Proprietary Pivot — C-.** A blackout, deleted evidence, generic security
  audit, and mystery login stack plot machinery without increasing character
  truth. Mara causes failure accidentally rather than choosing her exit.
- **The Mirror Trap — C-.** Spiritual indebtedness is on-theme, but nearly every
  field is abstract. Miriam's secret and Jonah's prior misunderstanding are
  unspecified; the forgotten note is a stock uncanny device.
- **The Proxy War — D+.** It shortcuts the triangle into an “us against the
  world” alliance, gives Jonah a cost-free privacy choice, and makes Mara's exit
  depend on him. This would flatten Livia and romantic friction simultaneously.
- **The Sacred Boundary — D+.** It invites a detachable sermon and treats
  conscience as a magic override. “Shared dream,” “spiritual struggle,” and the
  institution acknowledging a limit are conclusions rather than dramatized
  consequences.
- **The Ritual Rupture — D.** Prayer causes a temperature drop, effectively
  confirming the supernatural and turning the Christian practice into a spell.
  The predicted institutional reform is frictionless.
- **The Protocol Paradox — D.** A forgotten rule forces recess, everyone accepts
  the new Doorway Rule, Jonah apologizes, and an object moves by itself. This is
  a chain of conveniences, not earned agency or mystery.

## Scaffold changes required before prose

1. Screen structural validity first, then plan quality, then diversity. Novelty
   is a constraint on the high-quality set, not a substitute for quality.
2. Judge plans on dramatic causality, character truth, costly agency, romantic
   voltage, epistemic ambiguity, and prose affordance. A confession, apology,
   warning, or risk is not a cost unless the plan specifies what is lost.
3. Penalize external bailouts (alarms, blackouts, unexplained hardware failure),
   instant institutional self-correction, alliance shortcuts, sermon-ready
   abstractions, and observations that confirm paranormal causation.
4. Give the novelist a concrete event brief, not the planner's abstract labels.
   Keep `epistemic_turn`, `romantic_engine`, and `relationship_delta` for
   selection/evaluation; exposing them during prose realization invites thesis
   sentences and emotionally smoothed outcomes.
5. Preserve the 16 sampled JSON objects and v1 lockfile unchanged. Produce a
   separately hashed v2 lockfile after blind plan judging and quality-diversity
   selection. Do not generate from v1.

## Legacy-prose diagnostic motivating the shorter realization brief

An `interpretive_coda` diagnostic now surfaces phrases such as “Mara realized,”
“she had found,” “for the first time,” and “she was no longer.” It is an
editorial lead, not a gate. Retrospective continuation-only counts in the
bootstrap batch were 32 hits across chat-direct records, 38 across raw
instruction-completion records, and 33 across base-program-to-instruction
records; every candidate was affected. Because the pattern spans all three
arms, the shared realization prompt is the leading cause. The v4.1 novelist
view therefore omits abstract plan conclusions and routes only four
scene-active source mechanisms while retaining the complete audit packet.

## Evaluation-rubric correction

The historical bootstrap continuation scorecards were generated with
`s01-romance-v2-gabaldon`. That rubric explicitly rewards the S01 wrist
exercise, cue-control reveal, and S01 pacing windows, so its compressed scores
in the high eighties and nineties cannot be treated as calibrated S02 quality.
They remain useful evidence traces and historical pairwise inputs only.

## v2 judge audit and v3 disposition

The two-pass blind judge completed, but its v2 selection is not approved for
prose.  The decisive failure was internal consistency rather than mere taste:
one pass described *The Ritual Rupture* as confirmed paranormal causation that
violated a hard limit while emitting `hard_reject: false`.  Similar evidence/
boolean contradictions affected *The Logic of Silence*, *The Calibration
Error*, and several external-bailout plans.  Since v2 trusted the boolean, it
promoted *The Ritual Rupture*, *The Proxy War*, and *The Sacred Boundary* over
plans the critical review rated materially stronger.

The parser now rejects this class of response instead of averaging through it.
The original calls, quality report, and v2 lockfile remain unchanged.  A
separately hashed adjudication policy records a decision for all sixteen plans,
and `locked_plan_set.v3.json` preserves each selected sampled payload alongside
a separately hashed causal-repair overlay.  Four selected kernels required an
explicit hard-reject resolution; none is treated as though its raw sampled
form passed.

The v3 set is:

1. *The Moral Mirror*
2. *The Vulnerability Gambit*
3. *The Social Paradox*
4. *The Irony Collapse*
5. *The Debt of Kindness*
6. *The Calibration Error*
7. *The Logic of Silence*
8. *The Sensory Breach*

This set follows the human critical ordering while retaining a deliberately
different heat-forward plan.  The overlays do only the repairs already demanded
by the rubric: Mara authors the exit, costs become losses, Fulcrum does not
instantly heal itself, and uncanny observations retain ordinary explanations.
They do not prescribe dialogue, imagery, paragraphing, or finished prose.

The lesson for later runs is not to add a larger judge prompt.  Keep stochastic
planning broad; make hard constraints deterministic; use model scores only to
rank survivors; and expose any human/editorial repair as a distinct artifact.

Future S02 scoring uses `s02-proof-story-v1`. It replaces the S01 mechanics with
accurate care becoming interpretive capture, a specifically Christian practice
yielding one next act, Mara-authored exit agency, situated costs for Miriam and
Jonah, character-specific stopping, and a concrete anomaly under competing
explanations. It also names the observed failure modes directly. No historical
score is silently migrated or compared numerically with the new rubric.
