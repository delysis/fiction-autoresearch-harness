# Diana Gabaldon excerpt control

This is a deliberately small, local-only conditioning fixture built from the
user-supplied copy of *I Give You My Body*. It is not a validated full-author
profile. Two excerpts from different novels support a bounded sequence-one
ablation; a third book excerpt is declared as an untouched holdout and is never
compiled, retrieved, or prompted.

The experiment transfers craft mechanisms—physical action as emotional
transaction, selective sensory logistics, embodied viewpoint, and
character-specific intimacy—into the Fulcrum story. Historical setting,
dialect, names, plots, scene order, and wording are excluded. Every prompted
word is included in the local anti-copy index, and the normal exact, fuzzy, and
streaming checks remain authoritative.
