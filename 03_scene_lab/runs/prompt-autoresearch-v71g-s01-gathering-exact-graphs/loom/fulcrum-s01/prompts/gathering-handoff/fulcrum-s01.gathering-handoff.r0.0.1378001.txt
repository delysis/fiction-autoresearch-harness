He reddened under the retort, but kept his eyes on her. "May is ready

There was a moment’s contemplative silence, then the confiding:

Birkin smiled to himself as he sat by the fire. When Ursula came down
he sat motionless, with his arms on his knees. She saw him, how he was
motionless and ageless, like some crouching idol, some image of a
deathly religion. He looked round at her, and his face, very pale and
unreal, seemed to gleam with a whiteness almost phosphorescent.

“Don’t you feel well?” she asked, in indefinable repulsion.

“I hadn’t thought about it.”

“But don’t you know without thinking about it?”

He looked at her, his eyes dark and swift, and he saw her revulsion. He
did not answer her question.

FULCRUM

A novel

Setting
Contemporary Fulcrum, a private psychotechnology institute studying attention and nonverbal cue-reading. Adult newcomer Mara is the only viewpoint. Livia's accuracy retains ordinary cue-reading explanations; Jonah's restraint costs him status and changes Mara's attention.

Principal characters
Mara Vale, twenty-two, an infrastructure-security engineer: sheltered, technically formidable, observant, and dryly funny. She wants difficult truth and fellowship, but resents any claim to know her without permission.
Adrian Voss, Fulcrum's polished founder: he makes status, method, and recruitment feel like hospitality.
Livia Sloane, Fulcrum's gifted reader of posture, voice, and timing: her care is real, her skill is real, and she wants ownership of the pace.
Jonah Reed, a hardware-security researcher: playful and socially fluent; he notices when a person becomes material for an audience and sometimes pays a public cost rather than perform her.

Continuity
Adult newcomer Mara is at the contemporary Northern California Fulcrum compound for the first time; she has never visited or interviewed here before. The blond man from the couch is adult Jonah Reed, and Mara now knows his name. Adrian has not yet entered the gathering. Livia has not yet joined the gathering. Mara is the only viewpoint. Unusual cue-reading always retains ordinary sensory and behavioral explanations.

Present movement payment
Arriving guests interrupt and reframe Mara's contact with Jonah. Adrian remains absent; the glass transaction has not begun.

Dramatic design for the next prose movement
Unnamed guests and their voices reach the common room, including the young fellow who will carry a glass. Let their arrival interrupt and reframe Mara's contact with Jonah. Adrian remains elsewhere and is neither seen nor mentioned; the glass transaction has not happened yet.

Manuscript so far
When Mara walked into the common room at six fifteen, someone was already there. Someone younger, it seemed.
She couldn’t make out the face behind the blond hair. But the body was fine enough, leaning back on the couch with bare legs crossed and arms flung in opposite directions, one palm pressing the padded wall and the other wrist resting on the low table beside him.
She stood stock-still by the doorway for a moment, aware of her breath and of his awareness that she was here. He stirred and tilted his head; a face turned toward her, expressionless.

The two pairs of eyes held each other through an entire exhale.
And then the young man had pushed himself up on the armrests, stretched to his full height, and strolled over to shake her hand.
“Mara?”
He was closer than she would have guessed from where he’d been sitting, broad-shouldered and strong-chinned in a white T-shirt, grinning at her through dark-framed glasses as he reached out for the handshake. She took it.
“Jonah,” he said.
“Hello,” she replied, and then they stood there, still shaking hands, not knowing what to do next.
