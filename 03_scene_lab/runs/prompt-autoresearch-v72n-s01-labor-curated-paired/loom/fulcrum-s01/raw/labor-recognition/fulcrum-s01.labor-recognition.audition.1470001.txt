"I'm Mara," said the woman behind her, holding out a hand. "We haven't met yet, have we?"
The brown-haired man took it without breaking eye contact. "No, we haven't." He looked from one to the other. "I'm Sam," he said, letting go and stepping back to lean on the table. "I don't think any of us have."

The three of them stood there for a moment, looking at each other. No one spoke. Mara shifted her feet and tried not to stare; the awkwardness was starting to become intolerable.
And then a woman came in through the door by the table, carrying a tray of clean plastic wine glasses. They hadn't heard her coming. She stepped into the circle without acknowledgment or announcement and reached down to retrieve Sam's empty cup.
