After the session Mara came out to a warm night, dry except for the smell of wet moss under the trees. Jonah was on the front steps beside a black pickup.

Mara turned as he closed the door. “I’ll walk home.”

“Okay.” The low voice didn’t ask for explanations.

Jonah closed his hand on the door’s metal bar and rested beside it. When she stood straight and met his eyes he offered a hand, palm up, wrist steady. His eyes said “I wait for you.”

Mara set her left hand in his and let their fingertips touch without squeezing. He held it with just enough pressure to balance her if she moved. His back straightened and the muscles around his eyes eased as if he’d just taken an easy breath.

The right edge of his jawline flexed at a point below the corner of his mouth. He hadn’t said “Come on.” She didn’t know he knew that one of her cues, but he didn’t wait to ask. He left the pickup and walked a small step, drawing her with him, and released her hand as she walked beside him.
