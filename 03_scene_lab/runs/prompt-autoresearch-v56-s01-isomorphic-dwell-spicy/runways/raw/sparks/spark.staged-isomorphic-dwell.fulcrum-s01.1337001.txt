The welcome party sprawled outside on Fulcrum’s redwood terrace. The moon was nearly full—as was Mara, thanks to a little liquid courage, as she approached the place she had come to find.
A small knot of people stood talking in front of the windows leading into an empty conference room; Adrian’s back was turned to her. On the ledge of a wall nearby rested a glass about half full with liquid. No one near it seemed to be drinking, and Mara watched for a moment as a catering worker emerged from the kitchen, looked at the glass, picked it up, and went away to empty it.
She took a breath to steel her nerves, stepped through the group of people, and went to the door of the conference room. “I’m Mara,” she said, “your infrastructure engineer. Do you have a moment?”
