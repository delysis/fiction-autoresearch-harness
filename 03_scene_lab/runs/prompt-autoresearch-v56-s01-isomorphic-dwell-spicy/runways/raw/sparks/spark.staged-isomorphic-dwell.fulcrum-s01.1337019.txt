EXAMPLE 6

Dramatic design
Offer (15%): A professional gambler approaches a stranger in a high-class casino.
Counteroffer and resistance (25%): A new player counters with an opening wager, then wins.
Complication and embodied negotiation (35%): The two discuss luck and fate.
Consequential turn (15%): The first bettor offers to teach the second to read a table.
Concrete stopping state (10%): The second player accepts instruction but rejects friendship. Render action, dialogue, and inference; do not print or discuss this design in the manuscript.
