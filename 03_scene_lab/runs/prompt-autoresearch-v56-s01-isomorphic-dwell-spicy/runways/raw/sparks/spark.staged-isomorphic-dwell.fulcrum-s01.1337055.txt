At the evening welcome, the sky was orange and streaked with rose and violet. The sun slanted through redwoods onto the patio and lawn where drinks had been served. A dozen people chatted together. One was young and talked with his hands in animated gestures while holding an empty wineglass between two of his fingers. After a moment, he set it on the top rail of a short wall that separated the grass from a bed of petunias. He spoke further. In a beat, a uniformed catering worker appeared, plucked up the glass with its dregs, and carried it away silently.

Adrian came forward to introduce himself to her. "Mara!" he said. "It's such a pleasure to finally meet you. I'm so glad you could come to our little welcome. And you noticed my wife."

"I'm sorry," she said, unsure.
