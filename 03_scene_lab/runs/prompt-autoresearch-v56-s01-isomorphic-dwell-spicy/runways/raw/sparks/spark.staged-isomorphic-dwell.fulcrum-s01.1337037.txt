“You must be Mara,” he said. He was dressed like the other man but for a pale-blue blazer—not uniform, but dress uniform—and he had a little bit of gray at his temples. He nodded and smiled as if to a beloved friend from his childhood. “I’m Adrian, cofounder.” He spoke in an upper-class American accent Mara had heard before, but not from anyone she knew.

She wondered whether she was supposed to call him sir. She wasn’t, he couldn’t make her, but she guessed he’d be annoyed if she called him Adrian. “Do you want me to show you around?”

She hesitated. In the end she went with him, because she had a feeling that she might need to know who worked at Fulcrum and how they treated each other before the tour was over.
