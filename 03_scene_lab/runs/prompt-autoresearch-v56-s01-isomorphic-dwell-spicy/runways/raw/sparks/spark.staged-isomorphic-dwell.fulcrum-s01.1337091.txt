At seven, she moved out to the patio. It was bigger than her whole house, the floor smooth gray flagstones in a running-bond pattern. A low stone wall defined it on two sides and the other edges were open, one onto the lawn, one onto the parking lot. There must be six hundred people here, she thought, with surprise. And so many of them were beautiful.

People mingled easily, laughing and talking, but there were no introductions or announcements yet. They had been invited to arrive between seven and nine and gather around for a drink before moving inside for dinner at ten.
