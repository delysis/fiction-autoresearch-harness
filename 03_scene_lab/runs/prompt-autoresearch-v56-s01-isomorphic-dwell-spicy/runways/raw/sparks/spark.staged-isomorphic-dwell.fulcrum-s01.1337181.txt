“What do you think?” asked Adrian, as his own hand withdrew. He turned to lead the way down the long driveway, between rows of gorse and tall fescue that ran for a quarter mile out to an automatic gate.

Mara followed him. “About Fulcrum’s vision?” she clarified.

“That would be nice,” said Adrian, but he was smiling, and the words felt off.

The glass disappeared behind them as they walked down the long drive toward a line of pine trees. There were a few other people scattered along the road, each being walked by one of the staff in similar fashion. Mara didn’t recognize any of them.

“About that?” she asked Adrian, gesturing to where the glass would have been, had she dared turn her head.
