
Her cheeks warmed and her heart skipped; she wished she could just sit down beside him and touch him. But he was still smiling that gentle smile, looking amused rather than pleased by the idea of her touch.

The room was too big for them both. She swallowed, realizing how awkward it must sound for her to want his name like this—without knowing anything about him. He wasn’t talking anymore either; just staring at her with those green-blue eyes that seemed impossibly open and warm.
There were more people here, though. Other guests in shirts and jeans or shorts stood around talking and laughing near the front door. A woman in a black cocktail dress came through from somewhere else; she recognized Adrian Voss but couldn’t hear what he was saying over all the noise. Then another figure appeared in his wake—a young man carrying an open glass of red wine—and he caught her attention.
She glanced back at Jonah, who was watching with mild amusement as she tried to identify the newcomer from this distance without looking too interested. He didn’t seem to have noticed her staring.

It wasn’t until they were closer that Mara recognized him as Adrian Voss: his silver hair styled back from his forehead like a lion’s mane; his broad shoulders filled out by the tailored black jacket; and above all else: those piercing hazel eyes that she remembered so well from photographs on social media.
She watched Adrian exchange pleasantries with whoever he was talking to; then Mara caught his eye as he turned around—and saw something unusual there in those steady hazel orbs. Some kind of excitement? Maybe even eagerness? It wasn’t until she was closer that it occurred to her: Adrian Voss was looking straight at her with unabashed interest.
"Hello," she said quietly, smiling nervously as her stomach turned over. "Hi," came the reply—followed immediately by a burst of laughter which sounded like wind chimes playing in the distance.
