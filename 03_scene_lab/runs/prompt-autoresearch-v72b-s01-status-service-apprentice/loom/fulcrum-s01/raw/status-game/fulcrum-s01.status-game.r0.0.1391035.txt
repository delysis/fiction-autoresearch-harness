
“Adrian?” the young woman called out. She wore a long, fitted dress and her voice was pitched to be heard at a distance; she moved in high heels with fluid steps and seemed at once confident and restless. “Where are you hiding now?”

A tall man came through the doorway behind her, leaning forward with hands clasped at the back of his head. “Right here,” he called out. His voice was deep and level; it sounded older than anything else about him, though perhaps that was just because Mara had never seen this person before. He smiled when he saw Mara standing there, still shaking hands with Jonah Reed—or at least they seemed like they might be shaking hands at first glance.

Jonah’s smile stiffened and then disappeared completely as Mara turned her head back toward him; now she could see his hand stretched out in front of her face without touching anything at all.

She watched it for another two seconds before taking another step forward to put some space between herself and this new person whose name she didn’t know yet but whose voice was still ringing softly off the walls around them both:
“What are you two doing?”
He walked toward them with a light step; Mara noticed that his shoes were dark brown leather loafers without laces or buckles, polished to a sheen across their surface so that each one looked as smooth as black glass reflecting back at her in its polished mirror finish. His dark eyes regarded hers steadily through his silver glasses frames while he spoke; even though Mara had never met this person before now, it seemed almost impossible not to take him seriously.

He reached out with both hands to pull her away from Jonah Reed, who was still holding one of her hands and watching them all in turn—including the young woman who had entered behind Adrian Voss’s tall frame:
“What’s going on?”
Her voice sounded worried as well; she came over to stand beside her husband with her head tilted down at an angle so that Mara could see right down into those dark brown eyes above her own—eyes that were now focused on Mara alone as though nothing else mattered anymore except for what she might be saying next.

“We’re just—”Mara stammered. “Jonah—” She pointed toward him; he was standing stiffly with his arms crossed in front of his chest now, looking tense and uncomfortable at being the center of attention like this—which made no sense because nobody else seemed particularly interested in them anymore either. Everyone was back where they had been before: talking together while Mara stood awkwardly by herself surrounded on every side by people who seemed very much more important than she did right now—
And then Adrian Voss stepped closer so that Mara could feel his warm breath brushing against her cheek as well when he whispered: “What were you two doing?”

She stared up at him blankly, blinking hard to try and clear away some of the tension in her face from having been surprised like this. The dark eyes behind those silver glasses frames regarded her steadily; they did not blink or break contact even for an instant while she looked away briefly toward the floor before turning back to stare back into those same eyes—
“We were—” She shook her head slightly, unable to remember anything else she had been saying or doing before now. Adrian Voss seemed like a person who expected answers; he waited patiently with his gaze fixed upon hers as though he knew she would eventually come up with something sensible enough for him to believe in it—
Or perhaps not. He let go of her hand after another long second passed without any reply forthcoming from Mara’s lips either: “I see,” he said softly when Mara finally met his gaze again through those same dark eyes behind those silver glasses frames once more. His smile seemed very small now compared with how big it had been before; perhaps this was just because there were so many other things happening right then in the common room around them both…
