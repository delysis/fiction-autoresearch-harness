He nodded against her palm, already remembering how this touch had felt at the beginning of their marriage when they first discovered what it meant to choose together. The same weight and heat, the same careful attention to her response as she let him feel how much was his responsibility now.

Simon lifted her hand again to trace the seal with his thumb before returning to the desk and recording their amended agreement. The inkwell sat empty by the bed where she had left it this morning. She remained still while he worked, watching him sign what they had already agreed through touch rather than speech.

Esther kept her eyes on Simon as he read from the note she had pulled from her sleeve: "We will not open a decision without each other again. Not for safety, not for curiosity, not because one of us knows how to do it better alone."

She placed both hands against his chest again, counting three times. One. Two. Three. The breath between them measured their agreement.
