

Simon brushed a hair from her cheek as if he might take it with him. "Shall we trust them?"

"Not to watch us. They have already done that."

"But not to enter?"

"No longer."

Esther stood between their beds and removed Simon's shirt with one precise gesture. She set the garment on her own pillow where the paper still showed dark where she had overfilled the inkwell.

Her touch remained constant as she removed the rest of his clothing.

"One question first," Simon said.

"One."

"Who did you tell about the house?"

Esther moved her head in the smallest acknowledgment. "The woman who rents from us."

Simon waited while her fingers explored his chest, the line of his spine, the hair on his forearms. "I will trust that choice," he said at last.
