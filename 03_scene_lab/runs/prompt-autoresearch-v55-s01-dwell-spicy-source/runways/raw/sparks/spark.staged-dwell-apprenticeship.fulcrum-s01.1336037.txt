The first night’s party, that Friday, was outdoors, with white lights draped in the trees. It was crowded but beautiful, and Mara was relieved to see so many others just arriving, their Fulcrum t-shirts still new. She’d known about the company for years—but only from outside. So much of their work was done in house, she’d had no chance to work on an internal project until now. She took a glass of sparkling wine and went out under the trees with a small group who’d just met each other.

They were standing on one side of a low brick wall that separated two levels, near the food. People talked with the casual excitement of an academic conference.
