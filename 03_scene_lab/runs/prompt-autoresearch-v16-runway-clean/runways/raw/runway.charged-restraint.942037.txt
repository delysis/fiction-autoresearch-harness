As soon as Jonah’s hand slid into hers, Mara realized how much she wanted this contact. The weight of his fingers, the warmth between them—it felt right.
She’d thought the night ended after their forced meeting with the council. But as she walked beside him, she could feel something shifting between them. Her pulse quickened when she caught sight of his arm brushing hers.
Jonah stopped just before they reached the Fulcrum’s entrance. His gaze dropped to her hand, and she wondered if he felt it too—the pull that hadn’t gone unnoticed.
“We shouldn’t…” he murmured, but his voice faltered as her fingertips grazed his wrist.
Mara couldn’t look away from him now. There was no denying what they both wanted; denying it felt like denying herself entirely.
