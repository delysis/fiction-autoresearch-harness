Mara felt the weight of Jonah's hand against her elbow, his grip steady but not possessive. His presence was a constant, yet they walked in silence as though the air itself was fraught with unspoken tension. She glanced at his profile, her curiosity flickering. "You always lead with such caution," she mused, her tone casual but layered with intrigue. He turned toward her, eyes deep and guarded, a flicker of surprise crossing his features.

"Caution comes naturally," Jonah replied, his voice low and measured. "But it's not always practical." His words carried an undercurrent of challenge, inviting her to lean closer into the space between them—a space thick with restraint and simmering desire.
