Adrian picked up a tiny savory treat from the plate between them,
popped it into his mouth, and chewed silently for a few moments. "Are
you ready to begin?" he asked around another mouthful. Mara tasted a
tiny savory treat. "Yes," she said, nodding slightly. Adrian rose from
his chair and led Mara down the short hall to her left.

They passed a room on the right with two white chairs. At the end of
the corridor was a large doorway without doors. Adrian pointed toward
a closed glass door in the outer wall of the large room. "You'll find
that your room has been assigned for you," he said. Mara nodded. Adrian
continued on down the hall, stepping through the open door at the end
of the corridor. A small desk sat against the wall opposite the door.
A chair sat behind it, facing another doorway on the back wall. Mara
hesitated, then stepped into the room and crossed to the chair, which
she pulled out and sat down.

Adrian returned and pointed at her. "I'm Adrian, the Head of Fulcrum.
Welcome," he said, with a slight inclination of his head. Mara reached
out with a hand to touch his shoulder. "Hello, Adrian."

"Mara," Adrian said, pointing.

"Adrian." Mara smiled and nodded.

"Mara," Adrian said with an inclination of his head.

"Yes," Mara replied.

Adrian pointed toward the doorway opposite the desk. "This door leads
to the lounge," he said. Mara turned her head toward the door. It was
not locked.

Adrian turned toward Mara again and smiled slightly. He raised one hand
slightly above the other, so they were at a ninety degree angle. Mara
took a deep breath and held it. Adrian let out a small exhale of air.
Mara released her breath. "Thank you," he said. Mara nodded, feeling
warmth rising inside her chest. Adrian rose and pointed to the door on
his left. "This is the lounge," he said. He turned toward Mara again
and pointed. "You are Mara." Mara nodded.

Adrian stepped through the door behind him and returned immediately,
holding a small piece of white paper with a number on it in his hand.
He placed it on the table in front of her and stepped back. "This is
your room key. Please sign it." Mara took the card and turned toward
the desk. A small pad of paper with a pen sitting on it was placed
there. She took the pen and wrote her name on the card, then placed
it back on the pad of paper. She turned toward Adrian, who smiled
slightly and pointed to her room's door. "Welcome," he said again.

Mara stood up and crossed to her room, reaching for the card and
placing it on the scanner next to the door handle. The light above the
handle changed from red to green. She pushed the door open and stepped
inside, closing it behind her. She set her suitcase down on a luggage
rack by the bed, and looked around. There were two other doors in
the room: one at the end of a short hallway on her right led outside,
while the other, a large glass door on the far wall, faced a terrace.

The terrace was narrow and deep, with a few small pots of flowers and a
small table with two chairs. A staircase led down to a small courtyard
on the far side.
