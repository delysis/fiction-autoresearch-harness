

Mara lifted her hand to his shoulder and touched its solid shape through a thin cotton shirt. It was dry except for a damp edge from the side of the building and a smooth patch across the left shoulder, as if someone had leaned there for hours. She leaned a little closer and slid her fingers along the seam of his neck and arm until his hand closed on her forearm. His thumb pressed down her elbow crease like it wanted to feel a pulse.

His free hand slid to her lower back and she stepped close.

A streetlight framed his cheekbones. One hand slid across his chest and found the hard curve of his hip bone and he turned his face toward her. She watched his mouth, waiting for some tiny warning motion, but it stayed still as stone and a little wet at the edge. She kissed him on one side of the mouth and felt a flicker under his lip as if the teeth there had closed and opened. The next kiss lingered longer and she felt him press back. Mara eased away.

He said, “Want me to walk with you?”

“I don’t know.” She set her head on his chest where his heart moved and thought.
