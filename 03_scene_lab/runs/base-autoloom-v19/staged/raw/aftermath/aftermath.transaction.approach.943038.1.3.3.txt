

Esther let herself go for only seconds before she pushed him away slightly and pressed her forehead against his chest. He made another sound that wasn't agreement and pulled her close enough to hear the change.

His breath caught between them when she arched toward him, and then it was his turn to hold her close.

They remained still until he could speak. Simon watched Esther count aloud on his chest, two fingers following each number in a silent sequence that they both understood.

His hands remained where they were even as she reached beneath the pillow to retrieve the drive. Esther did not break their joined breathing as she examined it.

"There," he said.

She met his eyes and then lowered her gaze to the seal. "This time," she said, "I'm counting the days since we last made love like this."

Simon pulled a piece of paper from beneath his pillow. "Four nights," he said. "Your own decision."

Esther's fingers closed around the drive and stayed there. She looked away toward the window. "Seven months ago you made one I couldn't undo." Her voice shook. "Tonight you broke my trust in this house."

He met her eyes. "I didn't choose to betray you."

She shook her head slightly.
