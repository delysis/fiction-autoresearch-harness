She could tell by the way his hands tightened that he was listening still, counting something other than heartbeats now. Pleasure rose in her with the steady rhythm of his thumb moving in slow circles on her ribcage. His hand felt both firm and light. She opened her eyes to look at how careful his touch had become.

When the tightness in her body shifted into something else, Simon stopped again.

"I hear," he said.

"Then watch," she whispered.

Esther closed her eyes while pleasure gathered at the junction where scar met skin. His hand on her waist moved with slow, steady pressure.

Her body relaxed suddenly.

Simon caught her against him before she fell back. Her legs tightened around him but he waited for her to catch her breath. He could feel her heart beating against his chest when it stopped.

"Tell me you understand," she whispered into his shoulder.

"I do," he said. "We'll go tonight."

The sound he made this time was no question at all.

When Simon finally released her, Esther's hand remained against his chest while she caught her breath. He listened to both of them breathing for several minutes before moving again.

She watched the pulse in his throat steady.
