Mara looked past them all and spotted a woman standing alone in the kitchen. Her dark hair was curly, with strands loose above her ear. She didn’t say anything; she just stood there watching the room without meeting anyone’s eyes, apparently waiting her turn.

Gerald glanced toward the kitchen and made an offer. “Ordinarily I would insist that we all join you for dinner, but I know it’s a very short notice. Just be prepared to share your experiences later, after we’ve all had our own dinner.”

The woman who wasn’t Livia disappeared, only to reappear holding two glasses—one for Gerald, one for Mara—then left again without speaking to anyone.
Mara was still holding her glass. She wasn’t sure what it was; the liquid was light pink, but smelled more like grape than berry. She took a sip just for something to do while she stood there, and decided she liked it better than wine.
Meanwhile Gerald had turned his attention to Jonah again.

“Is this your first time meeting everyone?” he asked.
“It is.” He looked around at his fellow newcomers, not quite meeting anyone’s eyes. He seemed nervous.
