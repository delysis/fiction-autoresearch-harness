The group turned in unison, and Mara saw a third young man, dark-haired and tall but thin, step in through the doorway.
“Adrian,” he said by way of greeting.

At the sound of his name, Jonah looked away from Mara and spoke up.
“Hey,” he said. “Where’s Livia?”

Mara noticed the catering worker then, just stepping back into the hall after the young man. Older than Adrian by far, she had a long face under thinning hair; no-nonsense eyes. She wore an apron, and now that Mara was noticing her, it looked as if she'd already been coming and going from this room several times, unobtrusively preparing for the evening ahead.

“She said she wouldn’t be long,” said Adrian. “I thought she’d catch up.” He looked around the group and smiled again. “The wine’s downstairs if anyone wants any. I didn’t know if we needed more.”

Gerald motioned at his glass. “Looks as though you judged right.”

Adrian walked to the low table by the wall and picked it up, giving Jonah an amused smile.

“Oh,” said Jonah, without any great urgency. “Is that yours?” He took a step toward Mara as if he could shield her from the sight.
“Thanks,” Adrian said as he drained his glass in two swallows. He set the empty down by the couch where the blond man had been sitting.

“Sure,” said Gerald, reaching for the table and taking up the plastic cup himself. “Anyone else want some wine? I think we might have a little left.”
