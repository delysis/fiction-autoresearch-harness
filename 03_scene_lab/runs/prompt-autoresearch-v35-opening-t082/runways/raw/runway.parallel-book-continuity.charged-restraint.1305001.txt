“There’s a full moon,” said Mara, who was walking behind Jonah, who was walking behind the group of lab techs who had already reached the parking lot. “And no stars.” She was still breathing hard from running down the stairs, but she was also a little dizzy. The last time she had run like that was when the dogs had cornered her and she’d had to jump over a fence, which was a year ago, just before she moved here. Jonah didn’t say anything. He only looked around, as if waiting for someone else to show up. The light from the parking lot wasn’t bright enough for Mara to see if he was smiling, but the way he stood—one foot forward, shoulders relaxed—made her want to stop and ask him if he knew where she was supposed to be going. But she didn’t want to talk about the dogs.

“Do you think the moon is a planet?” said Jonah after a few minutes of walking. “Or is it just a moon?”
