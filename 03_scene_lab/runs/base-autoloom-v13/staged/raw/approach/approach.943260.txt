

His breath caught at her touch. Simon had forgotten that feel, the way her skin both warmed and tempered his body. He leaned into the touch only because he knew she could pull back.

"My wife," he said finally.

Esther's fingers left his lips but stayed on his chest. The drive lay between them on the table.

"If we're having sex tonight," she said, "the question becomes what to do about this."

She didn't say that the seal made every touch a potential risk. She didn't have to. The cracked seal meant something could walk freely between his body and theirs.

Simon traced the outline of her shoulder harness beneath her shirt. The weight of it reminded him that she'd worn it into every room but this one since he'd returned.

"We can wear it," he suggested. "Let you run everything."

Esther took her hand away. Simon's breath left him for a second time.

She put the drive back in its case without touching the seal.

"We can't," she said. "Not here. Not this."

"Then what do we do?"

"That's your question tonight," Esther said quietly. "Before you answer, let me ask you something else." She took a step closer and rested her hand over his heart. "Where am I when you have to make these decisions?"

"There," he whispered, pressing the back of his fingers against the hand at his chest.
