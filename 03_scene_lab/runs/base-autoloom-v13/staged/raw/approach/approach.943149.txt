

Simon traced the faint lines of her question mark with his tongue.

She laughed, low and precise.

"Now I want to know everything," she said.

He lifted one arm and touched the long scar running down the back of her thigh. A year of salt water had bleached it white where it crossed her knee.

"This is the injury you should have shown me last."

"Your concern for my well-being is noted," Esther said. "And?"

He shifted his hand to where her ribs curved. Beneath her skin, the fresh bruising patterned their map of the ocean floor. "And I did not rescue everyone you named."

Esther moved her left hand up from his jaw to his chest and let the back of her fingers press lightly against his sternum.

Simon took the finger and put it to his lips without looking away from hers.

"You are checking for heartbeat," she said. "Does my name appear in your rescue priority?"

"I brought you out first."

"And then?"

His body stayed under her gaze. His hand closed around her right wrist. "We can find the pattern together."

Esther turned his wrist to read the faded ink from his last password attempt. She looked up with a smile that cut just below its edges.

"What sort of husband opens a sealed drive with his own blood?"
