

"No one."

The drive's cracked seal and his answer created a quiet moment that would not disappear without speech. The silence came from two places at once: a room and the person who entered it with Simon.

Esther turned from him and walked slowly toward the workroom window, each step carefully avoiding loose papers and small tools on the floor.

"How do you trust that this isn't a trap?" she asked when she reached the glass. "A drive that arrives open is either a gift or an ambush."

"We verify its contents together, immediately."

"The other way to trust is by trusting you."

Simon felt her weight shift with the word. The act of looking directly at the cracked seal had placed them both in motion.

Esther put her left hand on the windowsill and opened her right palm toward the room. Her fingers moved through the air as if signing a message in ASL.

Simon took two slow steps toward her and put his own right hand on hers. He pressed with enough strength to hold her palm open and no more. The movement placed his chest against her back and made her breath catch.

"We don't need to be enemies here," he said. "I was never meant to make that choice without you."

She opened her mouth to speak, then closed it. Her breathing deepened. A thin line of tension remained visible beneath the surface.

The room around them continued to exist. Papers still waited on the floor for her to organize. The drive's cracked seal made no sound
