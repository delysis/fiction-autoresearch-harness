

Simon hesitated. The hesitation told Esther what she needed to know. "A part of me, always."

She dropped her hand. "Not an answer."

"If you make me name it," Simon said, "you'll want to take it apart immediately."

Esther lifted the drive and turned it between her fingers, watching the broken seal catch light from the lamp. "I can't trust what I don't understand."

"Try anyway," he said. "For two minutes."

Simon was not wearing the red leather harness that bound him to her when she put his hand on her chest.
