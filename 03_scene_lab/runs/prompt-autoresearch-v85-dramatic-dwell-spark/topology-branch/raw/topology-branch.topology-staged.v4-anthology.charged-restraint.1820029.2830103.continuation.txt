
The footsteps stopped. They came back toward her, slowing to a walk. He stopped again when he reached her.

Mara looked up. She could have sworn Jonah had turned his head to watch her as soon as her feet touched the sidewalk. She blinked and shifted her weight away from him.
She still held her purse in both hands across her body, and her forearms were very cold.
“I’m sorry. I… I’d like to walk with you.” Mara cleared her throat and started again. “May I walk with you? I think you might be able to help me.”
