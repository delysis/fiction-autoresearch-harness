
He stopped. She heard him breathe. A breath she could almost smell. “Yes?” His voice was gentle. Mara looked up in time to see him turn around and start back.
He reached her first and took two steps past her before he realized she had not followed. “I thought—I’d lost you,” Mara said. She didn’t want to tell him that it felt like the end of a dream when she couldn’t hear him breathe anymore. She didn’t want him to know.
“Are you okay?” he asked. His face was grave and shadowed by darkness. He stood very still while he waited for Mara to answer.
Mara made the answer herself. “I want to keep walking.” She held out her hand to him, and he took it.
