 “Jonah!”
She heard him stop and turn back, but couldn’t see him. A small noise came from the dark—something moving in the brush? Her heart raced; her legs felt as if they could not support her. She let go of Jonah’s name again and heard him running toward her.

His voice was quiet when he spoke her name, though his face was sweaty and flushed and she saw that his shirt was wet. He stepped closer to her, and then a little closer still.
She could feel the air moving with his breath; it felt warm against her skin.
“Are you all right?” he asked.
She could not reply because she did not know what he meant. Jonah waited a moment and spoke again. “Shall I walk with you?” he asked.
