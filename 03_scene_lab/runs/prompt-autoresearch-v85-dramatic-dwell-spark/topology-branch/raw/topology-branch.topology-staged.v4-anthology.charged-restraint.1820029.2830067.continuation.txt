 Jonah stopped. The sound of his breath ended instantly in a deep, loud inhalation. “Wait up.”
Jonah stood still and waited, silent for ten seconds. Mara went to where he had stopped, walking over cold, frozen grass. His feet had left prints on the ground. The prints were deep in the grass at the sidewalk, and shallower, closer together, and fading farther along toward the drive. Jonah looked at his feet. He was breathing through his mouth, making a faint wheezing sound.
Mara took Jonah’s arm. It was warm and strong beneath her hand. He turned to look down at her; he hadn’t moved. “Please? Would you—walk with me?” she asked, as calmly as she could manage. Jonah put his hand over hers and squeezed once, hard. He didn’t answer. His fingers were cold. “Please?”
