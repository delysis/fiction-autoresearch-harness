

Esther kissed him again, briefly, and traced a line along his collarbone with her finger. "You're in this house," she said. "You've always been in this house."

Simon pulled her against him and placed their joined hands against his chest. Esther's breathing changed when she heard the pulse of his heart beneath her palm.

"Are you staying?" he asked.

"Yes."

He closed the last distance between them. The sound of sheets filled the silence, making the change explicit.
