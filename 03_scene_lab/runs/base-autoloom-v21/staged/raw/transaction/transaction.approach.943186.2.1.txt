

"You're always here." Her fingers moved across his collarbone. "Unless you tell me where else you are."

He ran his tongue along the seal on her lips. "I'm trying to get there," he said. "But I need to get home first."

Esther's hand went to the edge of his shirt, slowly loosening the buttons. He reached for the lamp on the nightstand and turned it on low, illuminating the scars on his chest where the drive had been recovered. The light caught in her eyes as she watched him, breathing steady but not still.

"How far are you?" she asked.

Simon let his fingers rest at the base of her spine. "I'm getting there," he said, more to himself than to her. "Just one step at a time."

Esther moved closer, pressing her body against his. The scars on his chest made visible maps they could both feel. "You need to come home before I can tell you how far you are," she said. "Or else we'll both be waiting."

He took her hand in both of his, slowly drawing her touch across the scars again. "I am home," he said, looking into her eyes. "And I am getting there. One step at a time."

Esther closed her eyes and leaned her forehead against his chest. The sound of his breathing slowed her own until their chests moved together. "I'll be here when you get there," she said softly. "That's where I need to be."

Simon lifted his hand to cup the back of her head. The scars on her neck were still visible where they had opened during transport. Esther leaned into his touch, and the sound of her own breathing steadied as he held her.

He ran his fingertips over the scars on his chest again, slowly tracing each one. "I need to get there first," he said, feeling her hands rest at the base of his spine. "But I need to know how far I am first."

Esther moved her hand to trace the scars on his neck in turn. The seal on her lips was still visible as she watched him. "That's what home is," she said softly. "The place where you can tell me how far you are."

Simon took her hand and guided it to rest against his chest, feeling her hand steady as they both slowed their breathing. The scars on his back were still there where the drive had been recovered, making visible maps they could feel. "I'll be here," he said. "And I'll let you know when I get there."

Esther opened her eyes and met his, moving closer until she could feel his scars through his shirt. The light from the lamp on the nightstand caught in her eyes as they watched each other, their breathing slowing together.

"I'll be here too," she said. "That's where home is."

Simon moved his hand to trace the scar on her cheek where her teeth had caught, and Esther leaned into his touch as they watched each other breathe. The sound of their breathing grew steady and slow as they continued to watch each other in the dim light.
