She hoped he’d tell her what he was thinking, but when she said nothing he took a step toward her as though she were answering. His mouth moved to speak but no sound came out. She waited to see if he’d ask something else or do something more. Her breath came quickly now. She wanted him to be honest. She didn’t know what she could trust. His shoulders dropped without moving, as though he were getting used to the weight of his jacket. The gesture was unconscious and slow but it made her relax her throat.
