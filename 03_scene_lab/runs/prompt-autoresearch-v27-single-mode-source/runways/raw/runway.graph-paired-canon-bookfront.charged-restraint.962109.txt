Fulcrum's late sessions always end badly. But Mara had only herself to blame for letting it get to her. "What do I have to do?" she asked Jonah. "Wear a sign?" He paused before responding, but eventually she got his attention. "A sign?" She nodded, "A sign saying please pay attention to the research, not to me."

He pulled her toward the elevator. "If I'd been the one taking notes," he said as she reached up to push the button, "I would have stopped the experiment. Instead we have to redo everything." He turned to her, his eyes narrowed with anger.

The elevator opened and she stepped inside. He grabbed her arm before she could get away. "No one has to take this crap but you," he said.

She yanked her arm away, her face turning cold. "I'd rather be treated like a colleague," she said as she pressed the basement button. He let go as she faced the doors.

As they went down, she looked up to meet his eyes. The doors opened and he stepped out, and she walked off into the parking garage.
