
He released her fingers. “Would you like a drink?”
“Oh, I can—”
“Here, you can have mine.”
He went over to the bar, pulled the bottle of red from the rack and a glass from the stack, poured into it until she protested. “Thanks,” she said, taking a sip.
They stood facing each other again.
“So how long have you been here?” She gestured with her glass.
He glanced at the wall behind her and then back at her eyes. “About a week, maybe.” He gave her a smile that was definitely meant to mean something.
“I’m new, I guess,” she said, with a small laugh.
“You guess?”
“Well, yeah—it doesn’t feel like it, really. I mean…”
His eyebrows lifted; he looked at her curiously. “Interesting,” he said. Then he held out a hand to show her the way to the terrace.

As they reached the terrace doors and stepped through into the open air, the first guests had just arrived. She recognized them as the two people from the video. A couple of women were seated on a low wooden bench a little farther away from the group, both dressed in bright dresses with full skirts and bare arms. One of them looked up when they came in, her eyes flicking quickly over Mara before resting on Jonah with obvious approval.

Jonah leaned one elbow on the doorframe as if to give himself something to do while he watched the scene unfold below. “Ah, the vultures have arrived,” he said in a low voice.
“Vultures?”
“Watch.” He pointed down at the group with his chin. “You’ll see.”
