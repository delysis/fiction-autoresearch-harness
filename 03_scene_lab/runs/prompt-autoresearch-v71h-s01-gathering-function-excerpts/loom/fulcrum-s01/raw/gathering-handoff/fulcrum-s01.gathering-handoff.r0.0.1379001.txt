
Mara remembered what Livia had said about how long Jonah had been at Fulcrum, that he was a hardware researcher, that they all liked him. None of it explained the way he looked at her. “I’m glad to meet you.”
“Welcome,” said Jonah. “This place gets interesting when Adrian comes home.” And he grinned again, but this time Mara could tell he didn’t mean it for her.
Jonah had said ‘Adrian’ the way someone might say ‘The big boss man who isn’t around right now.’ And her stomach turned over again.

It was at that moment that Mara heard people outside coming toward the common room. A familiar voice spoke, a man’s voice that sounded vaguely like Adrian. She thought she recognized another voice too, female this time.
Jonah didn’t even wait for her to make up her mind before he let go of her hand and stepped back, turning his head toward the door as a woman and two men entered. The tall one looked to be at least forty; he was dark-haired and clean-shaven, but she didn’t see the face behind the glasses.
The younger man was a gangly fellow with black hair, a few years older than Jonah, maybe. And then there was the woman.
She was small-framed, short in stature, slender and tanned. She wore her short, light brown hair straight, and had sharp cheekbones and hazel eyes.
Her voice was just as Mara had heard it in the morning. “Jonah. You were first, I see.”
“Couldn’t wait.” Jonah shrugged and smiled.

Mara remembered her manners and cleared her throat. “Hi,” she said, looking at the dark-haired man. “I’m Mara.”
He nodded. “Glad to meet you. I’m Nathan,” he introduced himself.
