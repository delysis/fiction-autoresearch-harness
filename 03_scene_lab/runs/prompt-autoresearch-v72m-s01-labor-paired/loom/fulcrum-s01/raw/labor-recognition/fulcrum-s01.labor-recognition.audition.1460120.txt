Once all three of them were settled around the low table, the brown-haired man raised his glass again and took another sip before setting it back down between Jonah’s crossed ankles and glancing at Mara. “I haven’t had a chance to meet you yet. I’m Tim. We’re in the same cohort.”
Mara nodded hello, and then noticed that one of the catering staff had picked up Tim’s glass and set it aside without remark. Jonah’s knees parted, releasing the plastic cup onto the carpet.

The brown-haired man took no notice, just carried on his casual conversation while a woman walked over from across the room with his empty glass in one hand. She was taller, heavier, older; her short brown hair was streaked with gray and she had the look of a woman who has spent a lifetime managing people.
The older catering worker placed his empty cup on the edge of the table without so much as a glance at him. And when she turned and walked away, Tim didn’t even bother to follow with his eyes.
Mara watched them go, and waited for a lesson to begin.
