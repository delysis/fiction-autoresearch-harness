But as soon as Jonah and the brown-haired man started talking, another man emerged from the hallway into the main room: an older man. He was tall enough to be conspicuous at that point of the conversation, and he had dark hair slicked back from a ruddy face. A name tag said Jonah Reed.

Mara waited for the old man to introduce himself as Jonah, but he didn’t say a word as he walked past them toward the far door, leaving Mara unsure of how to respond—should she have expected him to stop and talk with them? But since he was carrying a clear glass bottle from which he drank continuously, she guessed that he must have been just leaving the kitchen and making his way home.

And sure enough, the door at the other end swung shut behind him, and then nothing more happened for about a minute.
