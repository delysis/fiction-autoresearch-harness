By six forty-five there were eight men, four women, and two children in the common room at Fulcrum. Two more women in white lab coats with “FULCRUM” stitched above the pockets pushed open a pair of glass doors down the hallway, announcing themselves to the group, and Mara recognized one as Dr. Grace Williams, Jonah Reed’s boss.

In the last half-hour, Mara had watched the young fellow who first introduced himself to her take a glass of white wine from the bar across the room and bring it back to join the crowd standing against the wall on the left side of the door. When she followed his eyes over to see who he’d been looking at, she noticed an older catering worker had taken hold of his abandoned glass, lifted it up from the table where he’d set it down, and turned on a heel to walk right out.
