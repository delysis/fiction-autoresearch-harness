After a late Fulcrum lab session, I walked through the door marked EMERGENCY and onto a deserted street. Jonah fell in step behind me. He said nothing for the first several blocks. I knew he was behind me but did not turn back to look. The streets were empty of traffic and nearly empty of people. Only two figures approached us, walking on the other side of the street. I watched one turn onto our sidewalk, and a moment later felt the familiar sensation of being brushed past by a stranger. I tried not to look, and succeeded only in turning my eyes downward toward my feet. Then we passed beneath a streetlamp and I looked up. The other figure on the other side of the street was a boy who looked familiar. I stared back at him, and felt a cold sinking sensation. I watched him walk by, my hands clenching at my sides.
