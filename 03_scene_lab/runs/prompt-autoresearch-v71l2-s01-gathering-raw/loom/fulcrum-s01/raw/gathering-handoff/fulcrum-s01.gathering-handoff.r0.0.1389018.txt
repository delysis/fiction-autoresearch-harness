
The grip was dry and steady, a little firmer than hers but not threatening. She let herself follow his movements as he pulled back toward his original position; the left arm moved with the right, raising until they were both standing straight again. And then the other hand detached from her own and rose as well, to push up his glasses with a finger.
His head was tilted toward her. “I wasn’t expecting you at this hour,” he said in a low voice.
