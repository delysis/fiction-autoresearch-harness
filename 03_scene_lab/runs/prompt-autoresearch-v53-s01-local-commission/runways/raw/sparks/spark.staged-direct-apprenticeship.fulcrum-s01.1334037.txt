The welcome reception was held on a wide terrace paved with irregular
slabs of pink marble overlooking a vast meadow in the Sierra foothills
below. The sun had set in a blaze of gold and purple over the snow peaks
of the High Sierras to the east, and its last rays touched the peaks
with pink. In the deepening purple of evening the meadow glowed with
the gold light of its wildflowers, which stretched as far as the eye
could see, and the air was full of the scents of cedar and pine and a
thousand other wild herbs.

some short—all dressed in formal evening attire, talking and laughing
