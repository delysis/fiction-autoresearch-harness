

A full breath took too much attention so Mara looked at their feet and moved hers under the truck’s bumper. Jonah put his right hand behind him on the step above and waited for her to move.

She tried a small step off the front porch. He let their joined hands stay steady without pushing against her. When she said, “Okay,” they walked.

Jonah took a wider step, moving her hand with the easy strength of his stride. After three steps Mara let the heel of her free hand rest on his elbow. She was used to holding someone when they walked. Jonah’s arm wasn’t tight, and he let her use his elbow without changing his pace or direction.

The walk took Mara through a part of herself she kept closed in the lab. When Jonah turned to look at her she let her hand slide higher on his sleeve to let them see each other more clearly. She watched his eyes follow the change. He said nothing while she moved her hand to rest between his arm and side.

When he looked forward again he took a wider step, then slowed his pace so Mara could move next to him. When she said nothing for a long time Jonah finally asked, “Why did you do it?”

“Do what?”

Jonah glanced over so the question was more of a look. “Stop them.”

“Why not? They’re hurting people. I won’t let people I care about get hurt.”

He didn’t argue with her. They reached the sidewalk in the street and Mara let her left hand fall from his to her side. She stood alone while Jonah unlocked the truck door, then watched as he put his keys on the front seat and shut the door again. When he turned to face her she knew exactly what he was going to ask.

“Can I offer you a ride?” The voice was steady.

“I’ll walk, thanks. I can walk to my own apartment.”

Mara didn’t expect an argument and didn’t get one. After two steps Mara said, “Can we just walk for a while? Can we be alone?”

He turned so they could meet his eyes. She waited to see what he would say.

“Yes.”

She took his offered hand and he let them walk slowly. After four steps she said, “I want to stop for a moment and put my forehead against yours so we can just breathe together.”

He stopped at the corner and turned to face her, then closed one eye. Mara moved to him and lifted their joined hands to his chest to stand steady. Jonah kept his voice low. “I’m breathing for both of us right now.”

Mara put her forehead against his while Jonah’s other hand went slowly to hold her arm. She was quiet until she heard the distant sound of traffic, then finally looked up. “What about a kiss?”

“If you want one.”

“Okay, then, I’ll kiss you. Wait, I’ll put my hands on your chest so I can stand steady.” Jonah let her move. When Mara leaned forward he rested his forehead lightly against hers and kept it there as she pulled away. Then his eyes focused again as Mara moved forward to meet him.
