

“I’m okay.” Mara shifted her weight onto the free right foot. She pulled her left hand out of Jonah’s to show him she was steady and didn’t need an arm.

Jonah closed his empty right hand and let it hang by his side. He didn’t look away. He held his arm steady so her fingers could reach without bending toward him.

Mara walked around Jonah to face him on the next step down. She rested her left hand on the open palm he had offered.

Jonah breathed out through his nose as though this was easier than words. Mara let her breath match his. She watched his mouth for words when they were both still. He didn’t offer any. His shoulders relaxed.

Mara cupped his hand. A breath held in her throat. Her eyes fell to Jonah’s lips but he stayed still, waiting for her. The pressure of his hand was a question.

When she raised her eyes, his brows lowered as though he wanted to give her something she couldn’t name. She met his eyes and pulled her left hand into her coat pocket without letting go. He waited for what came next.

Mara slid her right hand into Jonah’s coat pocket without asking. His pocket held nothing but air and the lining. The soft cotton rustled lightly.

Jonah’s mouth softened at the edges as if a quiet smile was almost there.

Mara put her right arm around Jonah’s waist and drew his body in. The pocket lining slid smoothly as his breath caught in his throat.

They stood like this until Jonah slid his left hand into her jacket and Mara tilted her head to meet his lips.

“Wait.” Mara backed away from the kiss but not from Jonah’s body. They were still close enough that when he spoke, her face felt the small puffs of his breath.

“What would you have me do?” He released her hand from inside her jacket.

Mara turned into Jonah’s embrace. “Kiss me.” Her right hand returned to his coat pocket.
