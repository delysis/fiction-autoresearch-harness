 They stood holding hands while he waited for what she chose next.

After a moment she shifted weight from one foot to the other and turned a quarter toward his truck. He met her gaze as he released her hand and bent for his keys from the front step, then stood again with his open palm waiting.

Mara folded her arms across her chest. Jonah reached for the truck door, pulled it open, and stepped away as he held the door with both hands to let her in. She climbed up. He closed the door and waited beside it.

When Mara lowered the passenger window, his head turned to put the truck’s hood between them. Her open hand stayed at the corner of the rolled-down window. His hand rested on top of her forearm where she held it out. After a moment she withdrew it and his hand fell back against his thigh.

Mara rolled up her window.

Jonah waited by the door, his face still turned away. She looked through the windshield. They’d stayed until almost eleven in a session that started at noon. How much time was left to the walk home?

She heard a small exhalation from beside her and his face returned to the driver side window. He stepped back, his hands away from the door. Mara climbed out and let the door shut by itself. Jonah stood still with his open hand waiting again. She took it and looked into his eyes.

After a moment she took his wrist in both of hers and lifted her voice so he could hear it under his engine. “I’ll walk.”

Jonah closed the driver-side window with one hand while he held hers and then turned to her with his eyes. Mara saw a question behind them.

“I can walk,” she said in a clearer voice. “I’ve done it before.” She let go of him and stood with her arms at her sides. He stopped holding her hand, straightened up, and stepped back.

Mara waited beside him and took a deep breath when Jonah opened the truck door with his eyes on hers. He pulled himself up and closed the door.

She watched the taillights shrink as the truck pulled out of Fulcrum’s driveway. She started walking.

He turned the truck onto the road that ran parallel to the creek between the town and the campus. Mara waited beside the gate to the campus and watched his taillights rise and fall as he passed the curve at the bridge.

The sky over the forest was clear and still. A breeze came off the water and cooled the air on her neck. Jonah’s truck stopped in front of the road that ran between his cabin and Mara’s. She watched his taillights reverse and come back to the campus gate. When he stepped out of the truck his headlights went off and his silhouette appeared beside the open door.

Mara crossed the road and put a hand on Jonah’s shoulder as she met him. He turned to face her without touching her back. His hand touched the door’s metal bar as if to say I’m not holding onto anything. Mara set her free hand on his wrist where it rested on the bar and looked into his eyes. When he didn’t move she took his wrist in her other hand. His eyes said I’m still waiting.

Mara folded his left arm out of the way and put her hand over his right. His palm touched hers and he pulled the keys from the door’s lock. She watched the reflection of her face in his glasses. When she squeezed his wrist he slid the keys down into her open hand. He took the glasses from his nose and tucked them in their case, which he placed carefully in the glove compartment.
