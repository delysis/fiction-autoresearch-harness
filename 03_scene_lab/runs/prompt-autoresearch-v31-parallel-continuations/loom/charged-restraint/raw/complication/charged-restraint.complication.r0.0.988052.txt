“Wait,” she said. “Just a minute.”

“That’s my turn.” Mara opened her eyes to meet Jonah’s steady gaze. He waited without asking if she wanted to continue. Mara saw how he was letting them make each choice as a new step.

Mara moved her hands from his shirt and dropped their joined hands. She pulled a full breath and closed her eyes again. “That was good. Thank you.”

Jonah said nothing when she turned away, but his pace told Mara he was staying near as she reached the cross street. When Mara moved past the trees under an arc light she stopped and turned to him. He let his hand rest on his truck’s hood as she started to speak.

Mara waited to hear his words, but only heard the sound of a distant train. The moment took her breath for an instant while she looked into Jonah’s eyes. “I wish it were later. I wish we were alone.”

“Me too,” Jonah said, his eyes still on Mara’s face. She felt her breath pull deep inside her as he met her look without moving. When she turned away and continued walking she was aware of his presence behind her.

“Can we do this again? Can we talk?”

Jonah waited for her words to stop, then took one small step closer. “When can we meet? How soon?”
