 His eyes moved but stayed steady. “We’re outside, in public.”

Mara felt her head drop to look down at her feet. She stopped when she said, “I don’t want any more experiments tonight.”

He was quiet for a long moment, then he said, “What will you do if you’re done with kissing me?” He moved to face her and his eyes met hers. Mara looked him in the eye when she answered him.

“I’ll go back to work in the morning. I’m not going to quit.” She put her free hand on her chest and he waited without moving. “I’m not going to get scared or make a scene.”

She felt his steady breath move across her cheek and knew he wasn’t just listening, he was choosing what to say. His voice was almost too quiet to hear, but Mara heard every word. “What will you do if I kiss you?”

Mara held his eyes until her own eyes got tired. She stepped closer and put both hands on his chest so she could lift her chin. When she said “wait” he let her go, releasing her hand and dropping his other arm. He moved to face her, then turned his head and watched as she dropped both hands to her sides. He didn’t hold her or speak, just watched as Mara turned and walked away from him.
