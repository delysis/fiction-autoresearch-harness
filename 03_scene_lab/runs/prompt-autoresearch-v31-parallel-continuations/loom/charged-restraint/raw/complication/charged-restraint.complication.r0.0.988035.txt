

Mara rested an open palm against Jonah’s chest and saw him raise one eyebrow. He was waiting to see if she wanted contact.

After two steps Mara put her left hand on his forearm and said, “Can I hold your head in both hands for a moment before I kiss you?”

When she met his eyes he gave her the full weight of his look. “Yes.”

She waited as his eyes focused on hers before saying, “I want to kiss you now.”

Jonah turned his head and lifted one hand while Mara moved hers to cup his head. When his head lowered Mara pressed her lips against his and let their faces rest together without moving. He kept breathing until she felt a warmth like light, then let him kiss back.

They let their lips brush gently over and over, as slow as the movement of the leaves when he lowered his forehead against hers.

He took two more breaths while his chest rose and fell against Mara’s hands. “I want to talk to you tomorrow,” he said. “I can give you time, if you need it.”

Mara felt a small smile in her face as she looked at him. “Thank you.”

After one breath she said, “Do you want to kiss again before we go?”

“Yes.”

She cupped his head and brought her lips against his. They tasted his warm breath like coffee when he lifted his forehead away.

Mara left her hand on his head while he opened the truck door, then turned away and walked down the sidewalk to her building. Her mind was quiet when she stood alone on her own stairs with a clear reason to meet him tomorrow.

End of chapter one.
