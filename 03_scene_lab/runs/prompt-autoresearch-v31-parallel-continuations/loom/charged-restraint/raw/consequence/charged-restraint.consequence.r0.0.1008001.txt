 Jonah was waiting for her somewhere in the darkness, out where she could almost hear him waiting. She thought about how to reach him, then turned and kept walking slowly while his scent still filled her mind. The night was cold on her cheek until she stopped by the street.

Mara took a full breath and looked up at the sky. It was dark enough for her to see stars through the fog over the ocean. She saw distant lights moving behind the trees and felt how they kept changing, just like the shadows in the woods. Mara heard a train pass slowly down the coast as she watched the waves come in on the beach. The sound of Jonah’s voice waited where she could feel it.

The End
