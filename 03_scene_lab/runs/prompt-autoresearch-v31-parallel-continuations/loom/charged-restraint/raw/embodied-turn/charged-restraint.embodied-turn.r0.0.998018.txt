

He said nothing when she walked away, but she could see him watching her cross the empty street and stop at the trees on her side. Mara walked three blocks until she reached the door of her apartment, then turned back to the sidewalk.

Jonah stood with his hand on his truck’s hood, waiting for her to speak. The street was empty except for his distant form beside the black vehicle. Mara raised both hands and moved her fingers as if they had joined again. His body straightened and he nodded without saying a word. The train let her breathe quietly until she finally said, “Tomorrow.”

His head tilted toward her, then nodded as he turned his hands to say, “Yes.” Mara reached the light as a car turned the corner. She heard Jonah’s truck pull slowly away behind her.

“Okay,” Mara whispered to herself. She didn’t turn back to check as she opened the apartment door and stepped inside.
