

He let her move to him. Jonah’s eyes widened a little at their second kiss, and Mara felt the touch of his hand on her neck just before her body tensed and she knew how close he was. The kiss broke when Jonah moved his head to touch his cheek against hers. He stayed with her while Mara pulled another long breath and tried to hold her balance.

She tried another slow kiss before letting go of him again. Her eyes opened as they turned to face her turn, then closed again as they walked. Jonah touched her arm and she waited for him to speak.

“There’s no reason you need to say anything right now, you know.”

Mara let a slow breath release tension in her neck, then looked at him without moving. “I can’t wait to kiss you again. I want us to find somewhere quiet so I can take off my clothes and feel your hands on me. I want to wake up alone in the morning and know you’re close enough to touch.”

The street lights let Mara see his eyes move slowly from hers down to her lips, then back again before he said anything. “There’s no reason to say it tonight,” he said quietly. “We can start whenever you want and end whenever you tell me. Just wait until we’re alone.” He paused while she looked up and pulled another long breath.

Mara opened her eyes slowly as the world came back to her. “I know we haven’t been careful enough. I want us to take time to do it right, with no chance of making mistakes.”

Jonah nodded without speaking as they reached her street. When she turned and walked toward her apartment he followed quietly. The street was empty except for an old station wagon parked under the street light in front of her door. Mara looked up at Jonah’s eyes without stopping to let him see she was ready before she spoke again.

“There are a lot of things we need to talk about, you know. The lab is a good reason to meet every day. I like it when we can talk.”

He watched her take one last look back before he said, “I’ll see you in the morning. You can call me anytime.”

Jonah let his hand drop as Mara reached her door and waited for a moment. When Mara turned toward him she could feel how much tension still held her neck. She opened her eyes slowly to meet his eyes and pulled another slow breath. “I will.”

Mara waited with her door open until Jonah turned into the street. He slowed his pace and let his hand rest on the truck’s hood. When Mara turned to look down at her shoes she could feel him watching without hearing anything he said. She reached to turn on the porch light and walked inside.
