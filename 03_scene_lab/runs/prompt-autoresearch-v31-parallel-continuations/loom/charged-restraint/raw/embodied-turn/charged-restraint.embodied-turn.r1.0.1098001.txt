 She didn’t have to ask to hear what he was thinking.

“Me too.” He let her see the change in his voice as she spoke again. “Is it possible we could get together sometime soon? Could you make a date for us? I’ll call you in a few days.”

Mara stopped when she heard a distant whistle. Jonah opened his mouth, then waited until she had taken three quiet breaths before speaking. “I think we both have a lot of work to do. But, yes. I’d like to see you again.”

“Good. I’ll call you in a few days.”

He let her say nothing before saying, “Okay. Take care of yourself.”

“You too.” Mara heard him make the sound she had started to say, and turned before he finished. She stopped halfway to her apartment and turned once more. A full breath took all her attention until she looked at her own hands again.
