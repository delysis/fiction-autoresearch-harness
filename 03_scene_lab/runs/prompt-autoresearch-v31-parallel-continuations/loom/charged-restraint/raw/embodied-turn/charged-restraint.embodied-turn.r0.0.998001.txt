

“Maybe it will be later, someday.” Jonah moved to rest beside his truck. He waited for her to speak while Mara took a full breath.

She closed her eyes and turned toward home. “Will you drive through this block?”

Jonah looked at her again without speaking. Mara felt their eyes meet for an instant before he closed his truck door. Mara said nothing when she heard his motor turn over. She took a full breath, then let him drive slowly past.
