

Jonah stepped away from the truck and walked toward her without hurrying. He held her upper arms in both hands for a moment, then let go to rest his hand on her waist while he spoke. “Me, too. And I’m sorry that I couldn’t be there before it happened.”

Mara had known he would say something like that. She tried another kiss and held on to his shirt as she felt his hands touch her hips. “When can we meet again?”

He let go of her gently, then turned his face away. Mara followed him with a question in her voice while Jonah unlocked the truck and opened the door. He waited while she stood in the street. Mara said, “I can walk the rest of the way.”

Jonah said nothing while he sat in the truck, then reached out the window for her hand. “I want to come see you tomorrow. Can I bring my notebook?”

Mara looked up at him with a question in her eyes. “You want me to read it?”

His hands dropped the window again while he said, “I’d be proud if you did.”

Mara let him drop her hand when she answered, “Yes. You can come tomorrow.”

Jonah took her hand for one quick kiss, then closed the window as the engine started. The truck moved slowly through the intersection and kept going while Mara watched it go. She stood alone in the street under the lights, feeling the sudden change.

When Mara let her eyes close she felt herself stand alone at the edge of the woods.
