

Jonah stood steady, waiting for Mara to continue. She pulled a short breath, then opened her eyes and met his gaze again.

“You know what I want. It’s too early tonight. I need more time. I need to see you another time and feel that you’re still offering it, before I trust this much of myself to you.”

The moment stretched without a word or breath until Jonah finally spoke. “I can be patient.”

She let him wait while she moved her hands from his truck. “Yes, but only as much patience as I choose. You’ll have to make it worth that much patience. I can wait for my own pleasure, too.”

Jonah said nothing for a moment, then smiled. “Then I’ll be patient as you need me to be.” His face was serious when she turned toward the truck again. “I need my car. I have to go somewhere.” He moved toward her without touching, then moved toward his truck. When he finally reached Mara’s turn he stopped and watched for a moment.

Mara’s feet moved ahead before she said, “I’m working tomorrow.” She wasn’t sure what she would say to Jonah when she got to the corner, but saw a black cat under a streetlight. When Mara spoke it was without looking back at him.

“You can see me in my apartment tomorrow evening. We don’t have to use the lab.”
