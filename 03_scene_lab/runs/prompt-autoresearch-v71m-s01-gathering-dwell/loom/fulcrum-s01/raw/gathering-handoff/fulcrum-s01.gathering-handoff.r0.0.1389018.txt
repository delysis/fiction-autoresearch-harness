
The room was quieter than when she’d been here before; fewer voices were coming from other directions. Some of the chairs had been rearranged, and two of them now sat empty at the far side. Mara took a few steps toward them—

And that was the moment when the young man's eyes widened and he broke out into a smile.
Mara wasn’t sure if she’d ever seen his teeth before or not, but now they looked remarkably white against his pink lips. She couldn’t help returning the grin as well.

“Jonah?” Mara asked, her voice trailing off to a question mark because this seemed very strange and also somehow appropriate? Was it just that he wasn't what she'd been expecting? “You... you live here, then?”

Jonah nodded enthusiastically, letting go of her hand finally as he pulled open the door to the main hallway outside.
“I do,” he said brightly, stepping back from the entranceway so that Mara would have plenty of room to follow him through. “Do you mind if we go into my office?”
Mara blinked.
“Your office?”
