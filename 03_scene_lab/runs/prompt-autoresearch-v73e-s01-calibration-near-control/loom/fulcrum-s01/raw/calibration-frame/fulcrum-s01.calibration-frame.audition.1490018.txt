
The next time the door opened, Mara looked over just in time to watch a pair of legs cross the threshold and disappear past her left elbow. Then she heard footsteps on the linoleum, followed by the creaking of an armchair and the click of a leather sole on tile, as its occupant moved away from the table by the hall door toward the couch that Jonah Reed had just vacated.
She turned her head to look.
It was Adrian, again—wearing the same suit as he’d been in before, only now with the jacket discarded on the floor behind him; the back of his white dress shirt, with its hidden buttons, was a little crumpled, but that might be where he’d just sat.

He looked up when he caught her glance, smiled, and held out a hand, palm up.
She met his gaze for a moment longer, and then stepped toward him to take it. She couldn’t remember whether they’d shaken hands or not; she might have been holding that glass of water the whole time. He looked her in the eye as he shook, and said something, but her brain caught no words through the ringing.
“Did you say something?” Mara asked after the handshake ended, feeling both foolish and uncertain.

He looked at her for a long moment, and then smiled and took the chair that she’d been standing near—the one with his jacket still under it. It had been a question: “How are you feeling now?”
She opened her mouth to respond but then heard his voice again before the words came.

“Your reading was remarkable,” he said, looking into her eyes, his voice clear and slow. “I’d like you to be one of my apprentices.”
Mara felt herself flush, from her cheeks all the way down her neck, and she didn’t know whether to smile or look away, or if there even was such a thing as ‘too late’. “I—”
“There is no obligation,” he interrupted her. “You may take some time to think it over.” He gave her another one of his small smiles, and then rose and moved to stand beside Jonah by the doorway, out of range of their conversation.

Jonah leaned over and looked at her for a few moments longer, before turning to say something quietly in Adrian’s ear.
