They stopped just before their lips touched. The pause crackled with tension, a moment that could slip into either comfort or danger. She hesitated, her breath catching in her throat. What did he want? Why did he pull back?

"I'm not going to force you to stay," he murmured, his voice low but firm. His fingers lingered on her wrist, the warmth of his palm grounding her.

She wanted to say something—anything—but the words tangled in her throat. She wanted to push him away. She wanted to lean in and close the distance herself. It was all too easy to lose herself in him, especially after everything that had happened.

His voice cut through the silence, cool and detached. "We need to talk about your training regimen."

She blinked, the tension dissipating like fog. "My training?" She repeated, a sliver of defensiveness creeping in.

"You've been avoiding certain exercises," he said, his tone almost clinical. "It's slowing your progress. We can adjust the schedule to accommodate your limits, but you need to be clear about what they are."

She clenched her jaw. Of course, he would focus on that—her physical shortcomings, her lack of discipline. It was easier to analyze her movements than to unravel the mess of desire and restraint between them.

"I'm aware," she muttered.

"You'll report directly to me at 0500 hours tomorrow," he continued, his voice unwavering. "I want your full commitment."

Her chest tightened. "You mean no excuses?"

"No excuses."

The kiss hovered just beyond reach, an unfinished thought. She could almost taste the promise of it—soft lips, warm breath, a moment of surrender. But they stood there instead, suspended in silence. She could feel his gaze on her, assessing, measuring, always holding back just enough to keep her guessing.

"What do you want?" she finally asked, her voice barely above a whisper.

His eyes narrowed, the flicker of vulnerability buried beneath layers of control. "I want you to trust me."

Her breath caught in her throat. Trust. The word hung between them, heavy and fragile. She wanted to trust him. She wanted to believe that his presence wasn't just another tool for the Fulcrum to exploit her. But after everything, it was impossible not to feel like a pawn in their game.

"And what if I can't?" she asked, her voice trembling.

He didn't respond immediately. Instead, he reached out and brushed a strand of hair behind her ear. His fingers lingered for a moment, a gentle touch that felt almost dangerous in its softness.

"Then we'll find a way to make it work," he said, his voice barely above a whisper.

The silence stretched between them, thick with unspoken words. She could feel his desire, the way it pulsed beneath the surface, waiting for permission to rise. But permission was something she wasn't willing to give. Not yet. Not when everything felt so fragile, so uncertain.

"We should get back," he said after a moment, stepping back just far enough to create a barrier between them. "The others will be waiting."

She nodded, her throat tight with a mix of longing and fear. "Okay."
