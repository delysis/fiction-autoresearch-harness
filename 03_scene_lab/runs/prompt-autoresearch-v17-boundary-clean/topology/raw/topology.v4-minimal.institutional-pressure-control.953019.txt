 "I'm not going to stay in this building forever."
"No, of course not," Livia said. "But--" She leaned forward with the mug, her breath hot on Mara's cheek. "We were hoping you might feel up to joining us tonight," she murmured in her ear. "Jonah and I are going on a date. It would be--"
"Livia!" Jonah said, sharp and low. His face was pinched, his lips pressed together.
"It would be--nice," Livia said, finishing her sentence before pulling away with a nervous laugh. "We could get dinner. Nothing fancy." She waved a hand at Jonah, then gave Mara a lopsided smile. "You can even wear the same outfit. Just as you are."
Mara looked past them to the hallway door. It was open and mostly empty now; a few stragglers lingered to ask questions about their readings. She was feeling it already, that urge to sink into her bed and disappear from the world for the next twelve hours. But Livia was looking at her with earnest concern, a warmth in her eyes that Mara didn't think she'd imagined. And Jonah... well, Jonah was still there too, wasn't he? And he hadn't told Livia to back off.
She glanced down at the mug of hot liquid--tea, maybe, or maybe just water with something added to make it more palatable. She took a sip and frowned. "What is this?"
"It's..." Livia trailed off and looked to Jonah for help. He shrugged, looking mildly exasperated. "Well," Livia said, giving Mara a pleading smile. "It's not exactly a drink."
Mara eyed it dubiously. "I'm supposed to drink it, right?"
"You could, if you want to."
Mara looked from one to the other. "Is it supposed to help me recover?"
Livia nodded eagerly. "It's supposed to be very good for your nervous system." She reached out to touch Mara's arm, her hand cool and firm against the bare skin. "I thought it might help you feel better after..." She trailed off again, flushing a little. "Well. After the reading."
Mara looked away. "It's not going to help me get ready for my date with you," she said, her tone dry.
"It might," Livia said, her eyes lighting up. "Or it might make things... interesting." She leaned closer, her face just a few inches from Mara's. "How's your day been?"
Mara blinked at her. "My day has been..." She paused, trying to think of something to say that didn't sound too negative or too sarcastic or too anything at all. She didn't know how to talk about herself when Livia was looking at her like that. "It's been okay," she settled on, hoping that would suffice.
"That's good," Livia said with a nod. "I mean, it's not like you haven't had plenty of things to deal with today." Her eyes flicked over toward the hallway again, then back to Mara. "You've done really well with all of that."
Mara tried to think of how to respond, but before she could come up with anything, Livia was speaking again. "We should head out," she said. "Do you need any help getting your stuff?"
"No," Mara said, shaking her head. "I'll be fine."
Livia didn't look convinced. "Are you sure?"
