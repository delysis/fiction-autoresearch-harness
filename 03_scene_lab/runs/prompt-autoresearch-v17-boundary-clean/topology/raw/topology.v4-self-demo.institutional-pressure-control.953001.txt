 "Besides, it's not like you can tell me no when I come to you for help. Just bring me my gearbag."
The amusement in Livia's expression had changed, and Mara recognized that her attempt at deflection had failed. But before Livia could speak, a movement caught the corner of her eye.
She turned toward the sound; she didn't move her head far, but she knew Jonah had to be able to see her full face now.
She had no idea how much information he might glean from this gesture alone, and her sense of security dropped precipitously. She let her gaze linger on him for a moment.
"Yes, Mara. We'll help you."
