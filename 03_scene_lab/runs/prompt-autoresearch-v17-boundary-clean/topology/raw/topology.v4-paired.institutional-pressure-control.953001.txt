 "We're not at a hotel with room service."
She wasn't sure exactly what Livia had been planning in case Mara hadn't gone quietly back to bed after her last alignment; that time, she'd fallen asleep as soon as she sat down. Livia's first comment tonight had also assumed that a hot drink and quiet space would be the best restorative: "Why don't you sit by the fire for a little while?"
Now Livia said, "I won't make you go to bed until you want to. I need to run through tomorrow's agenda with Jonah before I turn in myself. I can be ready to talk if you want me to, or I can go to the next room and let you have quiet."
Jonah held out an unlit candle while Livia lit it from a taper, then took it to a table at the far end of the room. Mara looked down and took the drink in both hands as she watched smoke curl away from the flame. Jonah's tone was professional: he'd take her lead in interpreting the alignment, but not before Mara rested.
"I think I should have something to eat." She spoke from her own experience rather than a script. "Are there apples? Or honey?"
She felt Livia's gaze as she looked up and drank from the mug. When she glanced away, Livia's smile was open and pleased. "Would you like me to make soup?" she said.
