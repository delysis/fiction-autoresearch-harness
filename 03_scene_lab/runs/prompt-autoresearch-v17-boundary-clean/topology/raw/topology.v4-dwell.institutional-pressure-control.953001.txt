She had other reasons not to ask for an official accommodation, even if she wanted one. And she was used to functioning in spite of limitations; it would be fine. She could give her body and mind what they needed and go right back to work again tomorrow.
"I know your team's prepared," Livia said, smiling faintly, "and that you are too. But I think you should still allow yourself a full break." Her eyes held Mara's until it became uncomfortable for Mara to look away. She drank deeply and waited for Livia to continue.
"There will always be a job to do," she said quietly, "and a choice between giving it your all or holding something back. And you'll always want to give everything you can." Livia's smile widened, and she set a hand on Mara's arm. It was a gesture Mara had come to think of as Livia's own; not an intimate touch, but a caring one, neither coercive nor distancing. Mara was used to feeling both of those things around most people; she'd long since stopped noticing how many weren't able to make that distinction.
She'd nearly forgotten that there was anyone left who could.
"You can always choose what you have to give and what you want to take," Livia said, her hand still on Mara's arm. "Even gods can't change that without your permission."
