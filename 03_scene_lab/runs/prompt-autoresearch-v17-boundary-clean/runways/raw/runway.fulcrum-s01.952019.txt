"Jonah," Adrian murmured, his gaze holding Mara's while his voice trailed off, "the world is changing." It was a statement of fact, devoid of emotion or agenda. Yet it carried weight—a challenge to everyone in the room to accept or deny the reality it represented.

Jonah met Adrian's eyes for a long moment, acknowledging the unspoken subtext before he turned back to Mara, his expression unreadable. "I'd like to show you something," he said softly, leading her down a corridor lined with paintings depicting ancient mythic battles. "This is one of Fulcrum's archives." He pointed to a canvas depicting Athena holding the head of Medusa. "The stories we tell ourselves shape who we are."

Mara's curiosity was piqued; she followed him into an inner room filled with books and artifacts. "Why do you think that?" she asked, her voice low.

"Because they create our reality," he replied.

The weight of his words settled in the air between them.
