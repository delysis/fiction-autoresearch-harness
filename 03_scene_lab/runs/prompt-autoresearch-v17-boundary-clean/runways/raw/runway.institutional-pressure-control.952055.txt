The warmth of the Fulcrum alignment session was quickly dissipating, leaving Mara depleted and exhausted. She had pushed herself hard during the session and now felt the strain in her muscles and bones. Mara tried to catch her breath but found that it was becoming increasingly difficult with each passing second. She knew she needed help, but her pride kept her from asking for it.
As she struggled to catch her breath, Livia noticed Mara’s distress. The concerned expression on her face showed that she was willing to offer help without hesitation or judgment. Mara felt a sudden wave of gratitude wash over her as she watched Livia move closer with purposeful strides.
Livia’s voice was soft but firm as she spoke: "Mara, let me give you some water first." With gentle hands, Livia helped Mara take small sips of cool water until she felt refreshed enough to speak again.
"Thank you," Mara said quietly as they sat down together in silence. Her voice sounded hoarse after so much effort exerted earlier during the Fulcrum alignment session.
