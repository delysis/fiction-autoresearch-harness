"How long does that usually take?"
Mara glanced over at Livia and Jonah, who'd come to collect her when the Fulcrum alignment was complete. Livia was waiting beside her with a warm drink; Jonah stood a bit behind and off to the side, his face neutral and his gaze focused on Mara's.
"Five, six hours." She stretched her arms, shaking out the tension. "I'll be fine after I take care of myself for a little while."
"Do you think so?"
Mara gave Livia a weary, faintly amused smile. "Do I have a choice?" she said.
