They walked toward the city, arms swinging to the same side. In the cool air, the warmth of another body had a comfort Mara could not deny. He waited beside her without intruding until he offered her a hand.

Taking it wasn’t as hard as she expected. Jonah’s palm held hers gently, his fingers as long and strong as hers were small.

She leaned in slightly, and his breath hitched as he returned her lean. Her lips brushed his softly and stayed that way.
