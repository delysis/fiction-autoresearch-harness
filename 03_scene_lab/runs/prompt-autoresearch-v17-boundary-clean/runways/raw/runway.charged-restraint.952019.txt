"No," said the doctor. He would not give Jonah the serum for Mara.
Mara stared at him, bewildered. "Why?" she asked.
The doctor gave a slight shrug. "The Fulcrum doesn't care about your wellbeing, Mara," he said. "They don't care about you."
Mara felt sick to her stomach. The doctor had been right all along—the Fulcrum was only interested in using her.
She looked up at Jonah. He was still standing there, his face a mask of stoicism. But she could see the hurt and disappointment in his eyes. He had trusted her, and now he knew she couldn't help him.
She walked away from him, not looking back. She needed to get away from the lab, needed to escape before she did something she would regret.
She didn't know where she was going, but she kept walking. She didn't stop until she was far away from the Fulcrum, far away from Jonah.
