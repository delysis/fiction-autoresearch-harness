Mara had never been more grateful to be alone with her work. The Fulcrum’s latest alignment required intense focus and careful precision, and every ounce of concentration was drained from her after hours spent fine-tuning the system’s parameters. With a sigh, she closed the tablet, grateful to have finished but drained. Her shoulders ached, and her thoughts were clouded with the complexities of the job.

Before she could fully rest, there was a knock at her door. It was Livia. She had that soft, professional expression she always wore, a slight smile that didn’t quite reach her eyes, and Mara knew instantly what Livia wanted. “You look exhausted,” Livia said as she entered, her tone neutral but her eyes scanning Mara with an intensity that made Mara uncomfortable.

Livia stepped closer, and Mara took an automatic step back. The professional distance felt less secure when Livia’s eyes flickered toward Jonah, still standing at the edge of the room, watching the exchange with an unreadable expression. “I can help,” Livia offered, gesturing to her medical kit, and Mara realized this wasn’t just a simple courtesy. This was an offer she wasn’t sure she could refuse.
