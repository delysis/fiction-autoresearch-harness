Esther had not expected the laughter that greeted her words. Simon's eyes crinkled in amusement. “You know,” he said, his voice taking on a mock-solemn tone, “when we were younger, you'd have just tackled me. Now we have words like 'costly disclosure' to contend with.”

She rolled her eyes, the familiar playfulness between them resurfacing after months of separation and tension. “Oh, please,” Esther countered, her own lips quirking up in a smile. “You're acting as if I'm suddenly some delicate flower. I can still throw a punch, you know.”

Simon chuckled, the sound rich and comforting. He moved closer, closing the physical distance between them. “I've never doubted your strength,” he murmured, his hands finding her shoulders. His touch was gentle yet possessive, a reminder of what they shared and had nearly lost.

The atmosphere shifted subtly. Esther allowed herself to relax into his embrace, the warmth of his body soothing the lingering unease. “You never told me,” she murmured, her voice soft.

Simon sighed, pulling back slightly to look into her eyes. “I thought it was better left unsaid. I didn't want you to...”
