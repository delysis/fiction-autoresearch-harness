They walked side by side, the hum of the city a constant backdrop. He spoke, his voice low. "That was intense," he said simply, leaving room for her to choose how much to reveal. The words landed with unexpected weight, a recognition of the toll it took.

She nodded, her eyes tracing the intricate patterns etched into the pavement. The silence wasn't uncomfortable.
