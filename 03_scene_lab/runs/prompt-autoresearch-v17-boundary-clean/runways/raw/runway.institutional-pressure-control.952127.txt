Mara’s breath shuddered through her nostrils, damp and warm against the cold metal of her control rod. The Fulcrum Alignment session had drained her; the effort to synchronize the frequencies had left her physically and mentally exhausted.

“You look like you could use some rest, Mara,” Livia remarked, her tone gentle but firm. The older woman’s concern was genuine, but it didn’t change the fact that she held authority over Mara’s schedule—and Mara knew she was expected to recover quickly.

“I’m fine,” Mara replied, her voice a bit weaker than she intended. “Just need a minute.”

Livia didn’t seem entirely convinced. She moved closer, offering a glass of water that Mara had nearly forgotten was there. “I’ve adjusted your next session. You can take some time now,” she said, her grip steady but reassuring. “Let’s get you somewhere quieter.”

For a brief moment, Mara wondered if Livia was simply being kind or if she was setting her up for further scrutiny. But the offer of genuine care made it hard to argue. Mara took the water, feeling the pressure in her head begin to ease, even just a bit.
