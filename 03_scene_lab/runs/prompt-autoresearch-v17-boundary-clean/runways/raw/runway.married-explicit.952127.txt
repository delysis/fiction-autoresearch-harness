Esther's fingers paused in the shower's lukewarm spray, tracing the faded scar that now wrapped her left wrist in a rough bracelet. Simon watched her from their bedroom doorframe, his eyes lingering on the pale crescent before meeting hers in the mirror. The quiet lingered between them like morning mist on glass.

"You're back," she said at last, her voice softer than she'd intended, like someone afraid to break something fragile.
Simon took a step forward, each footfall echoing against the bathroom tile. "I've been here all night. You just didn't want to see me." His gaze dropped to the bandage that covered his right hand's knuckles, evidence of his earlier collision with furniture. "Your choice, not mine."

She turned toward him, water dripping from her bare shoulders to collect in small puddles at his feet. "I chose a lot of things last night, Simon. Maybe if you had—"

A sharp knock interrupted her. The bathroom door creaked open before she could object, and a uniformed officer stepped into their shared space, eyes widening briefly at their state of undress before focusing on a clipboard.
