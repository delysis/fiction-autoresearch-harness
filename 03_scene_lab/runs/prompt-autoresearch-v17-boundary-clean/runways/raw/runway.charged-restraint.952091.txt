"So I need the next two nights free."

Jonah paused and looked at Mara, holding out his hand without making it an offer. She looked down at his arm, then at his face. No words about their relationship, or who had to agree to what. No judgment about how he had touched her or how she had responded. He just waited.

She took his hand and turned it palm up. Her palm met the calluses and warmth; her fingertips brushed against the slight roughness of his stubble. No one else could offer this.

She touched the stubble more firmly, letting her fingers linger. "I have some reading to do for the next case," she said.
