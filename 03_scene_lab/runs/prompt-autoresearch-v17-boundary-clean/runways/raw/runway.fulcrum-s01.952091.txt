She hadn’t asked much about Fulcrum except the basics: a few questions about the purpose and focus of the experiment.
Now, walking through the doors into the center itself, she felt as if she'd walked onto another planet. The arrangement of furniture in the main room, the soft light coming from behind the walls, the colors of the floor and ceiling, the way the lights on the walls were arranged—all of these felt like they had been crafted by aliens. And she hadn't been wrong; the aliens she'd encountered so far all seemed to be in on this joke, too.
She looked around at the people waiting to speak with her—the two women who had been talking together were now sitting together in silence as she walked toward them—and noticed their expressions. They were both smiling slightly, looking up at her as if they'd known she would walk into their midst like this.
"Hello."
She offered her hand first to the taller woman, but the woman immediately shook her head no and gestured to the other one. She felt her face warm slightly—was this some sort of calibration test?
