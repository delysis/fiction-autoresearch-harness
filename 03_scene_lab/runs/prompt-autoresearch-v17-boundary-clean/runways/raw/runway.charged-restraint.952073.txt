Mara pulled the hood of her cloak up as the Fulcrum lab doors clicked shut. The cold outside bit through the heavy fabric, reminding her how long she had been inside.

She shifted her weight from foot to foot, wondering which direction to take now that Jonah had offered to walk her back. His hand hung in the air between them, palm facing up. She studied his expression—calm, respectful, without the usual smug satisfaction.

"I wasn’t sure if you’d be comfortable with me escorting you back," he said, his voice even. "It’s just that the Fulcrum isn’t the safest area this late."

Mara hesitated, her fingers itching to reach for his arm or shoulder in the way she used to, before everything changed. Instead, she lifted her gaze to meet his. She hadn’t been able to shake the weight of guilt from her shoulders, but he seemed to have made a conscious choice not to bring it up. Not yet.

"It’s fine," she replied softly, reaching up to take his hand. His palm was warm, his touch solid and unhurried. She couldn’t help the flicker of heat in her chest.

Their steps fell in sync as they began walking down the hallway
