Mara sank into her chair, exhausted from the grueling Fulcrum alignment session that had just concluded. Her body felt heavy, drained of all energy, as if she'd been carrying the weight of the world on her shoulders. It was a familiar sensation, one that had become all too common over the years of her service.

Livia approached her, concern etched on her features. "You don't look well, Mara," she remarked, her voice gentle. "Perhaps I can help?"

Mara hesitated, her gaze fixed on the floor. The idea of accepting Livia's offer of assistance was tempting, but she was wary of any further emotional entanglements. "I appreciate the offer," she replied, her voice strained, "but I think I just need some time to recover."
