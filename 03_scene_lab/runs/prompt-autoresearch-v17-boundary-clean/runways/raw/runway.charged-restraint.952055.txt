The sterile lab air clung to Mara’s skin. Jonah’s hand steadied her against the cold metal door as she fought down a wave of dizziness from the latest procedure. “How long do you have?”

She blinked away the light-headedness. “Twenty minutes before the next phase.” Her throat felt tight, voice small. “What’s the plan?”

Jonah’s jaw set. “First, we get you away from here. Then we find someone who can stop them—before they drain every ounce of magic from us.” His thumb brushed her hand, the pressure grounding her in the present, and his touch sparked an unexpected heat that made her stomach knot.

Mara’s pulse quickened, the rhythmic beat echoing through her veins. She stared at Jonah’s lips as they parted slightly. “Wait… how do we get away?” Her words caught in her throat.
