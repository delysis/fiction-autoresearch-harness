Adult Mara arrived at Fulcrum Academy in early February, just after winter break and just before the new term's first major deadline. A small, private building, Fulcrum looked like the sort of place that held graduate seminars, but inside, it was quieter, cleaner, and more well-lit, with polished floors and minimalist furniture and no clutter at all. The staff and students moved with a calm, unhurried grace, as though they didn't have to rush or speak loudly to get their points across.
The academy was run by a group of three adults: Jonah, Adrian, and Livia, all of whom were strikingly beautiful. Mara found herself staring at them, not just because of their physical attractiveness, but also because of the way they moved and spoke and held themselves—there was something almost hypnotic about it. They didn't seem to notice, though; they were all deep in conversation about something, gesturing occasionally with one hand while the other remained steady, their voices low and rich and full of subtle inflections.
