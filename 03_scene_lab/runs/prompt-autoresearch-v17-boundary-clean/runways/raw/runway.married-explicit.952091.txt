Simon stared at her while she was taking a shower.
He watched her soap herself, heard her humming in a lighthearted manner, and couldn't help but wonder about her. He'd known Esther for most of their lives, and in many ways, it felt like he'd been with her forever.
She was his best friend, his confidante, and his lover.
But it had been so long since they'd had intimacy of any kind that it almost seemed foreign.
In a way, they were like strangers again—strangers who shared memories and history but didn't share any recent intimacy.
Esther turned off the water and stepped out of the shower, reaching for her towel. Simon watched her intently, taking in every curve of her body. He could see a few bruises on her arm, left over from their last encounter with the gang members. He wanted to reach out and touch them, but instead, he just stared.
"You're awfully quiet," she said softly as she dried her hair.
