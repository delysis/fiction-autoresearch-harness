Mara's hand burned under Jonah's. A pulse of heat, then nothing, no echo in her skin or bones, and the empty spot at her left side made a sudden hollow ache. She squeezed his hand, hard enough to bruise, then dropped it. "This won't work, Jonah."

"I'm sorry," he said. "I can't control this."

She pulled away from him, hands on her hips, and stared him down. "Then maybe you shouldn't be doing it."

He reached out to touch her arm, and she jerked away. "Mara, I--"

She slapped his hand aside. "No," she said, the word a hiss between clenched teeth. "No, you can't do this to me. You can't. You don't--you don't have that power over me."

The power she'd given him. The power they'd given her. Her own power, taken and twisted and made into something she didn't even recognize anymore.

She turned her back on him, fists clenched tight, and walked away.
