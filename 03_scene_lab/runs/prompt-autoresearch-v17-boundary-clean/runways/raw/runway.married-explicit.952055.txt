Simon rolled onto his back, his face pointed to the ceiling where a pale green light from the corridor spilled onto him. He stared for a moment, blinking to try and make himself see anything else. There was only the ceiling.

Esther, on her side facing him, reached up and cupped his face with one hand. Her thumb caressed his lower lip while the other rested lightly on his chest. "Are you all right?" she whispered.

Simon's body tightened at the feel of her touch. Her hand on him was a lifeline in the darkness; it grounded him. He blinked once more before turning to meet her eyes. "I don't know. I don't know if I'll ever be all right again."

"You will," Esther said firmly as she leaned in close to his face. "I know it."

She pressed her lips lightly against his cheek before moving to the side of his face near the temple and kissed him there too. The light from the corridor flickered onto her face, illuminating it with an unearthly glow. For a moment, Simon couldn't help but feel as though they were being watched by something more powerful than anything he could understand.
