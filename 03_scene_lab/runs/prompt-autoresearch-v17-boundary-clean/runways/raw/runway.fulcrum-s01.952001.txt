Mara entered the Fulcrum conference room and looked around. She wasn't surprised to see four chairs; her interview invitation had listed the participants' names in order of seniority, and three chairs were occupied, while a fourth was empty in a space between her seat and Adrian's. But it took a moment to realize that each of the chairs was subtly different. Her chair was leather-wrapped; it rose from a narrow base with a low, flat back. The chairs beyond hers were upholstered in blue suede and had taller backs, with a lower, wider seat. Then, at the far end of the table, was an elaborate, wing-backed black velvet chair with a high, square back and a wide seat. It sat empty.

Mara glanced toward the only other unoccupied space. It had the same low-back leather chair that hers had, and the empty chair was directly across from hers, on the other side of the table. She took the empty seat closest to the door. It felt fine. But when her eyes moved across the room, the subtle differences between the chairs made the space seem oddly structured and balanced, rather than just being a matter of taste.
