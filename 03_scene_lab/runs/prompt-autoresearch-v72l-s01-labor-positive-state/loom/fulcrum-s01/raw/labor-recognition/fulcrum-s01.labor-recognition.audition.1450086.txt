The third man turned to Mara, held out his hand, and smiled.
“I’m Andrew.”
When the young woman spoke up, Mara didn’t expect that. They could have introduced each other; she could have waited her turn, even stayed silent. But she didn’t.

“Mara!” The girl came into the room then, in a full-length black dress that flared out at her ankles and a shawl covering her head in long triangular folds, walking straight toward Mara with her arms wide and ready. When Mara held out her own, the girl threw her arms around her shoulders and hugged, holding on for several full seconds before pulling away with a smile.

“Hi!”
And then she reached out to shake hands, too, and said her name.
“Amelia.”
