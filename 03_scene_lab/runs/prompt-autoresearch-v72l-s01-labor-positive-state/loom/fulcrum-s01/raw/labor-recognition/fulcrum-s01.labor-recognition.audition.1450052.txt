The brown-haired fellow didn’t release Jonah's hand even while talking to Mara: a quick handshake, a greeting, then a friendly nod, his gaze moving right on to take in Jonah from head to toe and then her again. Finally the other fellow dropped Jonah's hand and went back to his conversation.
Meanwhile, a woman in blue jeans and a blue Fulcrum T-shirt had set a plate on the counter in the kitchen and now stood behind the door in the entrance hall, not entering or leaving; she was turning back and forth, listening to the two fellows talk.

When she heard the next voice, a female voice from the direction of her office, Mara turned and smiled at her new visitor. But as her glance went back toward the kitchen, something struck her. The woman in the Fulcrum T-shirt had picked up Jonah's half-full cup and moved it off to one side while carrying on her conversation with the other fellow. He still didn't notice what she was doing.
