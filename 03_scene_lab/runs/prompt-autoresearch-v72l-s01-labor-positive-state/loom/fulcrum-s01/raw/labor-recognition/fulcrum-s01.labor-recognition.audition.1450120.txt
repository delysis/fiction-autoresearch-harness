After that, there was a moment when Mara wasn't quite sure where the brown-haired fellow would go next, but he didn't linger. He made an almost inaudible noise that might have been clearing his throat or might have been preparing to say something more; Mara could have sworn it had some kind of musical intonation to it, almost like a tuning fork. But then his eyes flickered toward her and then back down the hall, where there was a door closing. And then he looked back at her and offered his hand as well, like an afterthought, though it was clear from his expression that he'd been trying not to show any hesitation over what was supposed to happen next.
When she took the young fellow's hand, the conversation behind them continued. Mara saw someone walk by in the background on the way to the bathroom at the end of the hallway—a blur of a person, short and stocky, with graying black hair; Jonah shifted out of the way as they walked by without looking up from the brown-haired man he'd just introduced himself to.
Then, as their brief introduction concluded, the young man turned and walked away down the hallway in the opposite direction—and the person who'd gone past earlier came out of the bathroom. The older fellow stepped over to the table against the wall and picked up the plastic cup, and then carried it away with him to the sink—all while the conversation between Jonah and the young man continued at full volume, with both of them talking at once.
