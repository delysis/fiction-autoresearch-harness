They had made some progress in talking before Mara saw the young fellow’s half-full cup of wine with the Fulcrum logo on it. She hadn't realized that the compound had a bar where drinks could be served, and she was vaguely aware that most people here avoided alcohol to ensure their alertness.

Then the fellow tossed it back in one gulp without setting it down, and said to Mara, "So how do you like the place?" And Mara felt her gut clench.

His conversational tone didn't match her expectations of what the question might be intended for; he sounded a little tipsy or inattentive as he asked it. It was possible that he had just been thinking about what a strange place the institute must appear to her as a newcomer and then said the first thing that popped into his head. But he’d seemed to understand perfectly well when she was speaking before, so how drunk could he be? She didn't think this fellow would deliberately go for that particular provocation at all; it simply didn't fit his behavior or her sense of how far he was likely to push social boundaries. But if not him, then who, or what?

When he saw her glance down at the empty glass in his hand, he laughed and said, "Sorry about this. I'm supposed to be working on being more present in the moment." He handed it back to her, and Mara took it automatically before remembering how it must look: she was holding a stranger’s empty wine cup.
