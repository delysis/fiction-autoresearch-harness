Mara watched, standing next to the two of them by the hallway doors, as he went back and picked up his empty glass and started in that direction, still chatting. He set the empty plastic glass down on a round table across from where she'd seen him leave the full one earlier. And walked away with Jonah.

She looked around again at the other people in the room. They were all either talking or making drinks. And it felt like they had all looked over at her now, and were waiting for her to speak up and join a conversation. But she still hadn't heard a word of what any of them were saying, because she was too busy watching for how the blond young man—Jonah—had just left his plastic glass on the wrong table, where she herself would be looking for a trash can.

She looked at that table again and then at Jonah’s retreating back. For a moment, she wanted to follow him, turn the tables. But he had moved too far, had left her behind for a conversation with a group of people that didn’t even include the first friend he’d brought into the room. She thought about how the blond one had been there in the common area already when she had first come in, and then he had gotten up and introduced himself to her and made sure she felt included, and now he didn’t need her anymore. She was part of the furniture for now.
