“Then why are you here?” he asked. She could hear the hurt in his voice, even through the rain.
She turned away from him and watched the lights of the cars passing on the road. She didn’t want to go back to the apartment and face another day of what this had become. She felt as if she was losing control of her mind and body. “I don’t know.”
“So what are you waiting for?”
Mara looked away from the lights and turned back toward Jonah. She tried not to think of what had happened and focused on what she felt in her body. Her skin was wet and cold, but inside, a fire was still burning. It made her feel alive and powerful. “I don’t want to go home.”
