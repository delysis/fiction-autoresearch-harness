

A drop of water fell on Mara’s cheek. She looked up and saw a single light from the street above. The rain would start again in fifteen minutes. She looked at Jonah. He still had his hands in his pockets. The creases were deeper now, but he still walked with his shoulders squared as if he expected something.

She wondered if he felt like this at the end of every session. His body moved, but he had a stillness in his eyes. She was afraid to touch him. She was afraid to break the silence.

He walked through the door without turning back. He paused in the doorway and looked over his shoulder.
“You want to come inside?”

Mara felt her throat close, but she managed to shake her head. She could feel his eyes on her. It was as if he could read her thoughts, and she wished she could run from him. She turned away and walked down the sidewalk. The rain had started to fall harder now. It was dark again, and the street was silent.
