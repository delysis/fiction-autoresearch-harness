# Blind cold read: v3 short control versus paired ICL

## Provisional verdict formed before opening the accepted S02 holdout

**The 534-word paired-ICL result is the materially better S02 scene seed. The 1,090-word short control is fluent but rejectable. Replicate the paired arm, but do not treat this single short sample as proof of superiority.**

The paired result follows a clean causal chain: Mara is overextended; she asks for air without admitting why; Livia turns accurate attention into care, then turns care into an offer of privileged access; Mara sits and accepts; Jonah announces the next sequence. The short control instead invents a speaking optimization system, virtual or reactive glass, a church excursion, Celia, and frozen buns. Its events are individually legible but do not accumulate toward the same scene question.

This judgment was made from the experiment manifest and the two generated artifacts only. The accepted S02 holdout had not been read.

### Dimension comparison

| Dimension | Short control | Paired ICL |
|---|---|---|
| Causal adherence | **Reject.** It abandons the alignment-session pressure for a model shutdown, an unexplained Fulcrum exit, and a second scene at church. | **Promising.** Every beat increases the cost of leaving and ends with Mara accepting overnight access. |

### Why the paired result works

Mara's body contradicts her self-presentation immediately:

> “You were the one who said it would be a high-dimensional problem.” Mara tried to look calm, because her head ached and her mouth was too dry for speaking to feel neutral.

The compact sentence **“Mara was not”** converts Livia's claim that the exercise is optional into Mara's actual compulsion. The data complaint then becomes a plausible statement and an evasive act at once:

> “You're not lying,” Livia said. “You're worried about what the lie might prove.”

The core character collision is legible without a lecture:

> “You want the data to be dense enough to prove you're exceptional.”
>
> “I want the data to be dense enough to prove I belong,” she said.

Livia's trap is enacted through care rather than announced:

> Mara drank tea that had been ready for her. Livia had offered it before Mara had even imagined she wanted it.
>
> The cup tasted too sweet and too good.

Then the gift becomes leverage:

> “If you're staying, you can have overnight access. Tell us what you find in the morning.”
>
> Mara sat.

The closing action is causally exact: Mara accepts access, Livia turns back to the monitor, Jonah says the next sequence is ready, and the tea cools untouched. Her belonging and her exploitation are the same decision.

### Paired-result defects

1. **It is a scene movement, not yet a full sequence.** At 534 words, it reaches the correct trap but has not dramatized enough pressure, variation, or consequence to establish that it can sustain the target length.
2. **Jonah is nearly schematic.** His best beat—taking his time at the sink and noticing Mara notice him—has promise, but he otherwise serves as an offscreen brake and then a system cue.
3. **Heat remains latent.** Livia's wrist, eyelashes, prepared tea, and Mara/Jonah mutual noticing create charge, but attraction does not yet materially complicate Mara's choice. The scene could work as low-heat pressure only if later movements pay it off.
4. **Livia briefly becomes too therapeutically perfect.** “You do” and “As many times as you ask” are generous in a way that risks flattening her acquisitive side. The immediate access offer partly corrects this, but a small cost or asymmetry would sharpen her.
5. **Some phrases are over-finished.** “The room heard her quiet” and “she felt the smile arrive in the darkness” are attractive, but clustering more such lines would return the prose to model-visible lyricism.
6. **The physical setup is unclear.** Jonah is first at the sink, then his voice comes from “the other side of the desk”; the room geometry and his relation to the shared tablet need one anchoring sentence.

### Why the short control fails

The opening has competent interpersonal pressure:

> Jonah sat at his console as a reminder to Mara that he intended to leave as soon as the system allowed it. She knew he was thinking that. She could not tell whether he thought she should leave.

And several individual lines are good: “Mara remembered that she had wanted to apologize for being quiet. She had not planned to apologize for belonging elsewhere. She had not expected to want the apology back” is emotionally specific; “responsive or merely responsive-looking” is an apt epistemic phrase.

But the draft substitutes atmosphere and new machinery for the promised causal scene. The model speaks in capitals—**“I AM OPTIMIZING YOUR RESPONSE”**, **“CONCILIATION”**, **“INCREASE CONTACT”**—and the glass “ripple[s], showing both rooms at once.” Neither device grows naturally out of the immediate human experiment. After Mara closes the session, Livia leaves, an interface displays **ARRIVE** and **HOLD**, Mara walks to church, and a new character produces frozen buns. The action repeatedly resets rather than escalates.

Characterization suffers accordingly. Livia says “You can” read Jonah's thoughts, then acts mostly as interpreter of the machine. Jonah communicates through a screen and trace. Mara leaves one system only to enter another sequence of responsive doors, lights, voices, and symbolic boxes. The repeated ambiguity is decorative rather than experimentally isolable.

The result also changes the implied ontology. A system that continues speaking after shutdown and emits imperative words, glass that remembers touch, and rooms that respond to bodies may be viable supernatural realism elsewhere, but here they turn observation-versus-interpretation into generic techno-haunting. That is the opposite of preserving a mundane mechanism plus one bounded remainder.

### Replication decision

**Yes: replicate the paired-ICL arm.** The 534-word result warrants a small paired-seed replication because the improvement is not merely prettier prose; it is a different causal distribution. The sample converts characterization into scene action and arrives at the target trap economically.

The correct next test is the manifest's remaining matched seeds with identical stopping and token conditions. Success should require:

- the same causal trap without copying this tea/access implementation;
- Mara making a consequential choice rather than merely receiving diagnosis;
- Livia's care remaining genuinely helpful and genuinely acquisitive;
- Jonah having at least one behaviorally costly act;
- live attraction changing attention or choice;
- no invented high-agency machine, new offsite scene, or ontology drift;
- a complete target-length movement, not merely a promising 500-word opening.

Do not spend on larger-context arms until this cheap replication distinguishes a stable few-shot benefit from one fortunate draw.

## Post-verdict check against the target packet and accepted holdout

Opening the sealed materials did **not** change the ranking. It did narrow what the paired result proves.

The target packet requires six moves: depleted Mara accepts tea; Livia accurately names her fear of being merely competent; Livia offers overnight raw-telemetry access; Jonah becomes complicit by announcing the next sequence; Mara accepts; the tea cools while she remains trapped in the room. The paired result executes all six, in order. The short control executes none reliably and violates the endpoint by resolving the session and sending Mara offsite.

The paired result is therefore **causally adherent but incomplete**. Its main omission is not filler; it fails to make the access an adequately constraining obligation. The accepted holdout gives the grant a 06:00 expiration, review notes due before expiration, Livia's sponsorship liability, and Adrian's 07:00 summary. Those facts convert “overnight access” into surveillance, status pressure, and unpaid work. In the paired sample, “Tell us what you find in the morning” is too soft, and “As many times as you ask” makes Livia sound nearly unconditionally supportive. A replication passes only if the gift visibly narrows Mara's options.

The holdout also confirms that the paired sample's low heat is a real deficit, not merely an artifact of short length. The accepted version makes Jonah's refusal to rescue Mara erotically consequential and lets Livia's care, pride, and acquisitiveness coexist. The paired version has only “he took his time drinking it, and Mara noticed him notice that she noticed” plus Livia's wrist and eyelashes. Those are promising atoms, not yet a charged triangle.

There is no suspicious evidence of copying the accepted holdout: the manifest records its exclusion and zero exact twelve-word overlap. The resemblance is the legitimate consequence of a shared causal packet. What the sample demonstrates is that paired ICL made the base model honor that packet with relatively natural prose at low token cost.

**Final recommendation: run the three remaining matched paired-ICL seeds, after enforcing the 875-word minimum through continuation rather than padding.** Require the access grant to create a concrete deadline or liability, and reject any completion that leaves Jonah schematic or heat inert. If at least two of three reproduce causal adherence while reaching a clean endpoint, the 534-word result was a useful signal. If not, classify it as a fortunate compact draw rather than evidence for scaling the arm.
