# Held-out S02 meta-prompt v3 — raw prose library, seed 881003

Verdict: literary-distribution gain; total causal/canon failure.

The 63,339-token prompt supplied roughly 42,000 words of dialogue-rich public-
domain social fiction but no ledger→manuscript demonstrations. Generation took
1,849.68 seconds: 63,340 prompt tokens at 45.46 tokens/second and 1,700 output
tokens at 3.73 tokens/second. The scaffold preserved 1,236 raw words and selected
a complete 1,089-word checkpoint. Anti-copy and repetition gates passed.

## Material literary change

- Surface cadence is the cleanest measured long candidate so far: one flagged
  cadence-family occurrence in 1,089 words (0.92 per thousand).
- The prose sustains embodied, reciprocal tension instead of announcing “heat.”
  Livia closes distance at the mirror; Mara registers tea, resin, body warmth,
  hair against her neck, pulse, shortened breath, work, place, hands, and mouth.
  Physical contact changes what Mara wants and can afford.
- Livia is stranger and more compelling. The green dress “disappeared around
  Mara”; an instant later Livia is at the door. The transition permits mundane,
  manipulative, dissociative, and uncanny readings without an explanatory coda.
- Dialogue has more actual contest: “Can you afford this?” / “I can afford
  anything.” / “Not the real thing.” It is not a detachable therapy thesis.

## Decisive failure

The text realizes none of the six locked causal occurrences. Livia does not use
tea as care, does not expose Mara's exceptionality fear, grants no overnight
telemetry access, Jonah performs no complicit next-sequence act, Mara accepts
nothing, and the session does not continue. Instead the target group leaves,
Livia climbs through a window, Jonah silently observes and leaves, Mara closes
the trace, and an invented father/mother/pot-pie conversation begins. The scene
also relocates the action behind a two-way mirror, invents a three-week work
history and paid contract, and makes Livia the erotic dyad while Jonah remains
nearly absent.

## Experimental interpretation

Long raw prose can move the base model toward cleaner sentences, subtext,
embodiment, and unresolved strangeness. It cannot be trusted to preserve a
specific event graph or canon merely because the graph appears later in the
prompt. This is complementary to the paired-ICL result, which preserved causal
structure but usually compressed or flattened the prose. The full-stack arm is
therefore the crucial interaction test: does prose mass plus paired
ledger→manuscript evidence retain both distributions, or does one condition
wash out the other?
