She stood up, and Livia smiled at her, at least this time not with a knowing leer. Mara tried not to grimace back as she left the room, and she was fairly certain she succeeded. It was all she could do.

Livia caught her elbow. "No rest for the weary, I know." She pressed one hand to Mara's lower back, the other against her elbow as if to lead her into some private alcove or corridor. It was not a gesture with which Mara was familiar.

"Are you all right?" Livia asked.

"Of course, Livia," she snapped, but her own words stung more than Livia's did.

Livia released her back to rest one palm lightly on Mara's shoulder.
