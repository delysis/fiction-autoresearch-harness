Mara had no idea what she was about to witness, but the instant she crossed the threshold of the Fulcrum research center, she knew it was different from anything she'd encountered before. A beauty both exquisite and alien permeated the space, an architecture arranged around social asymmetry. Adrian, a scientist of enigmatic intent, framed a calibration game that rewarded players for their behavioral accuracy, not for the explanations they gave.
Livia, a woman whose presence seemed to subtly bend light itself, made unnervingly accurate reads through posture, timing, and vocal response. Jonah, her enigmatic partner in this dance of calibration, declined to display Mara as an object of expertise, paying a visible social cost for restraint. A wrist-contact exercise made attraction alter Mara's attention and experimental judgment in unexpected ways. Deliberately changing one cue channel, Mara discovered which apparent insights depended on feedback and which were genuinely perceptive. As some accuracy collapsed, a smaller, multiply interpretable remainder survived.
