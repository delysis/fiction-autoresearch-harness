Esther lay supine, the back of her neck stretched taut against the cool sheets and her face turned to the open window. She watched a moth dance against the screen, fluttering like an orange silk scarf, until it dropped dead and Simon said, "What's wrong now?"

"Nothing," she said. "Everything."

"Okay, well, everything's something," he said. He put his hands on her hips and rubbed one thumb over the curve of her hipbone, as if that would do the trick.
