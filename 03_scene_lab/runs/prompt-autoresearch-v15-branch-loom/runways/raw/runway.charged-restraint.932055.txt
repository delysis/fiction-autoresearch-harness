The man offers his hand.
He does not know whether she desires him, but he knows her touch will calm her, and he offers.
She wants him too. But she has promised herself not to become so lost in him. She has promised herself the freedom to leave.
Her fingers brush his palm.
He leads her.
Her hand aches and her palm itches under his. She pulls away.
He tries to hold her. She jerks out of his grip.
His eyes grow wide with hurt. Her eyes narrow.
She watches for the twitch of his lip, the tilt of his head that means anger is coming. It will come, it always does, but she is ready this time. He will not own this moment, not this time.
He turns away from her.
And she takes him by surprise, as he never dares to take her, by kissing him.
