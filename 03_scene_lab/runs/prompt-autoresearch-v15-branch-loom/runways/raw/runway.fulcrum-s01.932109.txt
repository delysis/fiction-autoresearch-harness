The ship slowed as the Fulcrum came into view, a sprawling, tiered city that rose up out of a sea of fog. Its architecture was a mix of ancient and cutting-edge technology, with golden domes and spires reaching high into the sky, and sleek, modern buildings nestled in between. The contrast was breathtaking, and Mara couldn't help but stare in awe as the ship docked.
The Fulcrum was built by the Aumans, and their influence could be felt in the beauty and attention to detail of every inch of the city. It was a place where elegance was valued above all else, where everything was perfectly arranged to create a sense of harmony and balance. And yet, even from afar, Mara could sense that beneath this facade of beauty, there was something else at play.
