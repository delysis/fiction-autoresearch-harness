A hard-won agreement had let her return to the field, on condition she’d visit the lab weekly for “maintenance.” Today Jonah had taken a long time coaxing her in circles with his hand, testing her responses.

“All right?” He offered a palm.

Mara put her own hand in it. The skin felt cool and the touch reassuring. “All right.”

His lips brushed hers, the way a flame flickers in a breeze. She could only barely taste him. It took her longer than he expected to recognize and return the kiss, and she tasted it, just briefly, before pulling back.

Jonah’s smile was too warm to be a smirk.

Her body, which she knew well, seemed a little slow to respond, a little more distant, but she couldn’t be sure if it was true or in her mind. It felt as if his attention held her at the center, where a small, hot, soft thing grew impatient. “I’d better go.”

“Yeah.” Jonah smiled and kissed her cheek.
