Mara watched a man and a woman spar, the man in an elegant white dinner jacket and the woman in a black sheath with a single shoulder strap. The man's face was hidden but he moved with authority, while the woman leaned forward confidently, her gaze traveling across his body as she talked. The man shifted position to take her in at a new angle, then reached out to touch her elbow, and her voice lifted sharply. She stepped back, but her tone grew more insistent and the man turned away to avoid eye contact as he tried to keep the conversation going.

"It's all bullshit," Mara said, but she couldn't look away. She'd come here to confirm the scientific evidence that sex had no place in this crowd, but even without touch they radiated an impossible kind of attraction.
