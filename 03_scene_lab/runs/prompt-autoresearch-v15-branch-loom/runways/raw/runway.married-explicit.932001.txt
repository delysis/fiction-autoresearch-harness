"He's not the boy I married." The man behind her snorted. "If you want a boy, let me get my son on the phone. He'd be happy to show you the way."
Esther snorted too, feeling her lips pull to her teeth. She turned to face him.
She'd never seen her husband so naked, but he might as well be dressed. All muscle, bone, and scar; no place to hide or hide from. "I don't want your boy," she said. "I want my husband, the man you weren't, the one you abandoned when—"
He grabbed her arms and kissed her—a sudden, painful assault of teeth and stubble that shocked and sickened her. "Don't you say that," he rasped, and she felt the burn of his eyes on her skin.
"Don't you do that." She spat back, but it didn't matter. They were the same words, and they were going to get said regardless.
