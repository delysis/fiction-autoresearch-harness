She's gone. Simon wakes to the weight of a bed hollowed by an absence he knows well. His sleep has been lighter ever since he gave her the key and he could have sworn he'd felt her stir, felt her shift. But all his body bears now is the cold shadow of what used to be her space in their bed.
Simon's hand gropes at the pillow where her head had been. His fingers come back dry, no longer slicked with the residue of Esther's oil-slick hair. His mind goes through a quick list of things that could have sent her fleeing in the night: did she think he'd been cheating? Did she find evidence that he's still going out into the field? Did she hear one of the phone calls, decipher a coded message from the spy he was supposed to have killed?
"Where're you going, E?" he says aloud in the darkness as he slips from the bed.
Esther had asked him once if they ever used protection. She'd always been so careful. So...prudent.
