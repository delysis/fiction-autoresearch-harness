Livia released her chin and drew her finger slowly down her throat. The touch left a faint trail of heat on Mara's skin. "I won't force you," she said quietly.

Mara knew what she wanted, but there were things she couldn't give Livia without losing everything she had gained over the past few days. She felt dizzy just thinking about it.

"Mara?"

She swallowed again, hard, and licked her lips. "What do you want?" she asked.

Mara closed her eyes and breathed deep through her nose, then let it out in a long sigh. "I want you to stop. Right here."

A chuckle escaped Livia's lips, soft and cruel. "You want me to stop?" she asked quietly.
