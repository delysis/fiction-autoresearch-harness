 Simon watches the sheet slip to the floor and the rest of her body follow. Her hair is still up in a bun.
"Are we playing again?" he asks.
She turns, not answering. The moonlight glints on her earrings.

A long silence passes while Esther stands before the window. The moonlight pools on her legs, her bare feet. The sheet is crumpled on the floor. She is still wearing her clothes.
Simon: "What are you looking for?"
Esther: "Nothing."
Simon: "Are you still dressed for a reason?"

Esther turns back to face him. Her face is cold in the moonlight. Her hair is still up in a bun. She doesn't speak for a long time, her lips moving silently. Finally, she says, "No."
She comes toward him, sitting on the edge of the bed and looking down at her hands. They are curled into fists. Simon watches her, his breath held, but she doesn't look at him.
She finally raises her head, looking up at the ceiling, then at him. "You're staring," she says.
He nods. "I'm sorry."
"Are you?" she asks, her voice softer now.

Esther: "Don't ask me that again. You'll regret it."
Simon: "I won't. But if I did, would you tell me the truth?"
Esther: "I've never lied to you."
Simon: "Except this one time."
Esther: "This was a lie."

Simon: "And what are you doing here? What are you doing now?"
Esther: "You want an explanation?"
Simon: "I'm not ready to accept a blank check yet."
Esther: "It's been three years. We have an established pattern of trust."
Simon: "Has it?"

Esther looks at her watch, as if counting down the seconds until she can leave. But she doesn't move. The sheet is still crumpled on the floor. The moonlight glints on her earrings.
Simon says nothing, just watches. Her face is calm now. There is a question in his eyes that she cannot answer.
She finally stands up, brushing imaginary crumbs from the front of her skirt. She turns to him. "I have to go," she says, her voice soft. Simon nods, not looking at her, and she walks out the door.
