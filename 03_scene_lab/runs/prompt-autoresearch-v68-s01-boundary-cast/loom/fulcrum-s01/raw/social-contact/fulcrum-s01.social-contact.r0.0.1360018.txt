
He had nothing on but a faded gray T-shirt with some cryptic tech-themed joke in black print: 404 NOT FOOLIN’ AROUND, it said in all caps.

Chapter Two
Welcome

“Can I take your coat?”
This was Voss, Adrian Voss, the head of Fulcrum and her tour guide through this place for the past hour. He’d been showing her around in his understated way, not too personal or obsequious, but with a disarming air of casual confidence. He’d taken her around the grounds, which stretched over some large acreage, including gardens where flowers seemed to bloom out of season; labs and classrooms where scientists were already conducting various experiments on human subjects; housing and amenities such as a small café and a library stocked with books on psychology and philosophy; even some outdoor recreational spaces where guests could play sports or enjoy nature.
And then they’d come here, to this common room which seemed to serve as the main social hub of this facility. It was cozy and intimate, decorated with tasteful art and furnishings that suggested a sense of warmth and hospitality. And there was this young man lounging on the couch, waiting for her with his dark eyes fixed on her face.
Voss took her coat off without asking and hung it up on one of the hooks by the door. He gave her a slight nod as he did so, acknowledging her presence but not intruding into it.
“Welcome,” he said gently. “Make yourself comfortable.”

Chapter Three
Contact

Mara glanced at him as she stepped forward into the room. She had no idea who he was or what he was doing here; but his presence alone was enough to make her feel slightly uneasy. She took a deep breath and tried to compose herself before seating herself in one of the chairs facing him across the table.
He didn’t say anything either; but his gaze remained locked on hers like a spotlight searching for secrets hidden deep inside her soul.
