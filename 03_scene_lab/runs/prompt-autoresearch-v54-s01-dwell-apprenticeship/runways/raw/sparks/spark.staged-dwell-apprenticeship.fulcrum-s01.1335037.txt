EXAMPLE 4

Dramatic design
Viewpoint and approach (20%): Third-person narrative focused on the protagonist
Concrete status transaction (45%): Interaction between the protagonist and a new character involving a social faux pas
Institutional turn (25%): Reaction of others to the situation, including comments and judgments
Stopping state (10%): Resolution of the immediate situation
