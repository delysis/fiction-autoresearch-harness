EXAMPLE

Dramatic design
Offer (15%): Intimate conversation and physical touch
Counteroffer and resistance (25%): Emotional distance and rejection
Complication and embodied negotiation (35%): Reluctance, emotional tension, and a kiss
Consequential turn (15%): Realization of emotional pain and detachment
Concrete stopping state (10%): Resignation to loss of connection
