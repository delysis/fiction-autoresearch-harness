Mara knew about Fulcrum's famous Northern California redwood-and-glass
compound from company videos she watched in her spare time. The
videos always began with a drone shot over the Golden Gate Bridge,
because most viewers would never enter its private grounds, but Mara
had just done exactly that. From the air it was impressive, a series of
overlapping quadrilaterals surrounded by forest; on the ground, it was
even better than she expected.
