At this time of evening the warm light from every indoor space flowed
out onto the terraces, reflecting back off the redwoods to give the
whole complex a rosy glow. She had arrived late because of the traffic,
and now the guests at the evening welcome had already begun mingling in
groups across the compound, moving between indoor rooms and outdoor
spaces like water between channels.

A young man Mara recognized as one of Fulcrum's new hires approached
the low wall by the door, set a half-full glass on the wall, and stepped
back into the room. He remained by the door, carrying on an animated
conversation with another man, one Mara didn't recognize.

A little while later, a white-haired man in a plain dark shirt walked
along the wall, picked up the half-full glass, and took it back towards
the kitchen. Neither man noticed the other's actions.

The young man seemed to be paying attention only to his companion. The
catering worker's gaze swept smoothly across the compound, from the
kitchen where he had come, to the empty glass, back into the kitchen,
and then back out toward the other guests.

Mara smiled slightly to herself as the catering worker passed by her.
She knew what she would have done if she had been in his shoes. She
could feel Fulcrum already.

The founder, Adrian, approached her from behind. "Nice to see you," he
said. "Are you enjoying the view?"

"This place is beautiful," she said. "I can't wait to get started."

"It's good to have you here," said Adrian. "Follow me. There's
something you should see."
