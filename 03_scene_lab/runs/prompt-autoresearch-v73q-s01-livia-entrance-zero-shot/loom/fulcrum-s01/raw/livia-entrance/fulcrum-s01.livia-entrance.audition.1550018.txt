
“All right.” He brought their hands back toward the table. As Mara lifted hers away he spread his fingers wide, showing her that all three objects were still in her hand and not in his. When Mara looked up to meet his eyes he raised his left hand so the backs of his fingers pressed against the bottom of his chin. “I have a read already,” he said. He placed his open palm back down on the table to support his head. “But it’s only a partial one, and not particularly confident.”
