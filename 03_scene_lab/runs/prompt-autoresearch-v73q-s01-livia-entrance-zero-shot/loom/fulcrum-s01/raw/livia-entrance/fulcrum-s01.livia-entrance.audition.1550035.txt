Her arm brushed lightly against Mara’s elbow as she stepped into the room; a cold wash of sweat ran across her skin, and then there was a soft click and an inward closing door behind her. She looked down at the door, frowned, turned around in surprise, and only then looked up again to see who was here.
Mara heard her breathe sharply as she took in the room and the people in it.
“Hello?” she said. She had a soft-voiced kind of power to it; a tone that felt like a demand for an answer. Mara noticed her looking directly at Adrian, and turned her head to follow the gaze—but he was only looking back. He seemed to know her, although he was already smiling at the thought. Mara guessed he liked her.

She wore a plain black T-shirt and simple blue jeans, nothing at all like Adrian’s clothes; the belt around her waist looked cheap. No jewelry or makeup; no visible watch. Her hair was pulled back into a ponytail behind her head; it hung loose over one shoulder now, straight and brown-black, looking too long and thick to be tied up easily. Dark eyes above a straight nose; a small mouth smiling slightly, just showing her teeth.

Adrian was watching her step into the room; his hand still lay flat against the table in front of Mara.
