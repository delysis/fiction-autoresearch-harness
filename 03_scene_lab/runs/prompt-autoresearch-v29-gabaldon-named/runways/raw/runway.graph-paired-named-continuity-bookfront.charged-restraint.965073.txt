It was two a.m. as they walked down the steps into the humid, black velvet of a coastal California summer night, and Jonah said quietly, “Mara?”

She drew a deep breath, head bowed against the night’s heat, but didn’t look at him. He reached down, touched her arm lightly with two fingertips.

“Mara. Do you want to talk about what happened?”

“Not now.”

“Okay. That’s okay, Mara. Look … maybe you want to go on, I mean, walk a little longer than just to your apartment, okay? Just you and me.” He kept the offer short, unassuring, the better not to provoke her. “Maybe we can sit down somewhere, talk. Maybe you can show me a little bit of where you like to go in the city, tell me about yourself?”

She stopped walking then. “You really want to know?”
