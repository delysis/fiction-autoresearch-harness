“Aha,” I said with false cheerfulness. “We have come to the crossroads. You take the left path to go home. I take the right.”

That was as far as I wanted to go with him. The air had been chilly and damp, but as soon as my coat went back on, my body heated up in protest against being so near.

I swallowed hard, trying to push the taste of the other man out of my mouth.

I’d been at Fulcrum long enough to be familiar with the lab sessions, but tonight’s had gone badly awry, and the rest of the research fellows, standing and glaring at me from across the room, had looked like a pack of hounds on point.

There’d been only four people in the experiment. In a small-scale trial of this sort, even one person who didn’t cooperate could bring the whole thing down. It seemed that I was that person.
