The reception room was long, shallow, and full of people. Mara, twenty-two, infrastructure-security engineer, new hire, and first-time guest, stood a little to one side to take it in: redwood beams and glass walls; a dozen tables covered in white linen, each with a single white rose on a low, clear stem of ice; a dozen catering workers in black jackets moving among the guests with trays of canapés. One, middle-aged, graying, carried a tray and watched a young man walk past in a smart blue suit and a tie printed with little pink question marks. The young man glanced down, his eyes on someone in his line of vision, saw the tray, took a canapé, and smiled.

"Hi!" the young man said, and continued his conversation with an older woman
