A thousand miles from home and already an hour late, Mara pulled into Fulcrum’s parking lot and left her rental car with only one remaining thought: This is it. This was her big shot, the reason she had put in extra hours for eighteen months, the opportunity she had waited three years to create. At age twenty-two, she had spent her undergraduate years as a systems administrator for a start-up software company in Oregon. Now the time had come to move into infrastructure security as a consultant working for big customers. The most important of them all, with hundreds of employees and thousands of servers, was Fulcrum. They had flown Mara in for the weekend to see if she fit, and she was determined to make it happen.
