Excerpt from _The Circle_ by Dave Eggers

Mara arrived at the Cir-cle at about eight o’clock on Thursday evening, and had just entered through the
front doors when she heard her name being called. The lobby, which was several stories high, was
lit dimly with soft hanging lanterns that illuminated the glass walls and ceiling. The building
itself, which rose twenty-one stories and spanned four acres, was, she understood, a monument
to sustainable technology and architecture. Made of steel and redwood beams, the walls were almost
completely glass, so that it was always sunny inside, unless the sun had set, in which case
it was a little cooler, and always darker outside, so that guests and workers could see and be
seen at all hours, in the open offices.
