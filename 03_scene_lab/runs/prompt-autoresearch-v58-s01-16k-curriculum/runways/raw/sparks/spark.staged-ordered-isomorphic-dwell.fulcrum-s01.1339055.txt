In a redwood-and-glass space filled with people whose clothes cost more than a month in rent, Mara didn’t know where to look or what to assume. She’d packed the fanciest outfit she owned (an $1850 off-the-rack suit that would fit an hour if she never breathed) into the overhead bin on her flight from Minneapolis, but the casual chic of other guests had her regretting the jacket she’d left behind. A group of three men near the bar wore what looked like khakis and golf shirts until you got close enough to see the fabric textures and realize that each man had spent $500 on what looked like a Polo shirt from a discount catalog.
