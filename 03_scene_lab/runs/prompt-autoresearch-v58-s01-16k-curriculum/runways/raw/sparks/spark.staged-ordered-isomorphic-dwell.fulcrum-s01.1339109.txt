Dramatic design
Offer (15%): The narrator, a woman who had previously been an enthusiastic climber, has developed a profound fear of heights that disrupts her sense of identity and agency.
Counteroffer and resistance (25%): Initially, the narrator resists her fear, clinging to memories of her past prowess on cliffs and trying to rationalize her current struggles as temporary and trivial.
Complication and embodied negotiation (35%): The narrator's inner voice engages in a negotiation with her fears and insecurities, attempting to dismiss and trivialize her fear of heights while also grappling with the physical and emotional consequences of her anxiety.
