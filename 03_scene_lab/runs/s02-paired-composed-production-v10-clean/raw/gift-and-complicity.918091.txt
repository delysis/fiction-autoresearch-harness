Mara kept her hands on the cup.

“Eight hours of access,” Livia said. “This isn’t a fellowship assignment. It’s an invitation. I’ll take your failure as consent.”

Mara looked at Jonah. He met her gaze without taking it away from the screens.

His hair had fallen over his eyes, darkening the space around the monitor. The hands over the keyboard rested at a slight angle, palms facing in. Mara did not know whether his fingers had brushed the keys just then or were about to. He held her gaze without changing it.

He was waiting.

Mara looked back at the tea.

She had brought it in a white box with orange twine, then eaten half of one.

That had been an offering.

This was an invitation.

She had stayed.

She was staying.

Livia said something. Mara did not hear it.

Mara sat down at the interface with the ceramic cup beside her.

She turned to Jonah. “Run the next sequence.”

He looked at her and smiled. His eyes did not leave hers.

“I’m running it,” he said. “Are you joining?”

Mara let one hand rest on the sensor puck.

She had stayed.

Livia said, “Let me know when you’re ready.”

Mara watched Jonah open the file.
