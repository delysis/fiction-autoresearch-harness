Livia turned back to Mara. “We only give it to people we trust not to fuck with the data.”

“You trust me?”

Livia nodded. “Adrian made a mistake. We’re not correcting it tonight, but it’s yours overnight. That’s how it’s done.”

Mara let her hands go empty. “And in the morning?”

“Then we’re finished.”

“Then you’re finished.” Mara picked up the cup again.

“I know. That’s the point.”

Mara took another sip. The tea tasted less like tea and more like the other people’s attention waiting for her to drink it. She set the cup down firmly, without setting her mouth again.

“You’re new,” Livia said.

Mara nodded.

“That’s a good thing,” Livia said.

Mara kept nodding.

Livia put one hand on the shared interface, just above the ACCEPT button. Her nails were short and clean, her fingertips calloused. The offer remained visible behind her hand.

Mara waited.

Jonah stopped watching Mara and began typing again.

The permission window still said PENDING. Mara looked at Livia.

The data would keep running overnight, raw and unsupervised. Mara would still have to work tomorrow, and the night after that. She might do better. She might fail.

Livia tapped ACCEPT three times in a row.

Mara’s pulse picked up again, and this time she heard it. The lab door was still closed. The tea remained visible between them. The session had run long because someone had wanted it to.

“You’re new,” Livia said. “That’s a good thing.”

Jonah kept typing.

Mara put the empty cup back.

“What’s the exchange rate?” she asked.

Livia’s smile was the same shape and entirely new. “Whatever you think it’s worth.”

Mara took a breath.

The lab door did not open. Jonah did not look up.

Mara turned to the shared interface and put one finger on ACCEPT.

“This is how it’s done,” Livia said. “You’re doing it right.”

Mara pressed her fingertip into the screen.

The permission window changed to COMPLETE. The tea remained where she had left it. Her breath stayed ordinary.

She touched ACCEPT again.

The screen confirmed she had already accepted. Mara’s fingertip felt warm.

She took one more breath.

Livia watched without moving. The tea waited. The door stayed closed.

Mara touched ACCEPT again.

The screen held its shape.

Mara tried ACCEPT once more.

The screen said nothing about it.

Mara picked up the empty cup and put it down firmly.

Livia smiled.
