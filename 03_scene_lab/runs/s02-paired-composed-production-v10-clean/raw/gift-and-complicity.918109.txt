Livia’s gaze returned to Mara. “But you won’t need a month of training. You have overnight access to the raw data stream.”

The ceramic cup felt lighter in her hands. The room grew narrower.

“Don’t thank me,” Livia said.

“It’s not—”

“You could say no. You don’t.”

She would not have said no. She had hoped Livia would do this. Mara had already been working to prove herself capable when Livia offered the proof. She had expected gratitude. Livia did not care whether Mara felt grateful. Livia had used that expectation.

Mara had waited for Livia to offer the test. And here it was: data she knew how to read, access that no new fellow received, and Livia giving it only so far as her permission lasted. The night belonged to Fulcrum. The morning belonged to Mara.

Jonah’s hands had not moved. His face held only concentration, but Mara knew the feeling in her chest. It was not dread. It was worse than that. She looked at the permissions window. The raw data stream. Her name. Livia’s open smile.

“It’s not a reward,” she said. “It’s the test.”

The room stayed still long enough for her pulse to quicken.

Jonah said, “Next sequence is ready,” and turned to the interface, his fingers falling into position.
