The red traces flattened and turned black. “Accept.”

Mara’s mouth opened again.

Livia put a bare foot on Jonah’s chair and slid it a degree nearer Mara.

“You think you have to earn this,” she said. “You think you have to make yourself exceptional until I can’t take it from you. You’re right, and the door is open.”

Mara kept smiling. She felt her pulse pick up again.

“Accept,” Livia repeated.

She did not take Mara’s hand, though Mara felt the weight of the empty air where it would have been.

The cup did not shatter. Mara held it steady with the back of her right hand against her knee and waited until her breathing resumed its normal rhythm.

“Not tonight,” Mara said.

She made her voice neutral and her eyes direct. Her pulse continued its uneven pattern.

Livia’s face did not change.

Jonah returned to the screen without looking at Mara.

Mara watched Livia for one beat past the first uncomfortable silence and returned to her own notes.
