

Mara could not think of a good reason to stop; she did not see that it could hurt anyone.
But she still had no clear idea what they might be looking for.
“Do you want me to put one out of my sight and out of yours?” she said. “Which one?”
The smile faded from his face. “That depends on which one you chose. You’re the one who decides. I don’t get to ask you which one it is, and I don’t get to tell you which one to hide; that’s all you.”
