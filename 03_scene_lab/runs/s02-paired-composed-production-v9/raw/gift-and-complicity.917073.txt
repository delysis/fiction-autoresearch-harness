The heat was steady and safe. She could leave when she had finished.

Livia picked up the carafe again. “You’ve earned my attention. But you have to accept it.”

Mara sipped and waited for Livia to explain.

“I want you to have this,” Livia said.

She tapped one key on her laptop and waited until the screen turned black. Then she pushed it over the table to Mara.

Mara looked between the screen, the keyboard, and Livia. “You’re giving me your computer?”

“The access, not the hardware.”

Livia’s words were polite. Her expression said she had been ready to give Mara the hardware if that was what it had taken to make Mara move.

Mara touched the lid, slid her hand over it, and took it into her lap. “You’re not going to ask me to do anything with it, are you?”

Livia’s gaze met Mara’s before going elsewhere. “Not this evening.”

Mara ran the lid over again, fingers resting in the groove. It was Livia’s. It carried Livia’s permissions. There were keys in this box she had never seen.

“Why are you giving me this?”

“Because you want it.”

Mara’s pulse jumped.

Livia smiled, sharp and clean. “You can leave it closed until morning if that helps you sleep.”

She was offering the key and the gate, but not the reason to enter it. Mara looked at the black screen. She looked at Jonah, who had not taken his hands from the keyboard. His fingers were still, but the left one moved with enough deliberateness to look like a signal.

“You could just log me in,” Mara said.

“Could I?”

Livia’s gaze shifted. Her voice was teasing. But her attention moved between Mara, Jonah, and the door as though the game mattered too much for her to care whether it showed.

Jonah cleared his throat. “Next sequence is ready.”

Mara looked between Livia and the laptop. “I should go.”

“Go,” Jonah agreed.

The lab door opened. Mara reached for her bag and stood.

“I’ll come in the morning,” she told Livia.

“That would please me.”

Jonah did not stop working until Mara passed the threshold. She heard the click of the lock and did not look back.

She went to the fellows’ house and slept badly, woke at four, and returned to the pavilion to open the laptop and find Livia’s permissions intact.

She stayed at the table, waiting for someone else to come in.
