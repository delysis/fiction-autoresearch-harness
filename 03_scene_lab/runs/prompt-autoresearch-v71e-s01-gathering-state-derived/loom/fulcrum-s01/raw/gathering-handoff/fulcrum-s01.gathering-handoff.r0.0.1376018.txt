

She could tell from his eyes that he was still smiling, though his lips were closed. She dropped her hand but didn’t let herself look away, waiting for his next move.

“Sit down.” He gestured back at the couch with one of his empty hands, and then seemed to realize he had forgotten to introduce himself.

He put both hands together as if he were trying to do it again from scratch.

“I’m Jonah Reed,” he said. “Hardware security and firmware. I came in on Saturday for my residency.”
“I’m Mara Vale, infrastructure security,” she said. “Nice to meet you.”
He grinned again, a little self-deprecatingly this time. “Well, that’s a start.”

“Can I help?” she asked.

“You’re a guest,” he said. “Why don’t you take a seat? Let me get us some drinks.”
And he turned to the bar and poured himself a beer without waiting for her reply.

Mara went over to the couch, sank onto it, and drew her feet up beside her, careful not to bump her knees into the small table in front of her. He came back to the couch with two glasses.
“Want another?” he asked as she took her glass of water.

“No thank you,” she said. “That’s perfect.” She raised her glass and met his eyes over it. “To finding out what we’re doing here,” she said.
He raised his beer. “Hear hear.”
They drank.
He sat down beside her, close enough that they could have reached out to touch each other but far enough that they weren’t touching.

“So,” he said, leaning toward her slightly as he placed his glass back on the low table between them. “Infrastructure security?”

“Mm-hmm.” She nodded and took a sip of her own drink
