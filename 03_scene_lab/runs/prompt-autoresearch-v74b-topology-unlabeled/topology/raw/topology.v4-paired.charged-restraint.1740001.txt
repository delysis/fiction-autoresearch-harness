

A gentle breeze tugged at her hair, the scent of night mixing with distant city smells. He pulled her closer, their bodies aligning without words. Mara tilted her head, the tension between them thickening into something palpable. His lips found hers, soft and certain. She closed her eyes, letting go of the questions, the doubts, the exhaustion.

For a breath, it was just the two of them—skin on skin, heartbeat echoing in time. But then she pulled away, lips slightly parted, catching her breath. Her eyes met his, unguarded. He smiled, not the knowing smile of earlier, but one open and genuine. She stepped back, just enough to measure him. He took it in stride, acknowledging both desire and restraint.

"Thank you," she whispered.

His voice was soft, almost amused. "I'll be here when you're ready."

He wasn't forcing her hand. Instead, he gave her a choice—an anchor in this unpredictable journey. As he turned to walk away, leaving the lab's oppressive atmosphere behind, she watched him go. For now, the stopping was as important as the kiss itself. It meant something. Something real.
