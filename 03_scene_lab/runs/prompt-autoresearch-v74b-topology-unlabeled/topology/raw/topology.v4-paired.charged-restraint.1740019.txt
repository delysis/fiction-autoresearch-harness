

"Do you have any plans for dinner?" he asked. His tone was measured, carrying the weight of unsaid things.

She shook her head. "Nothing at all," she replied, voice rough from holding back too many emotions.

He nodded, a subtle acknowledgment, and shifted slightly so his thumb could stroke the back of her hand. The gesture was intimate yet restrained, a steadying force amid the tension in the air.

She didn't pull away but allowed her hand to soften beneath his touch. Her pulse quickened, the ache of desire unfurling like a long-submerged current. He studied her face, eyes catching on the flush beneath her skin. Without a word, he leaned closer, as if daring her to resist.

The press of his lips against hers was firm yet tentative, a slow claim that left no room for resistance. She responded, her hand sliding into his hair, pulling him deeper. Their kiss deepened, breath mingling, and the taste of him set her nerves aflame. Each touch felt like a release, every caress a deliberate unraveling.

But just as the air between them charged with more heat than they could hold, Mara pulled back. Not abruptly, not with hostility, but with a clarity that left him standing close. His breath mingled with hers, soft and steady, their foreheads resting together for a moment before he leaned back.

She looked up at him, meeting his gaze with a quiet vulnerability she had spent so long denying. The ache lingered, potent and raw, but it was different—alive, unchained by fear or obligation.

"We can't," she said, her voice barely a whisper. But the words were firm, the decision clear.

He nodded, a slow acknowledgment, his eyes tracing her expression. His hand lingered on hers for a moment longer before he stepped back, giving her room to breathe. The city hummed around them, indifferent to the intimacy that had just played out on this quiet street corner.

"Not now, maybe," he replied, his tone carrying no disappointment—only the weight of unfulfilled desire and the promise of a future possibility.
