
Jonah looked down at their locked hands, and then back at Mara, and then he pulled them apart—but just a little—and turned his free hand so he could link fingers with her again in an arm-in-arm gesture.
He’d put his arms around her waist, she realized after a moment, and she leaned forward to meet him. It felt like the first time she’d ever touched another person that way—and it didn’t seem to be going too badly, though she couldn’t really tell if she was doing it right or not.

After a while he pulled back enough to let go of her, then took another step toward her.

And then there was a woman behind him, smiling at both of them. Mara turned her head quickly to meet her gaze; she felt an instinctive aversion to looking at people when they were too close. She was pretty—younger than Mara, probably around eighteen or nineteen, with straight brown hair and large brown eyes beneath thick eyelashes. Her smile widened as she stepped past Jonah to reach out for Mara’s hand; her fingers were soft and warm against Mara’s palm.
Mara smiled back at the stranger with a small nod of greeting, trying not to make it seem like a question
