
Mara wanted to ask him, so she did. “Do you mind if I sit?”
“Oh. Sure.” Jonah let go of her hand and backed up toward the couch.

Mara watched him sit down as she walked over to take the chair on the opposite side. The room was simple enough, but not dull. She liked the way the light fell on the smooth fabric of the wall padding and the sleek lines of the white furniture, the way the table had only a few thin legs that bent slightly outward from a square of metal and glass.
She’d felt at home as soon as she entered Fulcrum. And it didn’t feel like an illusion; she wasn’t just trying to prove anything here. It seemed real, and for once that didn’t scare her away.
She sat down, leaned back, and crossed one leg over the other, looking at this Jonah through him at all of them, curious how he would respond.

Jonah's attention had slid past hers in an instant, over to the door across from where she sat, a door which was closed for the moment.
She watched him watch the door and then said, "He won't be here for a while."
Jonah nodded and leaned forward, elbows resting on his knees.
“He’s good at making you feel welcome, isn’t he?”
“He’s good at making it look that way.” She shifted in her chair to face him a bit more directly, still keeping her legs crossed under the table. “Is that what you do? Social engineering?”
He shook his head. “Hardware. But I help out with the interviews when I can.”
