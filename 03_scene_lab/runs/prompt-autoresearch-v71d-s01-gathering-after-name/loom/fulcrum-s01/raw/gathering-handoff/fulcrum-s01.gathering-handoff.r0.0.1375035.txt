“I’m sorry if I’m interrupting,” she said, “I didn’t realize there would be others here this early.”
“No, no! You’re not—you didn’t,” Jonah said hastily. He was looking at her, smiling apologetically. “You’re not interrupting, I wasn’t busy. I mean, I was sitting, but I’m not actually on the job right now. I’m just here as a resident. And it’s nice to meet you—you’re the latest hire.”
