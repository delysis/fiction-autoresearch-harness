The car let Mara out on a cul-de-sac off the freeway with one suitcase
just before sunset. The ride-hailing app had routed the car here; the
last half mile was an unpaved road past the guard station, and a
graveled path up a small slope through ferns and oak trees and redwood
stumps to the compound. The compound turned out to be a few glass and
wood buildings arranged in an L-shaped courtyard around the former main
house, which had become a cafeteria. At the foot of the path an
attractive young man waited on the gravel. He was watching a text
conversation with another attractive young man.

The young man waiting said "Hello" in greeting, then "Jonah!" in
recognition as he lifted one arm to wave. The other young man looked up
from his phone with a smile and a "Hey" for Mara. They both seemed more
interested in each other than in her, which might have been an insult,
except that she suspected their conversations were often interrupted by
new arrivals; it was only natural that they would resume as soon as
they were out of the way.
