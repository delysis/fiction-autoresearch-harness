The rideshare deposited her just inside Fulcrum's gate, which was set in
a high chain-link fence covered with a variety of vines that looked like
jasmine. She had two bags, one a large suitcase, the other an overnight
case. She sat on the curb until she finished putting on a clean pair of
pantyhose, and then called the house phone. A young woman with a voice
like an angel answered it and told her someone would pick her up.

Mara walked into Fulcrum's inner courtyard and saw a half dozen people
sitting at tables under a trellis covered in green leaves and bright
red flowers. A fountain bubbled quietly. Someone was playing classical
guitar. She stopped by the fountain, which was about twice as tall as
her head, made of white marble and shaped like a shell on an elegant
pedestal. The water seemed to come out of a conch held in a mermaid's
hand.

A short, muscular, bearded young man with a shaved head ran up behind her and
greeted her with a warm, masculine hug. She had a flash of him taking her
over the arm of a chair and shoving her down into a rough bed.
