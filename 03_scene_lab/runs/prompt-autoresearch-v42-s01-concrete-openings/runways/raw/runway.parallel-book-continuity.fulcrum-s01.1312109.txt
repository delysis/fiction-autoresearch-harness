The ride share dropped me in front of a large redwood and glass building. The sky was light gray with clouds and mist from the ocean just a few miles west. The evening air had a cool bite as I stepped out, making my skin tingle. I paid the driver then waited for him to pull off, leaving me alone in the deserted street. I looked around me. It was hard to believe there were over twenty million people living in the San Francisco Bay Area just a few miles away. The building was very large and sat on a wooded hillside above a small river. There were other buildings scattered around the property, all connected by wooden paths snaking through the lush landscape.

I had been asked to arrive between five and seven pm, so I looked at my phone and saw that it was just after five thirty. A man in a T-shirt, shorts and flip-flops stepped out of one of the glass doors. He was young, maybe early twenties, with long blonde hair tied in a ponytail, a goatee, and large round glasses. He looked a lot like the stereotypical Silicon Valley engineer. He looked me up and down with an appraising stare before his smile disappeared.

“Can I help you?” he asked, holding out a hand.
