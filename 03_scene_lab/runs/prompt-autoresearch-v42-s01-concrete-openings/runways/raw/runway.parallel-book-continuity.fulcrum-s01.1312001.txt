The rideshare dropped me off in the early evening with one suitcase, two backpacks, and a purse at a redwood-and-glass building that looked more like a resort than the tech company that it was. I had just been offered the job of head of neuroethics. The place looked good but I was exhausted; I had spent the day packing my previous life into a couple boxes and a shipping carton and I didn't want to unpack anything.

I checked in, then was told to drop my stuff off at my cabin--a long walk over to a cabin that looked more like a motel or a condo than what I would have expected a "cabin" to look like. There was no key in the lockbox where I was directed to find it and no one to whom to complain. I ended up sitting on the wooden deck staring into the forest, not wanting to talk to anyone but also not wanting to just sit there. The only food I could find were a couple of bowls of cherries set out on a table near the front door.
