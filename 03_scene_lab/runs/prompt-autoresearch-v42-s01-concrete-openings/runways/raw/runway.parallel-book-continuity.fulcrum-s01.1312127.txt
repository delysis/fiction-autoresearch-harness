Chapter Two
The Half-Empty Glass

"Mara."

Mara turned away from the view and saw a woman walking toward her on the cement path, carrying a glass of iced tea.

"I'm Adrian."

She stopped ten feet away. Mara took off her sunglasses so she could see properly.

Adrian looked younger and older at the same time, as if she'd been both a teenager and a grandmother. Her skin was translucent, her eyes dark and intelligent. Her gray-and-white hair, long enough to fall over her shoulders, had no visible strands. She seemed taller than her actual five feet five.

"Welcome. Please come inside."

Mara's glance followed the woman's hand, which indicated a glass wall and sliding door.

"You can leave your things out here."

Mara glanced toward her suitcase and jacket. "Is that okay?"

Adrian was already opening the door. "Sure." She didn't seem to expect Mara to ask.

Inside, a few feet beyond the glass, was another wall.

"Mara, this is our public living room. People come and go here. Feel free to explore. Make yourself at home."
