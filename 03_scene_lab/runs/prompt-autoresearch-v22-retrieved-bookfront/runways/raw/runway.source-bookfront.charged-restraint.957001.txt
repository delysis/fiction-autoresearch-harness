When the lab door closed behind them, Mara and Jonah found themselves in
a different world. The cold and the wind had kept the daylight out. The
early-evening city was dark, almost empty, and silent except for a few
cars and buses. Even they would not stay long.

The walk to the bus stop took five minutes, maybe ten if they stayed in
the shelter of the alley and took the long way round. Mara wore boots,
a coat, scarf, and gloves, but the biting wind still chilled her. Jonah
wore the same as Mara except that his hat covered all but his eyes and
his coat covered most of his face. He was cold, but not uncomfortable
as the wind chilled and invigorated his skin and warmed his breath.

They turned right out of the alley, and the wind blew into their faces
for a change. That made Jonah laugh. He did not know why but Mara had.

"How do you know when you laugh?"

"Excuse me? I do not understand the question."

"How do you know that I'm laughing?"

"How do I know that you are laughing?"

"Yes, that is the question."

She answered, "Because you said that I made you laugh."

"Did I? That's true. I said that. I said that I thought that you had
made me laugh."
