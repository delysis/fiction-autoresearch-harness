The permission line remained on the shared interface: MARA VALE / PENDING. The ACCEPT control waited beneath it. Jonah’s next sequence remained ready; Mara still held the cooling tea.

She could have refused. It would have taken one small act: rising to pack, saying she could not review overnight access, asking to see the results in the morning. But refusal could be read as lack of commitment. As mistrust of the system. As a test of her own. The room would understand it. Livia already had. She was waiting for Mara’s choice.

Mara tapped ACCEPT before she could make a reason.

Livia turned back to the primary monitor without celebrating. Jonah returned to the data traces. The room resumed its ordinary motion around Mara. She folded her hands in her lap and held them still.

The lab door remained closed. Her bag waited beside her chair. The session had already run twenty-three minutes past its promised end. The permission line on the interface would disappear at six. Mara’s tea was cooling.
