"What! Three days after you've entreated her on your knees to hasten

"She's refused; that gives me the right--"

"Ah, you've taught me what an ugly word that is," she said.

He turned away with a sense of utter weariness. He felt as though he
had been struggling for hours up the face of a steep precipice, and
now, just as he had fought his way to the top, his hold had given way
and he was pitching down headlong into darkness.

If he could have got her in his arms again he might have swept away
her arguments; but she still held him at a distance by something
inscrutably aloof in her look and attitude, and by his own awed sense
of her sincerity. At length he began to plead again.

"If we do this now it will be worse afterward--worse for every one--"

"No--no--no!" she almost screamed, as if he frightened her.

At that moment the bell sent a long tinkle through the house. They had
heard no carriage stopping at the door, and they stood motionless,
looking at each other with startled eyes.

FULCRUM

A novel

Setting
Contemporary Fulcrum, a private psychotechnology institute studying attention and nonverbal cue-reading. Adult newcomer Mara is the only viewpoint. Livia's accuracy retains ordinary cue-reading explanations; Jonah's restraint costs him status and changes Mara's attention.

Principal characters
Mara Vale, twenty-two, an infrastructure-security engineer: sheltered, technically formidable, observant, and dryly funny. She wants difficult truth and fellowship, but resents any claim to know her without permission.
Adrian Voss, Fulcrum's polished founder: he makes status, method, and recruitment feel like hospitality.
Livia Sloane, Fulcrum's gifted reader of posture, voice, and timing: her care is real, her skill is real, and she wants ownership of the pace.
Jonah Reed, a hardware-security researcher: playful and socially fluent; he notices when a person becomes material for an audience and sometimes pays a public cost rather than perform her.

Continuity
Adult newcomer Mara is at the contemporary Northern California Fulcrum compound for the first time; she has never visited or interviewed here before. The unnamed blond man presently on the couch is adult Jonah Reed, though Mara has not learned his name. Adrian has not entered. Mara is the only viewpoint. Unusual cue-reading always retains ordinary sensory and behavioral explanations.

Present movement payment
Mara witnesses a concrete status asymmetry; Adrian turns the welcome into a nonverbal calibration game.

Dramatic design for the next prose movement
Continue from the present welcome without replaying Mara's arrival. The unnamed blond man is Jonah; Mara may learn his name through the exchange. Render one first dyadic contact through concrete dialogue, posture, and an imperfect inference. The exchange changes what Mara attends to without resolving whom she can trust. Adrian remains elsewhere and is neither seen nor mentioned.
