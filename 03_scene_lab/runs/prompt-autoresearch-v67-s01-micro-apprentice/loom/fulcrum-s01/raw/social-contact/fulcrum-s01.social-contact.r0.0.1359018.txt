
It was then she noticed how his whole frame took on a slightly different shape: it seemed like a very slight shift. She thought perhaps she had been wrong, that he wasn’t younger after all.
Mara swallowed and forced herself to step fully into the space. The room’s floor-to-ceiling windows offered a panoramic view of the hills beyond Fulcrum’s campus—the late afternoon sun had turned the sky into a kaleidoscope of purple and orange. But she couldn’t tear her eyes away from him. He didn’t move again as she crossed the distance between them, but he made no effort to hide his gaze either.
“Good evening,” she said at last, the words feeling stiff and awkward on her lips. “I’m Mara.”
The stranger took a second longer than she expected before answering. When he finally spoke, his voice was deep and resonant. “Good evening yourself,” he said with no sign of surprise or discomfort—but still no expression either. “Call me Jonah.”
“Welcome.”
He inclined his head toward one of the empty couches—a gesture so subtle that someone with less attention might miss it entirely—then turned away, leaving her standing there alone again.
As she moved to take the spot next to him, another figure appeared in the doorway, silhouette darker against evening light. Adrian approached with confident strides; his height seemed even more imposing from this angle than she remembered—Mara felt a brief twinge of self-consciousness under his penetrating stare but forced herself not to shift her gaze away as he came closer.
“Mara,” said Adrian, nodding at her—and then adding another slight nod in Jonah’s direction. “Join me here for dinner?”
She didn’t answer immediately; instead she followed the trajectory of his hand as it indicated a chair on the far side of the communal table from where they were now. The space between them seemed to shift—and something told her it was Adrian who had done so without even realizing what he’d done.
And yet… there was something else going on too. A quiet hum under the surface currents; some unspoken understanding that was already taking shape within this new context they were all being placed into together.
The man who called himself Jonah hadn’t spoken another word since introducing himself to her earlier—and yet his presence still felt palpable somehow around both men—a silent witness whose role would soon become clearer when everyone else joined them here too…
