
“Jonah?” she said aloud. She didn’t know why she had spoken, but he stopped at once and waited.
She followed him down the steps and then passed him without thinking, stepping out into the dark. He turned to follow, his face blank, his eyes barely visible in the shadows of the trees. “Would you walk with me, please?”
He said nothing. She waited, holding her breath. After a moment he started walking again, directly behind her, so that she felt the heat from his body against her back and between her shoulders.
She wasn’t sure whether it would feel better to be closer or further away from him. Her throat felt tight, but she hadn’t cried yet. She didn’t think she was going to.
His shirt moved up and down steadily between her shoulder blades. He walked very slowly so that he didn’t bump into her when she stopped in the middle of the street.
She leaned back into his chest without turning around. He smelled like coffee and wet wool, and the fabric of his shirt was warm and soft.
