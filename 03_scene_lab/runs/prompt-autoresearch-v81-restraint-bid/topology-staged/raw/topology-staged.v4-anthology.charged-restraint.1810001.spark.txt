
“Jonah?” she said aloud. She didn’t know why she had spoken, but he stopped at once and waited.
She followed him down the steps and then down the short hill. They walked side by side. Her mind was still stuck on the Late Session, and she thought of nothing else. The others in their group had found each other attractive or repulsive. The observers had analyzed the body-language and verbal cues. Jonah had been silent: he always was, when she needed him.
She stopped thinking about her nightclothes or the walk, and watched him walk without really looking. His legs swung loose at the knees; his feet landed with a quiet bounce. His arms were relaxed too, swinging slightly and easily back and forth beside him. It took some effort for her to remember to move one foot after the other, and even more to force her arms to swing. She tried not to think about what she was doing, but she kept falling behind. The sidewalk narrowed, and he moved a little to the right to let her walk closer beside him.
