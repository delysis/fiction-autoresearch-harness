 He paused at the bottom step and looked back; his eyes were dark but his face wasn’t cold or angry or contemptuous. His gaze passed over her but he didn’t see her.
“Mara?”
His mouth opened and closed, but no sound came out.
“Do you—do you need a ride?”
“No.” She could feel her jaw moving. “Thanks.” Her feet came down too hard on the uneven bricks, and her shoulders hunched reflexively as she walked. She didn’t like the feel of the damp grass or the bumpy sidewalks under her heels. She didn’t know where Jonah was going; she didn’t need to know. There was a hotel about four blocks away, she had a map to it, she could walk there on her own. She didn’t need anyone’s help tonight or any other time.
