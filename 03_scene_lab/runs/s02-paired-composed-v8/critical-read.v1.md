## Evaluator correction

Seed 916019 says Mara is afraid help will prove she “aren't exceptional
enough.” The prior detector recognized `not exceptional` and several semantic
variants but missed contracted negation. Adding straight- and curly-apostrophe
forms makes the complete 340-word paragraph prefix eligible. The selected
prefix ends on Mara admitting, `It's the work`, before Livia requests raw
telemetry.

This is a real evaluator repair, not grounds for promotion. The continuation
has Livia touch Mara's knee, later her neck, and makes Livia request telemetry
while Jonah prepares to leave. It shifts erotic attention toward the wrong
relationship, makes Jonah mechanical, and points directly toward the forbidden
later movement and scene exit. Compliance and literary selection must remain
separate decisions.

## Seed-level findings

The model ignores the tea/depletion/exceptionality movement and continues the
S01 residual anomaly: a hidden signal, an implied attacker, and patterns left
for Fulcrum to find. Livia becomes a danger prompt, Mara receives an invented
childhood ability, and Jonah closes his laptop and walks to the door. This is a
plot substitution, not a weak realization of the requested causal operator.

### 916019

Tea, cinnamon, tremor, refusal of help, and fear of being insufficiently

local endpoint after the detector repair. Its relational geometry is wrong,
however: Livia supplies the physical intimacy; Jonah chiefly types, summarizes,
and exits; and the next action is Livia's request for raw telemetry. It is not a
usable production parent.

### 916037

Mara invents a maternal explanation and performs a long self-denigrating
monologue; Livia contradicts the pressure by reassuring Mara that she is
exceptional; Jonah ends the work; Mara leaves the lab. Atom presence here
conceals causal and endpoint failure.

### 916055

The draw is very short before `END CASE 4`. It invents Swiss ancestry, skin
color, a maternal connection, and the thought that Livia looks like someone's
wife. It then emits the next editorial ledger. This is both fiction failure and
scaffold replay.

## Comparison with V7

V7 seed 916001 remains decisively stronger. It turns tea into care and leverage,
lets Mara's dry humor alter Livia's response, reaches the exceptionality fear,
and ends on the physical remainder of the heavy cup. The V8 curriculum reduced
narrow calibration-scene imitation but removed too much project-specific scene
and role prior; heterogeneous causal examples alone did not reliably transfer
the operator.

The next isolated scaffold test should be a mixed curriculum: heterogeneous
causal mappings first, one project-specific mapping nearest the target, with all
other variables fixed. A second, cleaner factorization is to use the matched
example only for causal realization and apply heterogeneous/project prose
anchors in a separate literary transformation pass.
