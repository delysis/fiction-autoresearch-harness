In the evening when the last of the gray hills finally became redwood-and-glass buildings, Mara Vale watched the rideshare’s windshield grow warmer and less distant while she rolled the sleeve of her black sweater up past the scar on her inner forearm. She took another photograph of the view. She was glad to see this place with her own eyes but worried that the ride could stop sooner than she was ready.

The road was now lined with tall redwood trees and a white-stone wall that seemed to have been quarried from the landscape.
