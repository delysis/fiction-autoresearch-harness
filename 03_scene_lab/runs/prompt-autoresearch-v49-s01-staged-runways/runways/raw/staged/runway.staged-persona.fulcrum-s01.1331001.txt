A few yards below the road, Mara saw a single tall redwood tree without lower limbs. A small concrete terrace in front of it held two white-metal chairs. A middle-aged woman was seated on one and leaning forward with her hands resting on her thighs. She wore black slacks and a lightweight black top that fell to her knees. A man dressed in a dark blue work uniform approached the terrace from the side, holding an old-fashioned broom.

Mara could not guess what they were talking about, or whether they had just begun or were about to part. They looked like two friends, or a boss and a worker, or strangers having the first of many such conversations.

Mara did not leave the car immediately, but remained looking at the sign while she waited for the rideshare to get going again. The rideshare did not move. Mara finally told it to end the ride. It took payment through her phone app, and she got out and collected her suitcase from the back seat. She walked along the road until she found a dirt parking lot that had room for twenty cars. A few spaces were occupied by pickups, SUVs, and hybrids with California plates.

Mara followed the instructions, putting down her suitcase along the way, and entered what seemed to be an outdoor courtyard or garden. The courtyard had white stone benches around its perimeter, and a large concrete fountain in its center that spilled water over four tiers of rocks into a square stone basin. There were no people anywhere, and Mara again had the eerie feeling of having arrived at the wrong time.

She returned to her suitcase and rolled it through the courtyard and along a short paved path until she came to the double glass doors of the building's main entrance. The doors slid open as she approached, and she wheeled her suitcase inside. She found herself in an airy lobby with a high ceiling. To her left was a reception desk, and beyond that a large open area with several comfortable chairs arranged around low tables. On the wall opposite the reception desk was a long row of windows looking out onto the courtyard.
