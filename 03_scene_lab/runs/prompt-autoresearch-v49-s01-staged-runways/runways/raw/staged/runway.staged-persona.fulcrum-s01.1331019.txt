She saw no point in calling. She had a phone number in her pocket, but it was for later, if she got lost. She would go where it looked as if others were going and let them find her.

The sun had almost set, and lights inside the house glowed through its broad glass panels: a row of pendant lamps along one wall, the muted yellow of a lamp on a low table. The evening promised to be chilly and still, with no breeze to stir the trees. There was a light from above where the path curved up past the house: some sort of porch. That looked as likely as anything for a reception. Mara picked up her suitcase and began walking.

The suitcase made the smooth cement of the path seem colder against her hands. She held it with her right hand only, so she could use her left hand to slide out her phone and check her watch face. The time said 4:37 PM, with no notification icon. Mara glanced again at the trees overhead. She needed a better sense for daylight hours; the air would get much colder once it was fully dark.

The front porch was lit by sconces, and she recognized the young man who had greeted her by email as the first face among the two dozen people in black around the house. The others did not see her as she emerged from the trees into the pool of light. They seemed to be absorbed in conversation in a way she did not think would make them look up at the sound of her coming.

The young man--Jonathan Reed--turned when the suitcase scraped on the steps. He smiled warmly, took Mara's right hand between his own and raised it to his lips. Mara smiled back; she was relieved that the hand had been dry when she set the case down. She waited for the other cheek to be kissed, but he seemed to have no intention of holding both her hands at once.

She stepped back with a little laugh and reached out the left hand herself. Reed took it, kissed it in turn, then turned and held out his other hand toward the crowd behind him.

"Mara, I'm delighted you could come. This is Livia Sloane," he said, then took her arm with his free hand and turned to face Livia. "This is Mara Vale. She's one of Adrian's newest hires."
