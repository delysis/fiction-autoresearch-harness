
The sound of voices approached from beyond the door.
“I guess your friends are here now.”
The young man smiled and let go of her hand, stepping away toward the doorway. “Yeah,” he agreed, turning to wave at the group filing into the room behind him, and Mara watched as a figure emerged from the crowd to slap Jonah on the back.
Another face turned toward her, and she realized there was a man standing by her side again—a man who made Jonah look young. She wasn’t sure if that was an age difference or an authority difference, but it didn’t matter for the moment, as long as she stood in a place where everyone wanted her to stand.
Someone poured wine into her glass—the oldest man she could see here, older even than the man standing next to her—and then his arm fell away and she stood there alone, holding her glass, listening to the sound of other people’s conversation.

“I guess you need to introduce me to everyone,” she said, hoping it wasn’t true.
