 “I’m finished.”

She looked up, intending to meet his gaze, and found him instead studying her hands. A woman had appeared beside them; a woman shorter than he was, wearing a white lab coat instead of the uniform shirt. Mara glanced over at her and thought she would recognize her somewhere. “I’ll tell you what I think you’ve chosen,” said the woman in white. Her voice was deeper than she had looked, with the same vaguely foreign accent as the man’s.
Adrian turned to her and held up her hand. “Tell us. This one?”
The woman nodded, smiled, and withdrew; leaving them waiting.
He returned Mara’s attention back to hers. “This isn’t like a card trick, I should mention,” he said quietly. “If you don’t understand how we knew which object, I may not be able to explain it.”
