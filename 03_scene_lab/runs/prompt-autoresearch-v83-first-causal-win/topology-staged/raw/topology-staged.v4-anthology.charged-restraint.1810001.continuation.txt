 Her eyes went shut on their own and she felt an ache in her chest. It wasn’t a sensation of pressure but of lack. She stood breathing, trying to think and failing. “Jonah,” she whispered.
His gaze was level and unreadable.
She had been standing for an age, paralyzed by his uncomprehending presence. “Do you want to walk home with me?” She spoke quickly, suddenly, without any thought. She kept her head down as he began moving beside her without answering. There would be no answer. He did not say things aloud unless he meant them or was compelled to.
She knew that.
