
“You ready?” He didn’t turn to look at her as he spoke, only moved his shoulders away from his ears and took another step.
“Not really,” she said honestly. She had hoped for a long talk with Jonah about tonight; he was the only person there who hadn’t been participating or taking notes. She couldn’t tell him what she had seen from her end. But he could see her face as she answered. His shoulders shifted again and he waited for another second, looking at the road, but then started forward.
“Okay,” she said. She moved faster to keep up with his stride. They went through the open iron gate and onto the dark street without pausing. The streetlights made yellow circles that moved like lamps on the floor of an ocean. They turned right and headed toward the town.
