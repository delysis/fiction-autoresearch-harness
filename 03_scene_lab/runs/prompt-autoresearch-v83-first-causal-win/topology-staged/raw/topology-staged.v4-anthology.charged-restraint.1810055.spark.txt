Mara glanced into the dark garage, where she had left her rental car in the afternoon, then followed him slowly, taking care not to touch his arm, which swayed close to hers with every other step.
The parking lot was deserted except for their two cars. Jonah reached for his pocket and took out a silver key. The key ring had a small brass cross attached to it, about the size of a penny. The car was a rented Pontiac—the same dark blue model they used at Fulcrum to conduct studies—but with different license plates. Mara followed his gaze to the hood. It seemed an odd thing to notice now: The light glinted across the Pontiac’s paint job in the glow of the parking lot, but it wasn’t a shiny new car. The dark blue was faded in some spots.
