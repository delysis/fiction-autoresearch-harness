
“Hello!” said the woman in a bright voice.

The man had entered first and was holding open the doorway for the woman, who must have been his date. She’d taken up the job of greeting them both. “I’m Livia,” she announced, grinning as she strode into the room, hand outstretched for Mara to shake. She was tall and dark, with smooth skin and short black hair. Her dress, deep blue, left her shoulders bare but came down nearly to her ankles.

“Hello,” Mara replied, smiling back. Her free hand went to her jacket pocket to hold the phone in case there was anything else she needed to take out right away.
“Welcome! I’m Jonah, you must be Livia.” The blond young man shook the woman’s hand with a wide grin of his own. Mara could see his dark eyes glinting through the glasses as he looked up at Livia. She was just about the same height as Mara; if anything, her date was shorter. But the difference between their attitudes was plain enough.
And then, from the hall beyond, came another voice—a familiar voice, warm and low, calling out to them all: “Friends! Please come in!”

Livia stepped away from the doorway and turned back toward the new speaker, opening her arms wide and calling, “Adrian!”
The man who’d entered before her had already been turning toward the new arrival. Now he shifted back toward the group and Mara could see that he was older than everyone else in the room by a good twenty years.

He looked like someone who’d spent half his life making himself taller than he was, but who’d stopped caring about it now. He was quite handsome—tall enough to carry off his slim frame and short hair without looking ridiculous; very tanned and wearing well-tailored clothes.

The new speaker took Livia’s hand and kissed the back of it in a gesture of old-fashioned courtesy. Then he turned toward Mara with an expansive smile.
“Welcome,” he said warmly, giving her just enough time to shake his hand before he turned away again to greet Jonah and the other young man.

“I’m Adrian,” he continued cheerfully as he walked on past them all toward the far wall where a small sideboard table held several bottles of wine and some glasses. “Please take what you’d like.”
“Thank you,” said Livia politely.

Mara saw her offer an empty glass to Jonah, who took it gratefully and turned toward Adrian as he poured the wine. She watched him tilt back his head for a drink—just two swallows at first—and then hold it carefully in both hands as he watched his host pour another glass and put it on a tray.
“And how are you doing tonight?” Adrian asked Livia brightly as he passed her. “Having fun?”
“It’s wonderful,” she replied warmly.

Mara glanced up at him for the first time, catching a glimpse of dark-lashed eyes and an amused expression that vanished immediately, leaving the young man to turn toward his companions.
“Very good,” said Adrian, nodding at Livia’s response before he moved toward the other pair. “And you?” he asked the blond young man, taking a step away from the table to take the empty glass from Jonah’s hand as he passed.

Jonah drank again—three swallows this time—and handed back the glass without speaking. Adrian turned to pour for the next person, passing by Mara as he did so, and she could feel his eyes on her in a way that made her nervous.
There was something about him that suggested he might be looking right through you if you weren’t paying close enough attention; it was disconcerting and a little scary.

But Mara didn’t let herself think about it much. She was here now, wasn’t she? She had come this far; surely she could manage at least a few more minutes before running away?
And so she took her wine glass from the tray and drank two swallows just as Adrian passed by to take the empty one from Jonah’s hand.
