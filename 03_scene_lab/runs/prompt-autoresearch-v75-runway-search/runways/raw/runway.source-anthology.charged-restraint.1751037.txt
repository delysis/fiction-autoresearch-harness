The Fulcrum Lab

The air conditioner hummed in the corner, and a fan oscillated back and forth. Mara sat on a bench in the lab and watched Dr. Epps, the psychiatrist, who sat at an angle to her, taking notes on a pad while he listened to her describe her dream.

Jonah was seated across from her and at right angles to Epps. She looked over at him, but he had his head down, scribbling something.

Mara spoke carefully, choosing her words and trying to remember the images from her dream in detail. She had a lot of dreams that were vivid and strange, especially when she was pregnant with her first son, and then when she was breastfeeding.

She had thought, as time had gone by, and the stress of the initial stages of weaning had passed, that things might begin to get easier. But lately, every dream that she had—or almost every one—seemed to end with her breasts full of milk, and crying. She woke up feeling so full she was in agony. Her breasts were tight and painful and usually she would wake up crying along with the dream-baby, and go out in the kitchen and run water into a cup to express, or pour hot water over her breasts to stimulate them and then lean over the kitchen sink and release some milk into the water.
