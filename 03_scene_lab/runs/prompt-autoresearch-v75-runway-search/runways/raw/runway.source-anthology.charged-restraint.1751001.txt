The morning after my twentieth birthday, I am summoned to see my father, Lord High Marshall of the Fulcrum. He’s in his private chambers, surrounded by the maps and papers he uses to plan strategy sessions. He hands me a cup of hot chocolate, made from beans from the southernmost parts of the country—a reminder of his power and status. We are supposed to drink it together.

“Your mother and I are very proud of you, Cécile,” he says. “We can’t imagine what your life will be like when you are Empress.”

“Thank you.” I hand him the chocolate without a word. He has been telling me for twenty years that I’ll marry a member of the imperial family and become Empress. It’s what is expected of me and he has never had any reason to change his mind.

I don’t ask what has brought on this morning’s speech, but as I start to leave my father says, “Oh, there’s just one more thing. Your mother and I have decided you should choose a Fulcrum bodyguard.”

My bodyguards are chosen by the High Marshall and Empress. They have a special connection to Fulcrum and will go wherever I go to ensure that I am kept safe.
