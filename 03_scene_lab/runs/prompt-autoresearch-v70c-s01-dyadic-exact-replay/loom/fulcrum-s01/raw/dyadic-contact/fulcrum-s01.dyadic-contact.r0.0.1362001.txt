 His body still. She saw hazel eyes behind dark lashes, a slender nose, full lips pressed together.
It took him three seconds to sit up and speak. “Hello. You must be Mara.”
She hesitated before answering, a little out of breath herself. The man on the couch was still only half-sitting. “Yeah, that’s me. Where’s Voss?”
“He sends his apologies. There was a scheduling conflict.” His eyes met hers. “I’m Jonah Reed. Welcome to Fulcrum.”
She didn’t quite know what to do. This wasn’t the welcome she’d expected. He wasn’t wearing a watch, or an expression that she could read.
Jonah smiled briefly and rose. “He asked me to show you around instead.”
The man before her was very tall, six three or so, with wide shoulders and a narrow waist. She noted the way his eyes darted about the room—watching her watch him. It gave her a weird feeling she couldn’t shake.
“I’m Mara.” She shook his offered hand, feeling heat rise in her cheeks. “It’s nice to meet you.”
His smile widened, revealing dimples on either side of his lips. “Likewise. Come on, I’ll give you the tour.”
