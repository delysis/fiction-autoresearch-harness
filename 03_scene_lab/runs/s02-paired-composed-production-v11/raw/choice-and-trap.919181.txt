Livia had returned to the primary monitor. She did not look at Mara. She waited.

Mara placed two fingertips on the ACCEPT button. The room did not change. Mara’s hand stayed in the same position.

The ACCEPT button was a rectangle of heat.

Mara’s finger moved from it.

“Jonah—”

Livia’s chair scraped. Jonah stopped typing.

Mara watched the tea cooling in her hands. Her pulse remained quick; she knew what it would feel like when the heat left her skin. She watched her pulse until it quickened again. The tea was still warm.

The ACCEPT button was still lit.

Mara moved toward it with her right hand. The tea in her left hand held the room at bay; the button on the shared interface would give it up.

“This is not,” Mara said, “a reward.”

Jonah watched the interface. “It’s not a test.”

“It’s a trap.”

The room went quiet.

Mara placed two fingers on the button. She looked at Jonah. Livia stayed silent. The heat remained in her palm. Mara’s gaze moved from Jonah to Livia; then she pressed the button.

The interface flashed GREEN: ACCEPTED. Livia returned to the primary monitor.

Jonah looked up. “You could have said no.”

Mara did not remove her hand. “No.”

“I mean it.”

She held both the tea and the heat of the button. “So do I.”

The test had begun.
