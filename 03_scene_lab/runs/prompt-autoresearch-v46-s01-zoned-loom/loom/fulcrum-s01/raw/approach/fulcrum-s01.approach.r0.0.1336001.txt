

"I'm curious," Adrian said, placing his flute on the table. "What made
you contact Fulcrum?"

Mara took a small sip from her flute. She put the flute on the table
beside Adrian's. "My brother-in-law told me about Fulcrum."

Adrian sat quietly for a moment. He was leaning slightly toward Mara
with his hands steepled on his lap. He sat up straight in his chair,
unsteepled his hands, and picked up his flute.

Mara watched as the flute rose slightly in Adrian's right hand, then
stopped abruptly. He placed it carefully back down on the table. He
reached for another flute with his left hand, then withdrew it halfway.

Mara laughed. "No thank you," she said, reaching to cover Adrian's
flute with her left hand. She smiled. "I'm good."

Adrian raised his flute again, slowly this time. "Thank you," he said
as they drank, placing the flute back on the table.

"So you're here about..." Adrian said, tilting his head slightly to one
side.

"Uh...I didn't mention that. I'm not really sure what to call it yet."
Mara shifted uncomfortably in her seat.

Adrian reached for the flute Mara had covered with her hand. He smiled,
holding the flute up slightly with his left hand. "Ah, yes." He turned
his head and looked over Mara's shoulder. "I think I see it now. What
was your brother-in-law's name?" He paused for a moment, then picked
up a tiny savory treat from the platter with his right hand.

"His name is..."

Adrian looked up, a small smile playing on his lips. He popped the tiny
treat into his mouth and chewed slowly. He nodded slightly as if in
agreement with himself, then swallowed. "Ah, yes," he repeated, looking
at Mara directly. "You said you're not sure what to call it yet." He
paused for a moment.

Mara looked at the empty space where the flute had been. She shifted in
her seat again, feeling uncomfortable. She cleared her throat. "Uh...no,
I don't think I have a name for it yet."

Adrian leaned forward slightly. "May I ask what brought you here today?"

"I was curious about Fulcrum's work. My brother-in-law mentioned it to
me. He said there might be some help for me here, some information on...
attention training."

"Interesting," said Adrian. He took another small sip from his flute,
then leaned back in his chair, crossing one leg over the other. "Tell
me more about what you're looking for."

Mara thought for a moment. "I don't know if I can put it into words,
exactly. But I'm interested in learning how to be more present in
the moment. To quiet my mind, and pay more attention to my surroundings.
Do you think you can help me with that?"

Adrian considered the question for a moment. He took another sip from
his flute.

Adrian replied. "At Fulcrum, we have a unique approach to this
type of training."
