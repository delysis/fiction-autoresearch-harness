
Adrian looked down at the covered object in his palm, and said to Mara, “Well, that was quick. Thank you.” Then he closed his own hand over hers, smiled, and stood back up straight again, leaving her palm on his. He reached under her other hand and picked up the remaining two objects.

He handed one of them to Jonah, who accepted it in his right hand and put it back down beside the plastic cup. With the third object in his left hand, he returned to his place in front of the couch, his right arm still covered over by Mara’s palm.
“And now?” she asked, lifting her right hand away.
Adrian took it and turned it palm-up in front of him. “This is yours, I believe,” he said, holding it out to her.

Mara looked down at the brass key lying there on his palm, then up at him, and back down at the key. “Yes. Yes, thank you.” She accepted it and held it carefully in both hands for another long moment, looking at it; then she looked back up at the man who’d given it to her.

Adrian was watching her face. He turned and gave a small nod to Jonah, who placed the token beside his cup on the table as Adrian picked up the stone from his palm. “Your turn,” he said to Jonah, handing over the stone. Jonah accepted it. Mara took a step closer toward Jonah as Adrian began walking toward the doorway.

As soon as he was past them, he stopped and turned back around, speaking over his shoulder toward the group. “And now?” he asked. “What else is needed to verify your choice?”
The door opened to reveal an older woman in a long black coat; she was short, with gray-white hair held back from her face. “Nothing, Adrian,” said Livia.
“Well, good.” He walked toward the door and took the plastic cup from Jonah. “Here is this.”
“Thank you,” she replied, taking it from him.
Mara looked over at Jonah, still holding the token in his right hand and the stone in the other.
