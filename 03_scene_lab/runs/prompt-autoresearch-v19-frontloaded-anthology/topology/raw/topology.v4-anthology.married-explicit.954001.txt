
Simon set down the novel, his gaze locking onto hers with a mixture of amusement and sincerity. “Esther, I’m sorry. I know we've been through a lot, and it’s been hard to reconnect.”
He reached out, taking her hand in his and leading her to the bed. She sank down beside him, the quilt pooling around them as he leaned in to kiss her softly. The weight of unspoken words hung between them, heavy but not oppressive.
“We'll take it slow,” Simon promised, his eyes searching hers for reassurance. “Whatever you need. Whatever makes you comfortable.”
Esther nodded, feeling the warmth of his touch soothing the rough edges of their recent separation. “Okay,” she whispered, her voice barely audible. “Okay.”
As they lay together, tangled in each other's limbs and words unspoken, Esther felt a familiar warmth spreading through her chest. Despite the challenges they faced, despite the uncertainty of the future, they were here, together.
“I love you,” Simon murmured against her ear, his voice a low rumble that sent shivers down her spine.
“I love you too,” Esther replied softly, her lips finding his in a gentle kiss that spoke of tenderness and unspoken promises.
Their reunion was more than just a physical one; it was a recommitment to each other, a promise to face whatever came next side by side. And as they drifted off into sleep together, their hands intertwined beneath the covers, Esther knew that their love was strong enough to weather any storm.
