
The faint scent of hot milk and ginger tickled her nose. A warm hand touched her forehead; Mara leaned into it. She shut her eyes.
"Is that what you usually do?" Jonah asked, after a moment. Mara heard him move. He sounded less far away. "Just rest?"
"Yeah." She was glad of the dark. The room was starting to look slightly wavy, as if she were underwater. "I shower, sometimes take some sort of sleeping pill or muscle relaxant--"
"We'd have to make sure you don't have a reaction to any medication," Livia put in. She moved closer, but it was still easier to breathe. She lifted the back of Mara's hair off her neck and gently smoothed one hand down across the muscles of her shoulder. "Is it just your muscles, or do you feel drained?"
"Mostly I get dizzy if I get up too soon." Mara turned her head away from the touch. It was making her woozy just to be touched.
She was still a bit dizzy.
"How do you know that it's okay to get up?" Jonah asked. "And what kind of pill do you use?"
Mara opened one eye and looked at him, trying to make sense of what he'd just said. He'd moved closer; she saw Livia in her peripheral vision, close on his other side. She shook her head slightly, blinking up at him.
"Why do you want to know?" she asked.
Jonah looked at Livia for a moment, then back at Mara.
"Because I'm going to help take care of you," he said. "And it's easier if you don't get hurt."
His words made little sense; she saw his mouth move but no sound came. She closed her eyes.
"Why would--why--" She heard herself ask the question, but couldn't make sense of it. The touch was so distracting. She opened her eyes to look at Livia.
She had stopped touching Mara, and was leaning closer, saying something, but there was a hum in Mara's ears like the whine of mosquitoes at twilight, and she couldn't catch the words. She could see the movement of Livia's mouth, though, and that helped; her eyes were starting to roll involuntarily.
"You need to lie down." It wasn't quite in time with Livia's mouth, but it made sense, so it was probably what Livia was saying. "I need a blanket, and a pillow for your feet."
Mara felt herself being moved--there was Livia again--and realized that she must have fallen down onto the floor; the carpet felt soft under her and there was the pillow that they'd found in the back closet of the meeting room. She let someone else move the pillow, since she was too dizzy to do it herself.
"She can't get up yet?" Someone asked. Mara thought it was Livia, but she couldn't see who. She couldn't see much of anything at all.
The pillow was under her knees; that made sense, she felt better.
"No." That was Livia's voice, and there were hands on her again. "If we make her stand too soon, she'll go into shock."
"How do you know that?" Jonah wanted to know.
