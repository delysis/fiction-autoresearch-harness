

Mara withdrew her hand quickly, as if stung, but Jonah moved closer, his voice calm yet charged. "Do you need someone?" he asked softly.

She paused, eyes wide, unsure of how to respond. Had he meant to suggest what she’d been thinking? The tension between them hummed like an electric current. She stepped closer, her hand rising to frame his jaw, a daring invitation. His gaze dropped to her mouth, lips slightly parted, breathing heavy as hers.

The kiss ignited something feral in both of them. Their instincts took over—no logic, no restraint. They moved instinctively toward each other, every touch loaded with raw desire, like a dam about to break. But before it did, he pulled away abruptly, leaving the air electric with unspoken longing.
"I am a beast," he said, his voice gruff and low, filled with regret. "You deserve more."

She laughed at him in disbelief and walked away.
