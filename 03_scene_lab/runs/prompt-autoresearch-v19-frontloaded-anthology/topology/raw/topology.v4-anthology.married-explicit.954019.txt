
“I’ll have you know,” Simon said, closing the novel with a dramatic flourish, “I'm an excellent multitasker. Reading, writing, and...” He paused, raising a brow as he looked at her. “You know, other activities.”
Esther chuckled, stepping closer. “Oh, I’m sure you are. But maybe we should test that theory.” She reached for his hand, her touch gentle but firm.
Simon hesitated, a flicker of emotion in his eyes, but he didn't pull away. She felt the tension between them dissipate slightly. There was still so much they needed to say, so many unspoken words hovering in the air. But for now, their hands were enough to bridge the gap.
