Jonah greeted him warmly and waved Mara over to introduce them. His visitor shook her hand and asked her where she was from, and then asked about Jonah—apparently they had known each other since college, which Mara found interesting and a little disquieting. It was a surprise that Jonah had already been in this place for that long and she had never heard of him before, or of any of his research.
The visitor started to pour himself more wine from the bottle that stood by his plastic cup, when a hand rose beside him on the wall—an elderly woman’s hand, strong but lined. She put a finger around the lip of the cup, lifted it, and slid it off the table onto the tray she was holding out with her other hand. The man didn’t seem to notice at first, or maybe didn’t acknowledge noticing, just kept talking and reaching for his refill. The woman waited with the tray until the conversation had paused and then quietly passed by to clear it away.
Mara watched her go.
