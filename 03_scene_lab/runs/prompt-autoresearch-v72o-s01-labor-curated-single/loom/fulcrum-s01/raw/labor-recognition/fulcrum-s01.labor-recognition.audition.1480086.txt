Jonah introduced her, and he shook Mara's hand too. Then the brown-haired man said something to both of them and turned away; after a moment, Mara couldn't place what he'd said either, so she stopped trying.

Jonah left for his own room too, and as Mara watched him go, she tried to tell whether the brief eye contact had been a message or just an observation. The last person left was the brown-haired man, and Mara turned toward him, but it took her a moment to see why. His wine glass had disappeared; he hadn't finished it when he got up to shake hands, and now it wasn't on the table where he'd set it down. She didn't hear another door open or see anyone else in the room, and then she heard the water dispenser whoosh out water for someone who wasn't her, so she looked in that direction too.

A woman was there; Mara couldn't tell from here how old, but gray-haired and wearing a green Fulcrum T-shirt and shorts. The woman pushed an empty plastic glass under the spigot, glanced back over her shoulder in Mara's direction once, and went right on with what she was doing; she held up the glass with one hand and waved the other through a dispenser menu projected onto the tabletop, then filled it with water, and kept going without waiting for it to finish filling.
