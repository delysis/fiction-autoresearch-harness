Jonah kept an eye on Mara as the man introduced himself as Daniel. They spoke for a moment about nothing in particular. The brown-haired man turned to Mara and took her hand between both his, and then turned back to Jonah with some comment about the wine; Jonah smiled politely, but then Mara felt him moving toward her. As he approached, Jonah offered up another greeting. “You are the first, or are more arriving?”
“I’m first.” Mara’s eyes rose from Jonah’s hands to his face, to find herself under his gaze. He had a very open expression as he looked directly at her and continued, “This is Daniel, our visiting fellow this month.” The words were addressed to her, and yet the gaze remained locked on hers.
“Hello,” she replied again.

Mara found that she still couldn’t take her eyes off of Jonah, despite being close enough to him that if she turned her head at all, he would be outside her field of vision.
“I’ll show you your room,” he said suddenly. “I’m assuming you’re here to stay.”
He sounded as though he thought this was something one did when one arrived; Mara wasn’t sure she understood.

She hesitated and glanced at the man next to them, who now appeared to be studying her as intently as she had been studying Jonah just a few moments ago. She looked back and forth between the two of them for half a breath, then finally offered up an uncertain smile in response to Jonah’s question: “Yes. To stay.”
