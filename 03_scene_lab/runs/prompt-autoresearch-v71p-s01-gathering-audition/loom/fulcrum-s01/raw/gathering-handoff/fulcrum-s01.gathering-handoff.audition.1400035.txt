In the next movement: the unexpected interruption of voices; Jonah’s quick glance toward the kitchen, and Mara’s surprise at him for being surprised; a pause where she searches her own face and can’t be sure why, since she’s perfectly relaxed about everything in the world right now except that it’s getting later and they’re still holding hands; a few guests moving past the doorway, their voices lowered and absorbed in conversation with one another; an empty glass carried by the hand of the visitor nearest her; Mara watching him walk away with the glass; Jonah’s laugh at something she hasn’t heard but is suddenly supposed to understand, his eyes flicking back up toward hers as though there was an inside joke about that; Mara smiling too, not sure why, since nothing has changed around here for miles, and the only thing that just changed about them was how they were standing next to each other, not even holding hands any longer but only brushing shoulders like strangers at a party.
