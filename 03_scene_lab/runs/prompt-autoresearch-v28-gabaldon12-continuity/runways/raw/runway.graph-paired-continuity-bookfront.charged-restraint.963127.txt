The laboratory lights dimmed automatically. A red EXIT sign lit above the door, casting a weak beam across the polished concrete floor. Mara looked across the room, feeling cold despite the heat from their small crowd, and saw that Jonah was watching her. The low red glow from the exit sign made his hair look almost brown. His eyes were unreadable.

Their eyes locked and he hesitated. She took that as permission to stand. She felt numb and light-headed. Jonah was still seated, watching her. She moved to the exit door, and then paused just in front of him, waiting. He followed her motion with his gaze but made no move. She thought he would offer her a hand. He did not.

She touched his shoulder lightly with one finger, waiting, and he lifted his hand so she could place her own in it. She gave him only a light touch as they walked, but he gave her more of his weight, moving with her. They went through the door without speaking and descended the concrete stairwell.
