Jonah Vale waited with his hand open beside hers on the bottom step. No one else was in sight. No one would come out, either; the late session was over. The lab group wouldn’t be here again until next Tuesday night.

He didn’t look at Mara. He kept watching the dark driveway.

She reached out slowly until her fingertips were in his palm.

His fingers wrapped around her hand.

She leaned on her elbow as she closed her hand.

They could walk or take the car. Their apartment windows were already black, the late-night campus security lights already white. Their parking lot was as empty as the street, and the campus was quiet.

She took a breath as he closed his other hand.

He moved with her as she stood. His hand was the only thing of his she touched. He held onto it, but he stayed out of her way as they walked down the path and across the lot, past the black car and down the curb.

No one else was in the empty parking lot. No one else was in sight. The buildings’ windows were all closed, the doors all locked. No one else was on the late-night street.
