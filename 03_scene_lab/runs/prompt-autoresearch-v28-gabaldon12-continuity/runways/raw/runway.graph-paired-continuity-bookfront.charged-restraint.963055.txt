The lights in the lab dimmed, and the last of the crowd scattered into the hallways. It took me a full minute to pick up my notebook and walk the five steps to the door.

"Are you okay?" Jonah stood in the doorway, waiting for me to pass.

I nodded and pushed by him.

"Sorry," he said, following me into the night.

I stopped on the sidewalk and turned back to look at the front door of Fulcrum. The night was clear and very dark. The only light came from the building's front windows and the single streetlight over the parking lot.

"It was worse than I expected."

I nodded. "It was really bad."

"Did he do it on purpose?" Jonah asked.

"I don't know. Maybe he really is that bad a teacher."
