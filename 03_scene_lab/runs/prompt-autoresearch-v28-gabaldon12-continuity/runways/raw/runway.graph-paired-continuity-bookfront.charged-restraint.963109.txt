I never dreamed of kissing Jonah Vale. I dreamed of other people—of a kiss stolen from a first boyfriend and the shock of another man's mouth on mine on a midnight beach—but those were a thousand years ago. I was nearly thirty when Jonah offered me his hand and asked me to trust him.
I took his hand. I knew better than to dream about this kiss before it happened. I was too busy avoiding it, even if I'd still dreamed of kissing anyone.
