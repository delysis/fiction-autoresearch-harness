

She thought that one of them should be able to find words to say, but he didn’t open his mouth and she couldn’t make a sound. She was almost afraid to make a sound. They waited on the bottom step.

His mouth kissed her hair. Then her temple.

Then she kissed back with her whole mouth. The pressure of his lips made her eyes close.

She pulled her hand loose as her legs turned to water and he caught her around the waist. She slid against his chest in a way she hoped was graceful, and his arms rose higher around her, one hand behind her neck, the other lifting her closer to him.

He kissed her, and the kiss opened her from inside. It broke the ache that had been building all evening and let it release like a waterfall. She kissed back and knew this was real, knew she wasn’t hallucinating again.

The kiss was everything he had withheld and everything she had hidden and everything they hadn’t known how to say.

She broke away and his lips opened to her cheek. Then he opened his arms and held her at chest level until her legs remembered how to hold her up.

He opened his mouth as if to speak, but he closed it again without a sound.

She closed her eyes as he turned her head with his fingertips. She waited while his hands held her face, and she wondered how long they had stood there, wondering if she would have done what he just did.

It was the only way she would have trusted.

She pulled her face away and opened her eyes. Then she took a step away from him.

His eyes followed her but his arms dropped from around her, and he stayed where he was.

She turned, and he hadn’t followed.

He watched as she climbed into the passenger seat. Then he got in the car.
