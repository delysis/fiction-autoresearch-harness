

“Don’t want you to fall,” he said quietly. “Or me.”

She glanced at him and felt his gaze settle on her neck.

The Fulcrum lab door hadn’t just been locked. It had been barred like a dungeon. This wasn’t their lab.

But they couldn’t fight the head of the Division of Psychology, and they couldn’t fight the university president.

When she tried to stand up from the step, his hand on her wrist pulled her down.

He kissed the top of her head where there was no hair.

She lowered her chin, but he moved her face and kissed her cheek instead.

Her hand went to his collar. She felt the warmth through the cloth.

“Where are you?” he whispered into her ear. “Tell me.”

His lips were on her mouth when he said it.

She felt her shoulders move. Her arms wrapped around his shoulders. His fingers were in her hair again, holding her close.

He kissed her like a man who was hungry but not starving.

It wasn’t only his need that kept her pressed against him.

It was the fact that he didn’t know how long it would last.

The kiss ended before she wanted it to, and she took a breath before looking up.

He was looking down at her, still close, with something in his eyes that wasn’t only desire.

“I don’t know what I’m going to do,” he whispered, “without you.”

REFERENCE SCENE 5
"I am afraid," the man said finally.
She looked up at him, her dark eyes wide and curious.
"Afraid? Of what?"
"Of everything. Of not being good enough for you, and of being so happy that something's got to go wrong." He felt ridiculous saying it, but she made him feel comfortable with the truth.
"I know how you feel," she said, reaching up to rub his cheek. "You're good enough for me. But sometimes I worry if I'm good enough for you."
He shook his head vehemently. "Don't even think that." He looked at her, his eyes searching hers, trying to make her understand how he felt. "You're the best thing that ever happened to me, and I mean that with all my heart. I don't want anything else, not if it means losing you."
She blinked back tears, her eyes shining bright and clear in the moonlight. "You say such things to me," she murmured. "I don't know how I can ever thank you enough."
He smiled, his hand still resting on her cheek. "You don't need to thank me for anything. Just knowing you're happy makes me happy."
They stayed that way for a long while, just enjoying each other's company and the soft glow of the moonlight. When he finally moved to pull away, she stopped him.
"Wait," she whispered. "Just a little longer."
He nodded and leaned down to kiss her forehead. "As long as you need me to."
