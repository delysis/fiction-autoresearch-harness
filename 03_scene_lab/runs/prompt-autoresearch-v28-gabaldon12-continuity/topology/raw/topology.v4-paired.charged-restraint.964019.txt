This would have been enough. His touch would have made an exchange of warmth that would last all weekend. It would have meant something about him, how he wanted her, something about her, something he could know about her body, and that was what she wanted.

But then he brought his lips to her knuckles and pressed his mouth, the way he had in his lab coat, his mouth warm and full even through the fabric. His breath fell against her skin as he kissed the backs of her fingers.

She had not thought he would want this. She could not have wanted it, because if she wanted it, then he must, and she couldn’t trust her desire to know anything about Jonah Vale. She didn’t believe it.

The press of his lips was still full through the fabric.

Her hand lay heavy in his, and her lips trembled. She didn’t breathe; she felt only her lungs. She had stopped wanting his touch. She needed it; she needed what she would not have even a minute before.

His mouth opened around her fingers, and a soft warmth fell over them. Her wrist twitched and he let go. She saw his eyebrows rise as he stepped back. Her heart was too big for her chest. She opened her hand to feel the air against the inside of her wrist, where his breath had fallen. Her heart pushed against her ribs; her chest hurt.

She wanted more.

The thought moved through her like a wave. She wanted more; she wanted to know if he wanted more. But she could not ask. She didn’t have the words, and she could not believe him. If she asked, then she had to hear a No, or a Maybe.

Jonah Vale’s eyes were dark. He said, “We can walk,” and Mara heard something in his voice that she had not heard before, something low and warm and uncertain and possible.

He reached out again. His hand was too large, but it fit her, his thumb fell against the inside of her wrist, the warmth of his fingers spread from the inside of her knuckles all the way to where the hand met her forearm.

“Okay,” she said, and his lips turned up into a half smile. She didn’t look at him; she looked straight ahead as she started walking. She saw their building windows against the sky, empty rooms beyond empty windows, and she felt Jonah’s hand in hers all the way to the apartment door.
