

The moment it clicked, she looked up at him. “Jonah.”

His eyes lifted, then looked away again. He kept walking without letting go.

She didn’t slow to a stop. She didn’t move forward into the dark. But she stopped where she was until he gave his hand another tug. It was the first time he’d ever done that.

He paused and tilted his head as her fingers tightened back.

The second tug came. She felt the pressure of him through her shoulders even before she let herself go. They both had one hand free now.

A kiss began, open-mouthed and hesitant.

His palm was flat against her back and hers was curled at the center of his chest.

She moved against him, and it made him look up.

Her hand rose into his hair, but he didn’t try to meet her. He kept kissing her for another two steps before he pulled back with his head turned away.

“Mara.”

She stood there and kept breathing. She could let go any time.

But she had a sudden certainty that the night was just beginning.

She could hold on to it, or let it go.

She could move closer, or she could step away.

The kiss could start again, or they could walk together to their car without saying another word.

He looked at her once before he turned and began walking.

His hand was still open to hers as the dark fell.
