

Simon looked at the broken seal. "Your question was impossible," he said. "There was no choice."

Esther kept her finger pressed to his mouth. "Sometimes my questions are meant to be."

He took her hand and carried it to his chest instead.

"Then how many breaths should this get?" he asked. "Four? Five? Six?" He watched her eyes follow the rise and fall of each breath until she pulled her hand away.
