She touched the broken seal again. Then she walked over to a bedside table and put the drive into a drawer without checking its contents. The click of the drawer closing echoed through the empty room.

"I know you were," she said.

Esther sat down on the edge of her bed. She watched him through a tangle of wet hair, her eyes following the slow movement of his chest as he tried to say what had happened without speaking.

He took off his wet overcoat. Water droplets rolled down the fabric in dark lines.

"Esther."

She closed her eyes, her hand touching her hip where the knife handle protruded from her belt. The damp scent of the room mingled with the sound of Simon's breathing.

"Yes."

Simon unbuttoned his shirt slowly. A thin line of dried blood was visible below his ribs. "I opened the drive because—"

Esther stood up suddenly and pressed her hand over his chest where he had been injured. Her fingertips caught on a half-healed cut.

"What else?"

"Nothing," Simon said, his voice shaking. He looked down at her hand, then back into her face. "The rest is true."

Esther watched him for another long moment. Then she moved his hand away and touched the wound directly with her forefinger.

"Show me."

Simon pushed past her without speaking. His boots made soft sounds in the hallway.
