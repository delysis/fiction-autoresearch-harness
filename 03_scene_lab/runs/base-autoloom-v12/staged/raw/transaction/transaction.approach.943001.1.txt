

Esther took a step back, still looking at him. Her hands dropped to her sides but didn't relax—he recognized the same tension he felt when she waited for confirmation before firing.

She took another measured step back, putting the bed between them. Simon saw the moment she let go of his jawline in her mind: the pull of salt-hardened skin against flesh, the sharp edge of the stubble where she'd been tracing the line.

Her eyes didn't leave him. "Take off your shirt."

The demand didn't fit what they'd just said about leaving names behind. Simon looked down at his shirt, then back at her.

Esther lifted one corner of her mouth in a faint smile that reached neither her eyes nor her voice. "If you won't tell me what else is inside," she said, "I will find out."

Simon unbuttoned his shirt slowly, feeling the weight of her eyes on every movement. The material caught briefly at the top button, then fell open to show the scar tissue still red from yesterday's salt.

He wanted to speak and found nothing that would keep the silence from hardening further.

Esther looked down at the exposed skin. "Turn around."

The words came as a single unit, her eyes still fixed on his scar tissue. Simon obeyed, putting himself between her and the bed. He felt her step closer as she walked his name around his neck and down his spine. The pressure of her hand found its way to the raised lines of scar tissue at his lower back.

She moved his head slightly with one finger. "Let me see."

Simon didn't resist when she turned his head. The angle put his face half against her body, close enough to feel the warmth through his shirt and catch the faint scent of her perfume. His hands remained at his sides while she examined the injury under the bed's dim light.

Her finger traced a line along his jaw. "The scar tissue is thinner than it was."

The salt from yesterday's seawater left a white line between their bodies. Simon knew they hadn't changed position yet. Esther's hand found its way to his side, pressing into the muscle there with measured pressure. The movement wasn't sexual—it was assessment.

The bed creaked as she shifted closer, her knee touching his thigh. Her eyes didn't waver from the salt lines still visible on his skin.

Her voice stayed measured. "How deep is this one?"

Simon turned to look at her, expecting another examination. Instead, her hand closed around the edge of his trousers where the fabric met skin, fingers pressing into the muscle there with a force that didn't try to be gentle.

"Take off your trousers."

Simon watched her eyes as he undressed slowly. His hands followed the sequence: belt first, then buttons, the fabric falling in sections while she remained completely still. The sea-stiffened edges of the trousers made a soft scraping sound against the floor.

Her eyes never left him. The pressure of her fingers moved up his side to his ribs, a steady count he could feel through his shirt. He caught a glimpse of her hand moving back toward the bed, then disappearing from sight.

The scent of the seawater remained in the room, though it wasn't close enough to touch. Simon put one hand on the wall behind him and turned fully to look at Esther.

Her eyes remained fixed on the scar tissue under his ribs. The pressure of her finger pressed deeper into his skin. He could feel the texture of her ring against his palm through the material of her shirt.
