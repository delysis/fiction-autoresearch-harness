She stood alone for a minute, watching the light die off the building in the distance, before she turned to look at the door again. A small card was inserted into the top of the slot, which read, “Fulcrum Research Center.” The words were printed in bold letters, and there was a number underneath.
She wasn’t sure what to do next. Should she call them, or just wait? There was nothing in her instructions about any of this, but then again, there was no one to ask. So she waited for someone to come along who knew something about this place.
She didn’t have long to wait. A young man stepped out from the building, his white shirt wrinkled at the sides. He looked her up and down quickly and smiled.
“Hi,” he said. “You must be Mara.”
“Oh!” She smiled back, feeling relieved at finally having someone to talk to about this place. “Yes, I am.”
“I’m Adrian,” he said. He shook hands with her, and then waved toward the door. “Come on in,” he said, opening it for her. “Welcome to Fulcrum.”
She took a deep breath and stepped over the threshold into her future.
