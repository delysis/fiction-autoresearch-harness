The flight to California had gone smoothly. At the airport, I’d asked about a shuttle into town and found a new ride-share app instead. On the short trip north in a borrowed electric sedan, the driver and I had talked about the election results in North Carolina. We both agreed that it wasn’t any worse than usual, considering that our district had just elected a senator who refused to accept evolution or global warming.

My new phone kept beeping at me from the seat next to the driver. When I finally opened my messaging app, I saw that Livia had been texting me every few minutes for the last three hours.

The texts started at 3:52 pm Pacific.
