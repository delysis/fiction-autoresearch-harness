When Mara walked into the glassed-in conference area, one of the young men lounging by the doors greeted her with a quick glance. She nodded back but didn’t pause. He caught up with her as she strode through the room to the open door onto the terrace.

“Want to sit down? The talk starts in an hour.” The terrace was lined with folding chairs; no one was seated, just leaning on the balustrade, talking quietly.

The young man—college age?—was looking at her but didn’t stop. She watched his footfall, the way the heel of his right foot struck a bit harder than the left, throwing a fraction more weight that direction. Was it due to the angle of his stride? He turned away as he walked past her. She hadn’t recognized a clue there.

“What are you thinking about?” she asked him.
