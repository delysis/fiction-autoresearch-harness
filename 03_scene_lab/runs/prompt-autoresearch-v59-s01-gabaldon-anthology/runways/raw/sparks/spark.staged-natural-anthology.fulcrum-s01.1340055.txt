In the room at the end of the corridor, there were seven people, each sitting in a different kind of chair. One was a chaise longue, facing the large window, with an unlit candle on a small table beside it and a pile of books scattered across the floor nearby.
A young woman who had evidently been lying on the chaise was standing in front of the window now, looking out at the tall cypress and redwoods that stood beyond the edge of a green lawn, their tops crowned by fog. The trees stood guard before a rocky beach and the open ocean beyond, and the noise of the waves could be heard through the window.
