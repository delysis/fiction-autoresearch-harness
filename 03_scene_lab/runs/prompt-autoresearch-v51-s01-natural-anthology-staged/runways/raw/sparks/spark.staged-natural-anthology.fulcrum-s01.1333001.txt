“This is Mara,” Adrian said. He was explaining her to a group of men
who were standing under a painting by John Singer Sargent, and who
turned now toward the open doorway. Mara stepped out onto the wooden
porch that went around three sides of the house, which had been built
in the 1930s. Its windows faced west, toward the San Francisco Bay.

“Adrian, where are you?” someone else called. “You promised to introduce me to Mara.”

“I’m right here!” he answered.
