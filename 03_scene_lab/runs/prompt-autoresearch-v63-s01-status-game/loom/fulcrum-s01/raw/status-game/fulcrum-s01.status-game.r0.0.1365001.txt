
He didn’t move; but he smiled at her, and she saw that his face was a lot like hers, with blue eyes, high cheekbones, and brown hair that might be blond when it grew out. The smile lasted long enough for her to see the little crinkle lines around his eyes and feel the warmth of his gaze before he turned away again.
She felt the air moving around him as he sat up suddenly and moved to stand before her.
He was taller than she’d thought, with broad shoulders and a waist that curved into hips that she could almost reach. A narrow chest tapered to a slender neck above long arms and legs. His skin was white, but his dark hair gleamed in the dim light. She caught a glimpse of something soft and warm between his legs before he walked out of the room without a word.
