When she woke, Mara wasn't sure where she was. The dim light, the unusual sheets, the odd angles of the room — a ceiling that slanted sharply, windows so high up she couldn't see through them — didn't correspond to her last conscious moment.
The phone on her bedside table rang.
"Mara?"
She picked up the handset and closed her eyes again. "Yes."
"How did you sleep?"
"Fine. I don't know where I am."
