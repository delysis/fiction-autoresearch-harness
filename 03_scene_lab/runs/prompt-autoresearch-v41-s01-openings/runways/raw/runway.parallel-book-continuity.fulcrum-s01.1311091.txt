Mara felt her head spin as she entered. It wasn't just the sunlight and the smell of lavender, or even the feeling that time itself had slowed down. It was something in the way people were looking at each other—like they could read each other's minds.

She followed the Fulcrum's guide down a gently sloping path towards what looked like a grove of palm trees but was actually some sort of solar collector. "This is our main experimental space," he said, pointing out what Mara could only describe as a giant magnifying glass. "We'll be meeting Livia there shortly."

Mara tried not to stare at the people around her. Everyone seemed to be wearing loose cotton clothes in muted colors—beige and white and navy blue. But their postures were anything but muted. Some sat cross-legged on blankets, their spines straight and arms folded across their chests; others walked with hands clasped behind their backs, eyes fixed on distant horizons.
