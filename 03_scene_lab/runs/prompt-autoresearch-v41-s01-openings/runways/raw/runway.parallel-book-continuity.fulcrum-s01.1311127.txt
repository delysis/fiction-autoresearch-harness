When Mara stepped out of the car, she saw that Fulcrum was as beautiful as they said. She paused with one hand on the door, gazing up at the tall cliffs of bare stone above her, the dark red walls of buildings clinging like vines to their faces. She thought she had never seen such a sight. The sky was bright blue, and the sun shone fiercely on the stones, but the canyon itself seemed full of shadows. It looked as if she could reach out and touch those dark red walls.

"All right, Mara," the driver called behind her. "Let's go, sweetheart. I have to get back."

Mara turned back. She smiled at him, but her gaze didn't hold for long. She took her suitcase from the trunk. It was small, as she had been told it had to be. She had only a few changes of clothes and some toiletries. They said they'd give her more clothes once she was inside.
