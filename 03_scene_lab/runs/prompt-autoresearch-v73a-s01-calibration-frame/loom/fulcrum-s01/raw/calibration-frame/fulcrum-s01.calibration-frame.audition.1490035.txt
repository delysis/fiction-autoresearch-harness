They were interrupted by the sound of another door opening, and Adrian stepping into the room with a fresh cup in one hand and a pile of papers in the other.
Mara tensed before she consciously knew why, but when Adrian greeted Jonah with a smile and set down both the papers and the glass on the low table, he made eye contact with Mara again. He looked at her intently, and she couldn’t tell whether she was under scrutiny or not. But he was looking at her.
“Good evening. Mara, right? I’m Adrian.”
She shook hands, too, and then everyone glanced down at the new cup of wine on the table. Adrian glanced from that back up to them, and seemed to realize something. He tapped the side of his own head, as if to say, Whoops, forget that for now. “Don’t worry about the drinks,” he told Mara. “The catering crew’s still here until seven.”
Mara wasn’t quite sure what he meant. She wondered if Adrian’s wife was the one who’d set up tonight’s audition process.

And then, as she watched them move toward the table, Mara noticed something else: Jonah had already set a second glass on the low table earlier. Had he been waiting for someone else to come? A fourth person, then, was supposed to join them here? But there was no fourth person coming in through the door, nor anyone walking in her direction. There were only those two people in the hallway, still talking—a woman with short dark hair and a man who looked like Adrian’s age but not his brother. They were heading down the hall toward the kitchen instead, apparently making their own way to get a drink, no longer visible now that the corner had blocked them off from view.

And the catering worker didn’t bring anyone else’s cup back with her through the open hallway, either; just Jonah’s glass that she carried away. So there were still two empty seats at the table for people who hadn’t arrived yet. Mara glanced back at Jonah again and thought: Are you waiting for someone else to show up here?

“Adrian, did you bring the agenda for today’s meeting?” Mara asked after a while, trying to figure out what to say next and wondering if he’d respond in turn.

Adrian looked up from his cup again. “Do I look like I remember anything about any agenda?” he said, chuckling slightly.
