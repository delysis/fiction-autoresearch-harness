There was only one man in the room left for Mara to meet; she’d watched his brown-haired counterpart greet Jonah, now he turned toward her and extended his hand.
“Adrian,” he said, with a firm handshake that let her feel the length of his palm against hers. He had a dark complexion, eyes so nearly black they were almost as dark as his hair, and a hint of a smile at the corners of his mouth that didn’t quite reach his eyes; he had to be an introvert, she thought, then instantly checked herself.
“Mara.”

And that was the moment that Adrian turned and saw something she didn’t see—saw, perhaps, her very thought—because his hint of a smile dropped away from his mouth without changing his eyes.
“Adrian,” he said, shaking hands again.
