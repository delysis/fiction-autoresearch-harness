
She was feeling more depleted than usual. She wasn't sure how much of the strain was from the actual shift itself and how much was because they were back home on her own planet--which meant she was working harder to manage the same level of power and influence, for some reason. Livia would probably argue that it was an equal mix of factors, while Jonah would look at her closely and say it didn't matter; it was all her responsibility whether she wanted it or not.
"Of course not." The warm mug settled between Mara's hands, filling her lungs with a fragrant mist of herbal tea. "I brought you something to eat."
Mara let herself relax. This was what Livia was like when she was making genuine offers without being pushy or provocative. It was hard not to like it.
"I know I told you to let me take care of myself first, but... this is easy. Come on." Mara drank deeply, inhaling the scent that reminded her of something warm and citrusy but couldn't quite name it.
"What are you trying to do to me?"
Livia smiled with just enough innocence to be convincing. Mara gave a small laugh and set the mug on the desk beside her computer.
"I don't know."
Livia took the cup. "Drink this first. It will settle your stomach and relax you."
Mara looked down into the steaming tea, wondering if she might have overplayed her hand by coming out so openly. Livia was smart enough not to push, though--she had a reputation to consider and Jonah watching from behind her--so Mara shrugged and lifted the cup to her lips again.
"If you're really so tired, we can always go for a walk on the beach." Livia's eyes glittered, challenging Mara to say no. "The sky will be clear tonight."
"I know I shouldn't," Mara said, glancing back over at Jonah. He was still standing there, arms crossed, his expression unreadable. She felt a small stab of anger that he didn't seem more concerned about her welfare; if Livia could pretend to care so convincingly, he should at least have the decency to do it honestly. "But if we're not careful..."
"Don't worry." Livia looked away from Mara towards the door. "We'll come back to bed before you know it." She was smiling now, her lips curved up in a soft crescent that was somehow both inviting and mocking.
Mara caught herself staring at the curve of Livia's mouth, then took a long slow breath to clear her mind. When she exhaled, she tried to push the thought away. "I should probably sleep," she said, forcing a note of lightness into her voice. "Maybe not right now..."
Livia raised an eyebrow, waiting for Mara to finish the thought. "You should do what you need to do," Mara said. "I can take care of myself."
"No arguments from me," Livia said. She lifted her mug towards Mara. "Drink that. And if you're not ready for bed yet..."
She paused, clearly expecting an answer.
Mara shifted uncomfortably. She had a vague memory of another offer like this one; something about taking a walk on the beach with Jonah? But when she thought about it, she couldn't remember what Livia had meant by the offer--if anything specific--or why she'd made it at all.
