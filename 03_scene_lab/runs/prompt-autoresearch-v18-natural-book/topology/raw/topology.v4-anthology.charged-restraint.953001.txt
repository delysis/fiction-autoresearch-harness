

They walked in silence, the hum of the city wrapping around them like a thin veil. It wasn't until they reached her door that the stillness felt suffocating. Turning, she met his eyes, searching for something she didn't yet understand. Without a word, she took the space between them and bridged it. Her lips were soft against his, a simple, tentative touch, but there was nothing tentative in the way she pressed closer.

The kiss lingered, neither of them pulling back even when they both knew they wanted to. Their breathing grew erratic, mingling in the cool air. For a fleeting moment, it felt as though the entire world had been stripped away, leaving only this intense, unspoken exchange. But the weight of what was unsaid hovered between them, and eventually, Jonah's thumb brushed her cheek—a gentle signal to break the contact.

Mara pulled away slowly, her heart racing, eyes wide with something she couldn't quite name. "Not yet." The words were faint but steady. "We need to keep moving. But… this needs to mean something."

He nodded, his expression unreadable. "We'll find a way."
