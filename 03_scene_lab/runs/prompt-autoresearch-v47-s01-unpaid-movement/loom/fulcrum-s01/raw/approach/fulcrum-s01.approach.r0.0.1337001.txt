

Adrian placed his flute on the low table and leaned slightly forward.

"Tell me your expectations," he said. "Why have you come?" Mara noticed
how Adrian placed both feet flat on the floor. She leaned slightly
forward. "I've heard so many wonderful things," she said with a slight
smile. "I'm intrigued." "And," he continued with a nod. "And what has
intrigued you, exactly?" Mara noticed how Adrian did not take another
sip of his flute. She looked at her lap and then back at Adrian. "It's
just that," she began and then paused. "Well, it's so interesting how
we don't say anything but we all understand so much about each other,
don't we?"

Adrian raised his flute slightly. "Indeed," he said as they drank again.
"You've done a lot of reading, it sounds like." Mara smiled at the
sound of the stone waterfall. "Well," she said. "I mean, it's only what
we all experience every day, isn't it?" Adrian nodded at her response.
"Well," he continued after a pause. "We don't only study ordinary
nonverbal cue-reading at Fulcrum, we develop more advanced methods for
nonverbal calibration and interaction that you won't find elsewhere.
Not on the internet, not in any book, not at any other institute." Mara
nodded. "Well, I'm intrigued," she said. "I'd like to learn." Adrian
leaned slightly back in his chair. "Of course," he said with an
unforced smile. "You're here now. You've arrived."

Mara turned her head slightly as the young man in a black suit who
greeted Mara at the door earlier reappeared. "Adrian, may I have a word
with you?" "Of course," Adrian said with an incline of his head and a
raised hand. He stood up from the chair and turned to Mara. "Take a few
minutes," he said. "Have another drink. We'll continue shortly." Mara
nodded as Adrian walked away with the other young man in a black suit
and the young man in a black suit disappeared behind the reception
counter.

Mara relaxed on the short couch. She glanced at her flute. The young
man at the reception area reappeared with another small stack of folded
white napkins and a tray with two flutes filled with golden liquid.
"Welcome to Fulcrum," he said cheerfully as he placed one flute next to
Mara's flute on the table in front of her, along with two other napkins.

Mara turned to watch Adrian return with the other young man in a black
suit. "I need you to attend to this," Adrian said quietly to the
younger man. "I'll handle it myself," the young man said. "No,"
Adrian replied. "I need you to handle this." The younger man looked at
Adrian with an open expression, then nodded and walked away.

Mara turned to look at her flute, still half-full. She turned to watch
as a woman in a gray suit with a name badge arrived, escorted by the
younger man with the flutes. The young man handed the woman a flute and
a napkin and then walked away. She looked around the reception space
while Adrian greeted her and then turned and walked away. She moved to
the table with chairs and white cloths.
