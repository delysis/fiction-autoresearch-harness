
Jonah smiled and said, “Here’s the host.”

As Livia stepped into the room, Adrian pulled back from Mara and moved toward the couch, letting her come into his space.
Mara did not follow. She stepped backward to give herself more room to watch this exchange—she wasn’t sure whether it was necessary or useful, but she was watching, nonetheless—and let her arms fall away from her body as she shifted her weight to her right foot.
Livia stepped to one side, letting Mara into her space now; she set her bottle down on the floor with a soft clink of glass against glass, and then she stood there for a moment looking at Mara, still silent except for the sound of her heel on wood. Her expression didn’t change; it just remained blank.
And then Jonah spoke again: “Should we do that?” His gaze went first to Livia and then back to Mara; then he held up the wine, took a sip, and set it back on the table with another clink against glass.
Mara could hear him clearly. His voice was soft, almost conversational—there wasn’t any tension or urgency there, but there was something else; a quality she couldn’t name, like an edge or a curve.
And then he took her hands and drew her forward to stand beside him on his right.
Her skin tingled under the touch of his fingers as they pressed together; it felt strangely good to have those two points of contact against her own flesh—a pleasant contrast against the cold metal surfaces around her—but she was too distracted by what he said next to think about that:
“Mara’s the one who found our lost cat.” He sounded like he was teasing himself; it almost seemed as if he might laugh out loud, though he didn’t. His eyes stayed on Mara’s face as he said this, but she noticed how Livia’s expression changed; she tensed just slightly at first, then relaxed again.

Mara didn’t see any tension in Livia’s face, but she could feel it, because of something in her body language—or maybe even because of that shift in air before anyone had spoken; she wasn’t sure which yet, and there was no time for more thought when Adrian turned back around again
