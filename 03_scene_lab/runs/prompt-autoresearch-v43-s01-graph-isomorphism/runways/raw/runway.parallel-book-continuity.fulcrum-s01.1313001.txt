A rideshare deposited adult newcomer Mara at the Fulcrum Institute of
Psychotechnology. She wore a black jersey dress, ankle-length with wide
sleeves and a waist sash tied loosely in front. A dark gray leather
backpack held her laptop and other essentials. With a single small
suitcase, she had begun as a day student the week before but now moved
into the residential compound.

The black-clad driver waited while Mara retrieved the suitcase from
the trunk. She carried her own bag but let him carry the suitcase.
In a parking area in front of a long low building, he turned and
opened his door. The early evening light faded as the sun set behind
trees and distant mountains. He stood at his door for a moment before
putting a hand to his chin while scanning the area. Mara took a moment
to breathe, feeling a wave of tiredness, even though she had slept the
night before.

The driver lifted the suitcase off the pavement. He carried it with one
hand, holding onto the handle and moving smoothly toward the building.
As he went, he turned his head, scanning the area ahead of him.
