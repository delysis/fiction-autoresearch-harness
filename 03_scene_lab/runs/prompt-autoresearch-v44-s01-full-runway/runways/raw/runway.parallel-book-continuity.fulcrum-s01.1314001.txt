A rideshare dropped adult newcomer Mara with one suitcase at Fulcrum's
redwood-and-glass compound on a curving Northern California coast
before evening. A tall man in a black suit stepped out from under
a red-and-white striped awning to carry the suitcase. "I'm Adrian,
the Head of Fulcrum," he said with an unforced smile. "Welcome." Mara
uncomfortably noticed how he held open a glass door with his body
rather than one hand.

Adrian led Mara in, past a row of small potted palms and a stone
waterfall to the right, under a painting of a waterfall. A young man
in a black suit walked out from the center doors ahead of them. He
spotted Mara. "Ah! You're here! Welcome." He stopped at the outer door
and Adrian passed him with a slight incline of his head. They entered
a double-height space with a skylight and potted palms between
the reception area, a long table with white chairs and white cloths,
and two short black couches. The other young man in a black suit led
Mara to a short couch facing a potted palm in a rough stone pot. "Wait
here please," Adrian said before leaving Mara to sit in silence for a
few minutes.

The sound of the stone waterfall became louder and more distinct.
Mara relaxed slightly. Another younger man appeared and offered a tray
of flutes full of golden liquid, a small stack of folded white napkins,
and a small platter of tiny savory treats. "Welcome to Fulcrum,"
the young man said brightly. Mara picked up a flute and took a sip,
noticing how smoothly the effervescent liquid slid down her throat.
The young man placed two other flutes with golden liquid on a low table
adjacent to where Mara sat, then disappeared behind the reception
counter. Mara tasted one of the tiny savory treats, noticing that it
was warm, not hot.

Adrian reappeared in a few minutes. He pulled up a chair and sat down,
placing his flute on the table between them. The young man at the
reception area reappeared and silently placed another flute with golden
liquid on the table near Adrian. He returned to the reception area.

"Welcome," Adrian said with an unforced smile. "I'm Adrian, the Head
of Fulcrum." Mara looked at the flutes in front of Adrian. She smiled
and nodded. "Mara," she said. "Thank you." Adrian raised his flute
slightly, and Mara raised her flute to match. They both drank.
