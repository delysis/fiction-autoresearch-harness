The voice seemed both loud and distant at first, then settled into a pleasant rumble that made sense with the face of the bearded man standing next to two people who were talking about a conversation.

Mara waved off the man and woman who had met her and introduced herself to Adrian, who shook her hand, nodded to the others, and walked toward the far end of the building. She followed without catching any conversation as she passed people she was hearing and seeing for the first time. The others scattered in different directions while she stuck near Adrian, whose voice seemed louder in her presence.

He mentioned the food waiting and said she would meet people for dinner at the welcome, so there was no hurry. She smiled and looked around as he spoke about the area around them. They passed several low buildings that looked like they contained dorms, and she wondered where to look for hers. Adrian pointed out two structures that were farther away. She recognized them from the videos.

Adrian stopped to introduce Mara to a couple of people who were sitting on a couch outside the structure that he said contained dorms. The couple looked sleepy, but smiled and shook her hand. Adrian continued toward a wide outdoor dining area. He described some activities that would take place there over the next few weeks and asked Mara if she had any questions about the food, which seemed to be his focus. She asked if there was vegetarian or vegan food.

He answered yes and added that they had tried hard to offer options for different dietary requirements. She realized this might have been true when she first visited Fulcrum a year ago, but it had changed now that Fulcrum was an official institute. She wondered how many people there were in the room that Adrian had pointed out as the welcome location. It seemed huge even from the distance that they were, and she could tell that Adrian had already noticed the lights over there, which had turned on within a minute of their arrival.

She asked Adrian if he could show her the dorms before dinner, but he was in a hurry and suggested she ask someone else. He said goodnight as he walked away, but Mara heard him mention dinner to several people on his way to the welcome structure. She watched him for a minute before going back toward the sleeping couple. She saw a woman approach and offer her a glass of something that Mara recognized from the videos as an alcoholic drink.
