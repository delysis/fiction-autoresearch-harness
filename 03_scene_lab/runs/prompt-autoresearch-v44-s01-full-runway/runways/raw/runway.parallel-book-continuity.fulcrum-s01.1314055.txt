In my rideshare on the way up from San Francisco, the driver and I
discussed whether Fulcrum was a cult or not. He said he was looking for
a girlfriend, and that some women might be vulnerable to cult influence.

“What is your opinion?” I asked him.

“I think you should be careful,” he said.

When he pulled to the end of the paved drive at the Fulcrum compound,
it was just after 6 PM and a little too dark for my eyes to adjust and
see the whole thing at once. I had a little trouble finding the entrance
in the dim light. A group of people were gathered around the front
doors. One of them stepped forward and waved me over. “Hello,” I said.
“Are you Mara?” she asked, then opened the front doors for me. “I’m
Gladys.” We shook hands.

“Welcome to Fulcrum,” she said.

She turned out not to be Gladys, who was a younger woman with very
short hair. The taller woman who greeted me instead was a senior staff
member of the organization.

She took my bags and showed me to my room, and then told me there’s a
group dinner tonight. She said that if I’d like to join, I should go to
the main dining area now. “We start at 6:30 PM sharp,” she said.

I asked where that was.

She said she would take me there. We walked through the main building,
and then outside a bit and down some stairs.

“Oh yes, here we are, welcome to our dining area,” she said.

A lot of people were already standing around, mostly young, some older.
They all seemed very relaxed. I knew it was a nice dinner because I
could smell it; there was a large buffet table laid out with food. Some
of the people were serving themselves and sitting at tables.

“Let me introduce you,” she said. “My name is Jane.” She brought me over
to some of the other new people like me, and we all chatted about where
we came from and why we were here. One guy asked if this was a cult.

“So far not yet,” I said. “It seems friendly.”

Everyone laughed. We had all just come to work here. Some of us would be
doing research, but I was going to be doing administrative things. A
woman named Mary would be showing me where everything is tomorrow.
