# Independent Blind Review 1

The reviewer received only the anonymous A/B/C reader packet. Pipeline,
checkpoint, score, and reveal information were unavailable.

## Verdict

**B > C > A**

- A vs B: B
- A vs C: C
- B vs C: B

B had the best emotional exchange and clearest romantic agency. C had the most
embodied kiss and strongest uncanny sting, but was more declarative and
sermon-shaped. A was the most repetitive and least convincing about Jonah's
passivity.

| Finalist | Romantic pull | Heat | Character fascination | Trust | Prose freshness | Clarity | Desire to continue | Preachiness |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 5 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 6 |
| B | 6 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 6 | 5 | 5 |
| C | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 6 | 6 | 6 |

## Best evidence

For B, the reviewer selected Jonah's plain explanation of the wrist rhythm:

> “I just thought you looked like you were disappearing. I wanted to give you
> something that was actually there. Something that didn’t require a theory to
> exist.”

This is the packet's most human Jonah moment: concrete, emotionally legible,
and connected to prior action without making him a lecturer.

For C, the reviewer selected the moment when stopping intensifies rather than
deflates the encounter:

> “The kiss had stopped while they both still desperately wanted it to
> continue. Jonah stayed where he was, his eyes dark and focused. He didn’t
> look frustrated; he looked awake.”

## Principal defects

- Livia, Miriam, and Jonah form too neat a moral triptych: violation, rescue,
  and perfect restraint.
- Jonah needs a cost, temptation, blind spot, or private agenda. He is currently
  a consent principle wearing a jacket.
- Mara should accomplish more of her own escape; Miriam can support or impose
  consequences after Mara acts.
- The narration restates the moral after successful dramatic beats.
- Christian imagery is present, but a specifically Christian practice,
  relationship, doctrine, or memory does not yet cause Mara's decision.
- The kisses should retain the concrete sensations and lose roughly half of
  their interpretive gloss.

## Readiness

B is ready for ordinary human appraisal only as a **developmental prototype**.
None of the three should be represented as polished commercial fiction.
