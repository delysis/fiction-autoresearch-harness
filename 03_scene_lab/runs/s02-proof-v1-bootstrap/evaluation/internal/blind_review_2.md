# Independent Blind Review 2

The reviewer received only the anonymous A/B/C reader packet. Pipeline,
checkpoint, score, and reveal information were unavailable.

## Verdict

**B > A > C**

- A vs B: B
- A vs C: A
- B vs C: B

B won because Mara physically initiates the kiss, Jonah briefly sounds like a
person rather than a consent allegory, and their exchange has the most
reciprocal specificity. A had the cleanest mystery tag. C was the most
inflated, sermonized, and generically literary.

| Finalist | Romantic pull | Heat | Character fascination | Trust | Prose freshness | Clarity | Desire to continue | Preachiness |

| A | 5 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 6 | 5 | 5 |
| B | 6 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| C | 5 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 6 |

## Best evidence

The reviewer chose B's physical initiation as the strongest romantic action in
the packet:

> “Mara leaned in. She closed the gap, her hand finding the rough fabric of his
> shirt, pulling him slightly closer.”

The reviewer also identified A's restraint mechanism as effective:

> “Because he wasn't pushing, she found herself leaning in. The lack of
> pressure created a vacuum of desire, a slow-burning heat that began to pulse
> in time with the memory of his thumb against her wrist.”

## Principal defects

- The late session repeats S01's conceptual victory: Livia misreads Mara, Mara
  changes the signal, and interpretive certainty collapses. Stakes rise, but the
  scene does not change causal machinery enough.
- Jonah scarcely needs chastity because the narrative gives him no unruly self
  to govern. His silence during coercion is praised without testing whether it
  is protection, cowardice, or complicity.
- Livia is a coercive method wearing a woman's face; Miriam is an ethical
  referee; Adrian's institutional culpability disappears.
- The kiss follows psychological coercion too quickly and is interpreted after
  nearly every gesture.
- The operative worldview is autonomy in sacramental diction. There is little
  lived Christianity: prayer, Christ, Scripture, ecclesial relationship, or a
  conscience before God does not materially alter action.
- The repeated antitheses, abstract architectural metaphors, synchronized room
  reactions, and fully decoded images create a recognizable synthetic cadence.

## Readiness

Before broad human circulation, use B as the base, cut roughly one quarter to
one third of the late-session explanation, borrow A's cleaner access-log hook,
complicate Jonah's non-intervention, give Livia one credible humane motive, and
make Mara's faith causal through one or two concrete practices or commitments.
