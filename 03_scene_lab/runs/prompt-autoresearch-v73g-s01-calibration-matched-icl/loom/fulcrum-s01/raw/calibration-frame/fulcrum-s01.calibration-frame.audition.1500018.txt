
The older woman had brought the young man his glass, then taken it back after he drank from it. That was a job, and someone would pay her for it. This was what they’d done with money over generations: they paid women to clean up after men and children, and then the next generation considered it natural that some women always knew where the trash belonged.
A third voice came out of the hallway now, loud and clear; and Mara realized it was hers, and someone was answering it.
Someone named Adrian.

She was standing stock-still with her eyes fixed on the woman when she heard him say her name. She startled a little and turned around, to find him smiling at her. His face looked younger than his body’s size had made it seem, but there was a smile on it now where he hadn’t smiled before. He looked her over with slow curiosity and said, “Are you looking forward to meeting the Reader?”
“I haven’t met anyone else yet either.” And this wasn’t exactly true, though she wouldn’t admit how close to true it was.
“Yes,” Adrian replied, nodding very seriously, “I have noticed that I too haven’t met anyone yet. Let me introduce you.”

As he spoke, someone else appeared in the hallway behind Mara’s shoulders. She turned to look and found her original blonde man, Jonah, standing there with his own glass of wine.
“I thought it was your turn,” he remarked, watching Adrian move forward.
“Well,” said Adrian, “not yet, no. In fact, I wasn’t quite finished.”
His eyes shifted past Jonah to the hallway behind him, where the blonde woman who was supposed to be Mara’s reader now stood alone and unsmiling in a white blouse with gold embroidery at the cuffs, holding her own wineglass very loosely in her left hand.

Adrian glanced back at them both for a moment before saying, “Hello.” His voice sounded oddly light and airy as he looked at her.
She was young and attractive—maybe about Mara’s age? But she seemed older somehow; maybe because she wore glasses. Or maybe it was just that Mara saw so little of herself in her, even though they had the same brown eyes.
