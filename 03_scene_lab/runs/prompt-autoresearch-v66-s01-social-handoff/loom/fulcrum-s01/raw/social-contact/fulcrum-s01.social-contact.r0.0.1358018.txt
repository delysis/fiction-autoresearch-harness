
“Hi,” Mara said softly, then tried to remember what she’d been told to say. “Um, it’s my first day at Fulcrum. I’m Mara.”
She held up the small card in her hand. Her name was there, and the word TRAINEE.
His eyes drifted toward the card as he took a moment to reply.
“I’m Adrian.” He put his right arm down again. “Welcome.”
The right hand spread out over the surface of the low table; the fingers flexed lightly. Mara felt slightly exposed, though he’d already been here when she arrived. She tried not to think about how her own body was positioned, only to note how Adrian was occupying space. She stepped forward slowly.
“I don’t know quite what to do next,” Mara said. “Should I be sitting down or—”
“Wait.”
His tone was low and even, but somehow the word had an edge to it that she couldn’t account for. Mara stopped midstep, holding perfectly still. She looked at her feet and wondered if she was doing what he wanted her to do. There was a slight ripple in the air; one of the corners of his mouth seemed to turn upward. His hand shifted on the table surface, then turned over as if he was tapping something invisible just out of his reach.
Mara took her eyes off her feet, lifted them up the length of his arm toward his face. He was still watching her. She tried not to imagine what she might be seeing in his expression, what he might be seeing in hers. He cleared his throat.
“Can you come sit down here?”
Mara nodded slightly and took three slow steps to join him on the couch, the plastic card dangling at her side. “What’s this for?” she asked. “How do people use it?”
Adrian said nothing for a moment; then, without moving his lips or changing expression, he seemed to sigh deeply. “I suppose I should be showing you around first.” He pushed himself up with one hand, levering upright into an easy standing position.
She looked up at him, remembering that she needed to try and stay balanced between focusing on his body language and paying attention to the words he would use. She found herself breathing more easily as he stood.
“Welcome again,” he said, “to Fulcrum.”
