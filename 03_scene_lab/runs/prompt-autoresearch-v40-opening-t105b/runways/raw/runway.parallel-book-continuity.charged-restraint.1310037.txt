The building had two separate stairways. A public entry. The front door opened directly on a little terrace outside the session chamber. I saw a small dark tree. The black leaves had a light smell of night blooming something. No birdsong, nothing. A faint smell of the city, almost gone. I remembered my father always saying it would be over by the time he died, which was how I knew we were waiting to end something bad, but never what.

A sudden coolness in my air, almost too quiet, a stillness just above silence, all from two steps up, moving slowly on to stand beside me, breathing slowly into his own hands as if to calm them, one hand coming open then turning in toward him and holding still, the other holding an unlit cigarette he was not ready to smoke yet, breathing calmly in toward himself. The black tree was so quiet that even after it was gone it held the silence it made when it came to stand there with us.
