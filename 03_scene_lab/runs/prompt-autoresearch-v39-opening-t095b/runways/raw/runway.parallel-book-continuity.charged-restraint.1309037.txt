They had reached the parking lot. Her car was across the lot, under the security lights; his car was on the other side, near the security shack. He followed her as they walked beside each other; she could feel the tension in the backs of her arms from swinging them against her body, and the slight chill of the evening breeze, though it wasn’t cold. She could see that her car’s tail light was out, and wondered why she hadn’t done anything about that.

Jonah said, “You’re walking stiffly. Do you still feel tense?” He glanced down at her, as if trying to read something in her body.

Mara said, “I don’t know. Why do you ask?”

“You looked tense in the session, too.”

She felt a quick stab of anger. “We were roleplaying. We always feel tense in sessions.”

He seemed taken aback. “Oh,” he said. “Yes. Of course. It’s just that you seemed more—tense than usual.”
