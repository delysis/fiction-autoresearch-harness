I was too rattled to think, so I took the quickest path toward the exit. It led outside, onto the grass behind the building. It wasn’t far to the parking lot. I knew where my car was. I didn’t want anyone to see me. I wanted to be alone, where no one would have to talk to me or try to explain.

I knew what they would say if they tried. They would say that I had done well, in spite of everything. That I should not blame myself for a brief lapse of self-control. That we all struggle to stay attuned to our own sensations. We are each at the mercy of our own bodies, and that’s what we do here—we study that, together, learning how to see more clearly.
