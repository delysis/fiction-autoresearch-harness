

Esther leaned against him, close enough to feel the slight rise of his breath. "We don't have a map for this." She placed one hand over the heart of the drive. "You keep opening doors you can't see through."

He touched her back where she had been wounded. The scar tissue ran cool beneath his palm, like a promise of shelter he didn't have the strength to build.

Simon stood up. "Let me touch you first," he said.

Esther stood with him. She pressed his hand against the scar on her thigh before guiding it between her legs. He felt the slight wetness there, the heat that answered to his own urgency.

He entered her slowly, counting each inch like an oath. Esther put a hand on his chest and listened through the sound of their bodies coming together.

"You're listening again," he said.

"Yes." She moved her hips slowly, taking him deeper. "I'm not listening for danger."

Simon let her hear him break. He pressed himself forward, hearing the small noises she made in return—the breath that changed, the weight of her hand tightening on his chest.

"That wasn't in the plan," she said when he finished.

He kissed her shoulder. "No plan lasts."

They stood still together, listening to their own breaths and the sound of the drive turning beneath Esther's touch.

Simon reached for the empty notebook again. "This has to be written somewhere."

Esther took his hand. "It's been memorized."

He held her eyes. The inkwell had soaked its own reflection into the wood of the table. The paper he had filled earlier remained ruined beneath it.

She pulled a small slip from her sleeve. Simon took it, careful not to touch the edges. Esther watched him unfold the paper slowly. The writing was hers, but she didn't recognize the words as her own.

"Who does that belong to?" she asked.

"It belongs here."

Esther reached out and touched the broken seal again.

Simon traced the scars on her palm.

"We've been wrong about this house," he said.

Esther folded her hand around his. "What do you feel?"

He listened. "Something small moving inside."
