
She stepped lightly to keep up. “Do we take a car?”
He glanced sideways and hesitated, as though he hadn’t quite heard the question. She heard her own voice, light and fast, like a child’s. The lab was closed but the security cameras were on and they stood under a tall light on the corner of the driveway. The night air blew across the back of her neck. Her hair moved in the light.
“Walk.” His voice was low as well. He turned his head and looked ahead as though there was nothing to say. He had dark eyes and a thin nose, and he didn’t smile. He was wearing dark clothes. She walked beside him but couldn’t see his expression. She walked close enough for his body to block the light, but still he walked like he wasn’t even with her.
“Sure.” The word came out wrong, sharp. She wanted to take it back but had nothing better to say instead. He started down a dark path, and she went after him, closer than she would have on purpose. “Where are we going?”
