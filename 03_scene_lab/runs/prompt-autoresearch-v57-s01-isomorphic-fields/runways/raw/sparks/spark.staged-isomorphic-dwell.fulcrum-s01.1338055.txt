Dramatic design
Offer (15%): The woman invites a man to join her in the surf.
Counteroffer and resistance (25%): He joins her despite his initial reluctance and doubts about her sincerity.
Complication and embodied negotiation (35%): Both feel the erotic tension of their proximity but maintain a playful, innocent facade.
Consequential turn (15%): They discuss the possibility of physical intimacy later, hinting at their growing attraction.
Concrete stopping state (10%): They exit the water, maintaining an air of innocence while acknowledging the potential for future encounters.
