Adrian’s smile was a polite request to engage on his own terms. For the next hour, Mara made no move, did not let it cross her face that she had noticed this particular fellow, although he kept popping up at the edges of her field of vision, standing among groups, bending toward others with a friendly gesture. He didn’t rush anywhere; he was careful in his movements, never colliding into anyone, never knocking anything over, as though he were holding himself together in case he had to leap.
