She walked down the wide stairs to the main floor of the compound, into a large room with high ceilings, walls of redwood, and huge windows looking out into the night. There were tables set with food, but people stood at them, not sitting—more milling around with glasses than eating—and groups clustered in conversation here and there. In other words, exactly what you'd expect at a tech party.
The room smelled like a cocktail of marijuana, perfume, and incense. Soft electronic music played. Mara found the room less intimidating than the stairway; she could tell where to stand so she wouldn't be in the way, and what to do with her hands.
