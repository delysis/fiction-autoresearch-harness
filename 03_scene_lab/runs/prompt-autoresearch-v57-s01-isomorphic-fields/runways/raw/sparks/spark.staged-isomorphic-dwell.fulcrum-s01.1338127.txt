They were in a redwood-and-glass pavilion near the central pond. At sunset it glowed with the firelight from a firepit on the stone patio outside the wide doors; the long table was set for dinner; guests mingled at a bar with an attendant who poured wine for anyone who approached, or a soft drink—and a few young men were drinking beer that had been provided.

A man in his thirties stood at one end of the table, leaning on the back of a chair and chatting quietly with three other people who stood in a circle around him; Mara knew it was Adrian from the photos online.
