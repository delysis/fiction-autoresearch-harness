At 7 p.m. on a Monday in January 2015, Adrian sat cross-legged on the wide floor of his living room, drinking sparkling water from a glass. It was the start of Fulcrum’s regular weekly welcome, and people were arriving at his house one after another. First a friend of an employee, then a guest, then two friends, and finally Mara, a young woman who had just applied for a job at Fulcrum.
