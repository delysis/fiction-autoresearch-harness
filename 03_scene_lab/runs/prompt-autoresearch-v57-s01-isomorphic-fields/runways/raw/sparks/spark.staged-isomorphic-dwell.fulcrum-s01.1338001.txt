Fulcrum’s main building rose from the campus like a redwood-and-glass cathedral, but it was the small wood-frame structures nearby that Mara passed first. She’d read that these were where the company’s founders lived during the early days—now they were used as art studios, for meditation retreats, and one held a Japanese garden and tea ceremony.
She could see through the glass doors and windows to a space that was bright, high-ceilinged, and more formal than she’d expected.
