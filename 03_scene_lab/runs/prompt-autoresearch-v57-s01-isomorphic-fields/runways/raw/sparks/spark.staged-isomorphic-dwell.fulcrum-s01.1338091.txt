She hadn’t heard of the company—Fulcrum. She had never seen their name on anything. They built an operating system, and Mara liked to know what was going on with operating systems, so when she saw they were throwing a party she checked it out. “Networking drinks.” A Silicon Valley startup holding a free open-bar at a downtown club.
