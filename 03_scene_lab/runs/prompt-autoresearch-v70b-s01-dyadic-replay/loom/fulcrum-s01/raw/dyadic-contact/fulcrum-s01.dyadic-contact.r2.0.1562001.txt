
The room was large, open, and quiet except for the white noise of the fountain in the corner. The broad windows opposite were high enough to obscure the view from outside, but she had no illusion that he wouldn’t be able to hear every sound she made, from footsteps on the polished wood floor to the way her breath moved her chest beneath the dress.
He didn’t speak or move again; he just lay there, still and apparently relaxed, watching her. She didn’t feel obliged to introduce herself.
Adrian was still at the meeting room off the common room; Mara had been able to see the sign on the door as she came in, and the lights were still on behind the frosted glass.
She looked at the blond man. He looked back at her, head still resting on his hand, other arm loosely supporting himself, legs crossed.
And then he smiled, just a little, lips not even really curling, and sat up straight.
