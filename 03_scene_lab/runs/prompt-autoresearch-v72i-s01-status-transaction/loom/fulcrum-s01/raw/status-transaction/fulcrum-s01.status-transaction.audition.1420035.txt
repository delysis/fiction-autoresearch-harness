“Mara!” cried Livia Crich, and everyone turned to look. Mara looked with them: a smallish person, this Livia, with straight brown hair past her shoulders and an apron over the orange sundress.

As she waved at Mara, Livia’s glass spilled in her hand; she laughed and tilted the bottle above her head, pouring more. “We’re having a little party,” she said, turning toward the group on the couch and raising her glass. “Toasts, everyone!”

The brown-haired young man raised his glass too and clinked it against hers. They both took a sip and looked at each other, and Livia set the glass down on a shelf.
“Speech, speech!” the young man cried. “Oh, let us have a toast to Fulcrum.”
