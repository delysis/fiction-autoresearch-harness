Her jaw tightened.

He did not move until her fingers reached the buttons.

Esther's hands found each other over his heart. She did not kiss him yet.

"Did you check?" she asked.

Her voice had become another surface.

Simon opened his mouth. No sound came. His lungs had stopped producing breath.

He moved his hand slowly over hers and pressed each of her knuckles until the pressure equalized.

Her left hand fell from the buttons.

Esther drew back. "Did you?"

Simon removed both his hands from her arms.
