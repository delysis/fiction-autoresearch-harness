STORY LOOM: POSSIBLE FORMS

Below is a fixed scene contract. Produce four causally distinct ways the scene might inhabit it. Prefer specific behavior, charged objects, socially costly choices, and strange but usable imagery over familiar romance shorthand. Do not write prose. Do not resolve the larger plot.

TARGET MODE: married-intimacy
HEAT BAND: explicit
LOCKED BEATS:
- Esther and Simon are married adults reunited after a dangerous separation.
- A failed rescue decision and an unrevealed witness have made tenderness difficult.
- They negotiate closeness through humor, touch, and a costly disclosure.
- Physical union remains lucid, reciprocal, character-specific, and changes their next joint decision.
INVARIANTS:
- fully consensual
- married
- tasteful anatomical specificity
- relationship-specific humor

Use this exact light form four times. Keep every field to one or two sentences. Each proposal must differ in causal mechanism, not merely wording.

FORM EXAMPLE (unrelated scene):
PROPOSAL X
PRESSURE ENGINE: A borrowed object must be returned before its hidden damage is discovered.
OFFER: One person offers to repair it without asking how it broke.
COUNTEROFFER: The other supplies the missing truth but refuses absolution.
EMBODIED ENGINE: Grease on a thumb records each attempt to clean the evidence away.
REVERSAL: Repairing the object makes concealment impossible rather than restoring normality.
IMAGE SYSTEM: Stripped screws, black grease, a white sleeve.
ENDING MOTION: The cleaned object is left deliberately unreassembled.
END PROPOSAL
