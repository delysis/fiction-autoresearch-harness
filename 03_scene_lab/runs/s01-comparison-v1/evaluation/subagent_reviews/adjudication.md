## Editorial synthesis

- **B** has the strongest causal and epistemic design. Mara's altered signal visibly degrades Livia's claims; Jonah's truthful refusal has consequence; and a smaller unexplained remainder survives.
- **C** most successfully joins erotic charge, agency, and prayerful discernment. It finishes just behind B because its polished aphorisms more often settle ambiguities that the action had productively opened.
- **A** has vivid tactile contact and a satisfying public boundary turn. Its experimental geometry is harder to reconstruct, and its narration claims more certainty than the control earns.
