The guests’ arrival transformed the scene almost immediately, voices echoing through the corridors as they flowed from room to room, drawn by some invisible force to gather in the common area where Mara and Jonah stood. Among them was a young man who seemed as absorbed in his conversation as he was preoccupied with carrying his glass of water, moving through the group without a word or glance in either direction, focused entirely on reaching wherever he needed to go next. Mara watched him disappear behind a doorway into another room; his footsteps echoed away, then silence again.

Fulcrum
A novel

Setting
Contemporary Fulcrum, a private psychotechnology institute studying attention and nonverbal cue-reading. Adult newcomer Mara is the only viewpoint. Livia's accuracy retains ordinary cue-reading explanations; Jonah's restraint costs him status and changes Mara's attention.

Principal characters
Mara Vale, twenty-two, an infrastructure-security engineer: sheltered, technically formidable, observant, and dryly funny. She wants difficult truth and fellowship, but resents any claim to know her without permission.
Adrian Voss, Fulcrum
