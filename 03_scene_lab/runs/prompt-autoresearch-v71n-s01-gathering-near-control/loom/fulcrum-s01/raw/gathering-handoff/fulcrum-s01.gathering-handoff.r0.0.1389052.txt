
There was only one other guest in the common room, now that Jonah had gotten off the couch, so Mara figured he probably meant to sit at the table with her. But she wasn’t certain, and he didn’t move.
“You’re not from around here,” he offered, reading her tag.
“No, I’m not.”
She tried again for the name she had seen on his badge, but it still wasn’t coming; her attention must have focused on his face and left the lanyard entirely out of frame. “What did you say your last name was?”
He smiled again.
“Reed. Jonah Reed.”
