# Primary literary review — S01 atom rewrites

## Disposition

Provisional ranking: **B > C > A**.

- **A:** accept as prose; reject as the production winner because the main
  counter-test changes both target and presentation, weakening the causal
  inference.
- **B:** accept for integration with immutable S02.
- **C:** accept as prose and retain as the strongest alternate; its method
  dialogue is excellent, but the calibration occupies more of the scene as an
  explicit audit and its residual blue-door hit is almost too perfectly shaped.

The primary reviewer did not author A or B. The primary reviewer authored C,
so C requires acceptance from both independent readers before it could win.

## A — gift/status

The first page is alive. “Fine,” she tells the buns; Adrian’s praise becomes a
gate she has accidentally passed; Livia turns a bakery box into flirtation and
rivalry. “The faction that owns the zester” is the first old-S01 joke that feels
written by Mara rather than supplied for her.

The wrist contact also contains real desire. Mara experiences “the sharp
private pleasure of Livia guessing wrong,” while Jonah’s last pressure event
appears on the trace without a corresponding pulse peak. The final unmatched
mark is concrete and appropriately unresolved.

The defect is experimental. Mara first holds the locked-door target, then
“pictured the church kitchen before dawn.” Livia’s miss therefore cannot isolate
the visible cue channel: content and expression change together. The scene
calls the result a control, but B and C actually perform cleaner controls. A’s
new one-word Jonah refusal repairs the required character beat, though it is
less organically braided into the scene than B’s “No performance.”

## B — somatic control

B has the strongest causal experiment. The machine supplies `REPEAT: ARRIVAL`;
Mara preserves “gravel releasing the wheels, cedar underfoot, the unopened
door” while reversing posture, exhale, knee direction, and gaze. Livia follows
the altered presentation and calls departure. The reader watches exactly what
changed and exactly what failed.

Character work is similarly exact:

- Livia’s read—“You did not plan for us to be impressed”—is accurate,
  flattering, invasive, and attractive at once.
- Jonah says “No performance,” loses the room’s laugh, and then reveals that
  his reluctance “did not extend to running its machinery.” His restraint and
  implication coexist before S02 exposes the deeper compromise.
- Mara’s desire arrives as “a practical suggestion—reduce the distance,
  improve the contact,” then becomes one input among several rather than a
  verdict.
- Adrian absorbs the deviation into Fulcrum—“Tomorrow we determine what she
  actually changed”—without making the result frictionless.

The residual beat is the main risk. Mara silently asks Christ what Livia saw;
Livia turns at the name. Because Mara inventories her unmoving hand and
unchanged breath, the event is concrete, but it leans nearer supernatural
confirmation than the rest of the story. Livia’s blood-sugar cover and multiple
ordinary possibilities keep it on the acceptable side.

B flows naturally into S02. It establishes sealed wrist telemetry, Livia’s
promise to repeat the test, Jonah’s technical access, and Mara’s accepted
place. An off-page repeat on day four cleanly supports S02’s references to
“this afternoon,” while S02’s “that first day” continues to refer to this
arrival exercise.

## C — method audit

C contains the best Mara–Livia exchange. Mara separates reflection, prior
knowledge, observation, inference, and feedback; Livia answers without shame:
“Confidence is how an observation gets enough time to become useful.” Mara’s
reply—“Or too expensive to question”—makes possible friendship, rivalry, and
methodological conflict operate in one exchange.

Its control is also sound. Mara holds the bolt image, changes breath, shoulders,
and contact surface, and watches Livia overclaim from the altered channel.
Prayer performs one attentional action: “The prayer did not remove the desire.
It returned the rest of the room.” Jonah’s desire becomes visible before he
contains it; his sensor signature and return to the workbench preserve
implication.

The main costs are density and elegance. The Silas deconstruction temporarily
turns the welcome into a very good methods seminar. Later Livia’s “Blue paint.
Opens inward” lands exactly on Mara’s childhood church door. The candidate
supplies ordinary explanations, but the hit is so thematically precise that it
can feel authored to satisfy the residual-mystery field. B’s stranger remainder
is less neat.

## Scaffold finding

All three scenes are unmistakably better than the inherited S01. The local
models supplied props and causal possibilities; sparse atom packets prevented
their explanatory prose distribution from crossing the novelist boundary.
The three realizations share locked beats without sharing sentences or event
roles. The key discriminator is no longer fluent compliance. It is whether the
chosen atom chain supports a real counterfactual experiment and characters
whose attraction, status, and institutional implication change what they do.
