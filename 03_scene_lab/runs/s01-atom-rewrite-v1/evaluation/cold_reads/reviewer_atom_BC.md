# Cold read — S01 candidates B and C

Read scope:

- `candidates/s01-b-somatic-control.codex.v1.md`
- `candidates/s01-c-method-audit.codex.v1.md`
- immutable seam reference `s02-compute-v4.3-a4b/frontier_novelist/s02.codex.v1.md`

## Disposition and ranking

1. **B — accept.** Strongest complete S01 of the two and the cleanest lead-in to the frozen S02.
2. **C — accept.** Excellent prose and the cleanest observation/inference audit, but its teaching becomes more visibly designed and its residual mystery is less restrained.

Both are usable S01 prose. Neither should be regenerated. B would benefit from one narrow anomaly repair; C from compression of the mid-scene audit and a smaller unexplained remainder.

## Candidate B — `s01-b-somatic-control.codex.v1.md`

**Verdict: accept.** The 3,027-word scene has a coherent dramatic engine: Fulcrum courts Mara, Livia accurately identifies what she wants, Jonah refuses one public performance while efficiently operating the apparatus, and Mara repeats the same private target while changing its visible correlates. The scene ends on freely accepted membership, shared food, sealed telemetry, and an unresolved pressure event.

### Character truth

Mara is concrete, technically alert, and funny without becoming precociously omniscient:

> “The driver found you. I supplied morale.”

> “Sealed from whom?” Mara asked.  
> “Participants.”  
> “Comforting use of the passive voice.”

Her attraction to Livia includes appetite, rivalry, and vulnerability:

> Livia laughed. It was a quick, delighted sound, and Mara wished she had caused it on purpose.

Livia is both unusually accurate and actively caring. Her read of Elias reaches beyond the visible calls and banking screen, but she stops using him once the point is made:

> “You can call him outside. No one here needs the fourth demonstration.”

Her read of Mara is tempting because it identifies desire rather than merely humiliating her:

> “You planned for us to condescend to you. You know how to survive that. You did not plan for us to be impressed.”

Jonah is especially well judged. His refusal costs him some ease with Adrian, but the narrative immediately shows that he is practiced and implicated in the machinery:

> “No performance.”

> Jonah connected a sensor lead to the silver terminal. He did it quickly enough to reveal long practice.

> Whatever reluctance he had displayed for the room did not extend to running its machinery.

That last sentence is slightly explanatory, but the underlying characterization is exactly right for S02.

### Heat and romantic impediment

The wrist contact changes knowledge and does not function as generic arousal decoration:

> Jonah’s thumb rested over her artery, wide enough that each beat seemed to meet him before it reached the rest of her.

> The desire arrived as a practical suggestion—reduce the distance, improve the contact, find out how his breathing changed when hers did.

The impediment is inseparable from the attraction: Jonah’s hand may be soothing Mara, feeding Livia, contaminating the trial, or doing all three. The best line of the dyad is:

> Mara could not tell whether the arc had calmed her, informed Livia, contaminated the trial, or accomplished all three.

There is no kiss or premature romantic settlement. Jonah closes the scene at the terminal, not at Mara’s side.

### Instructional action

The repeated `ARRIVAL` prompt creates a genuine control rather than a thematic demonstration. Mara preserves the content and reverses the visible evidence:

> She allowed her upper back to settle against the chair. She shortened her exhale. She turned her left knee three inches toward the room’s exit and let her eyes rest on the dark seam between two floorboards.

Livia then reports departure while the private word remains `ARRIVAL`. Mara reveals the unchanged target instead of giving a speech. Jonah identifies the pressure change, and Mara refuses to collapse the result into an accusation:

> “The channel is testable,” she said. “That’s enough for tonight.”

This is the strongest instruction-through-consequence in either candidate.

### Epistemic texture

The scene keeps the layers legible. Livia names specific observations, Mara identifies what those observations can support, Jonah’s pressure alters the channel, and no one acquires a total explanation. Livia’s response to uncertainty is characteristically hungry:

> Livia read the prompt. A flush moved across her cheekbones and disappeared. “Again.”

The sealed stream and Jonah’s covert replay establish a durable question:

> Across the room, Jonah was replaying the pressure trace. He enlarged one segment, checked who was watching, and closed it.

The weakest epistemic beat is the prayer-adjacent anomaly:

> Mara thought, without moving her mouth, *Jesus, what did she see?*  
> At the name, Livia turned.

The causal grammar, followed by explicit insistence that Mara made no gesture or breath change, overprivileges a telepathic reading. A glass reflection, movement elsewhere in the room, or a less exact temporal conjunction would preserve the anomaly without weighting it so heavily.

### Exact defects

1. **Residual anomaly is too psi-forward.** “At the name, Livia turned” treats an internal word as the apparent cause, then removes the obvious ordinary cues. This is the only material defect.
2. **Faith is underdeveloped as practice.** The name of Jesus appears at the anomaly, but Mara’s successful control comes entirely from technical bodily manipulation. This remains compatible with the story because S02 supplies the fuller prayer action, but S01 does not yet show much of her formation.
3. **Two narrator glosses could be cut.** “Whatever reluctance … did not extend to running its machinery” and “The distinction still placed the discovery inside his institution” explain implications already available in action.
4. **Livia’s first read approaches omniscience.** The rejected calls and banking screen support money and obligation; the brother, anger, and pleasure are a large inferential jump. Her tenderness afterward helps, but one more visible prior-context clue would strengthen the ordinary mechanism.

### S02 seam

The seam is excellent.

- B explicitly establishes that breath, pulse, pressure, room audio, and the wrist stream are recorded and “sealed during the cohort’s first month.” Four nights later, S02’s restricted raw-access gift follows directly.
- Jonah makes a repeated thumb arc, stops it during Mara’s control, calls it “baseline regulation,” and privately replays the trace. S02 can therefore ask the deeper question: not how he timed the pressure, but why he chose to apply it.
- B leaves Jonah at the machine and explicitly refuses to decide whether he soothed, informed, or contaminated the trial. S02’s later technical complicity does not reverse his characterization.
- Mara presses `ACCEPT PLACE`, receives a credential, and opens the buns. The S02 opening four nights later requires no bridge repair.
- Livia and Mara finish bargaining over access, with mutual appetite intact. The tea, almonds, raw-access gift, and refusal in S02 feel like the next move in an existing relationship.

The repeated question about Jonah’s pressure is not a contradiction. In B he answers the technical function, “baseline regulation”; in S02 Mara explicitly distinguishes timing from motive: “That’s how you timed it. Why did you do it?”

## Candidate C — `s01-c-method-audit.codex.v1.md`

**Verdict: accept.** The 2,809-word scene is fresher and slightly leaner than B. Its strongest contribution is Livia’s willingness to separate observation, context, inference, and post-claim feedback in public. The scene is less institutionally dense than B but remains fully compatible with S02.

### Character truth

Mara’s opening is immediate and particular:

> By the third switchback she had developed a method—kick, catch, lift, apologize to the buns—that would have embarrassed her mother and delighted the women who had packed the box after church.

Livia’s first boundary encounter is funny, acquisitive, and responsive rather than predatory:

> “Is there a liturgy?” she asked.  
> “You wait until the person who brought them offers,” Mara said.  
> Livia’s finger curled back. “Excellent. I almost failed the first test.”

Her central read of Mara is accurate enough to wound while remaining visibly constructed:

> “You want me to be excellent and still wrong about you.”

> “You worked very hard to arrive with nothing accidental … You want this place to discover that discipline made you unusual. And you’re afraid we’ll discover it only made you tidy.”

Livia becomes most compelling when she answers the audit without hiding her method:

> “His father is observation. His stomach is feedback after the claim.”

Jonah refuses to narrate Mara but does not become her declared protector:

> “Then tell us what you saw.”  
> “Ask her.”

He remains implicated through the repaired sensor, signed log, active pressure cue, and later return to the workbench.

### Heat and romantic impediment

The selective sensory writing is excellent:

> She smelled solder on his cuff and cardamom on her own skin.

> His fingers closed around her wrist. His thumb rested just below the cuff. The contact carried less surface than hers had and far more information: the dry pad of his thumb, a shallow nick near his forefinger, the exact pressure required to keep her from mistaking steadiness for possession.

The scene does not make Jonah’s restraint frictionless. Mara’s accurate door read exposes him, a joke visibly gathers, and he declines the laugh:

> “Close enough,” he said.  
> It cost him the laugh. Mara wanted him more for declining it, which made her distrust the wanting.

During Mara’s control, Jonah’s desire becomes observable without dictating action:

> His fingers tightened once. Desire moved visibly through his face before he put it away.

No kiss or private romantic walk occurs. The final two-finger wrist gesture remains recognition or warning.

### Instructional action

C dramatizes method before the wrist exercise. Mara reconstructs Livia’s evidence and asks where the father came from. Livia then distinguishes phone behavior, prior knowledge, reflected draft length, inference, and feedback. The exchange gives the reader a reusable audit:

> “So prior knowledge.”  
> “Context.”  
> “A larger cue channel.”  
> “You say that as if widening the channel is cheating.”  
> “I say it as if the width belongs in the result.”

The wrist control is bodily rather than discursive. Mara keeps the imagined bolt, changes breath and shoulders, increases contact, and allows desire to remain. Her brief prayer does not suppress it:

> *Jesus,* she thought, with neither eloquence nor alarm.  
> The prayer did not remove the desire. It returned the rest of the room.

She then names the event accurately and minimally:

> “That isn’t the same as receiving.”  
> “No. It’s a control.”

### Epistemic texture

C’s best epistemic passage is concrete and compact:

> Miriam’s screen showed pulse, not meaning. Livia watched both screen and hand. Jonah watched Mara. Adrian watched the watchers.

The control separates imagined content from breath, posture, surface area, pressure, and Livia’s interpretation. Livia’s post-control blue-door read restores genuine skill and prevents the scene from reducing her to fraud:

> “You kept the door,” she said. “Blue paint. Opens inward.”

However, this remainder is too specifically correct for the available evidence, and the following paragraph lists ordinary explanations in evaluator-like order. It maintains formal ambiguity but makes the scaffold visible.

### Exact defects

1. **The method audit is slightly too complete.** The long Silas exchange turns every layer into polished dialogue before the main experiment. It is intelligent and characterful, but it briefly feels like a worked example designed for the rubric.
2. **The blue-door remainder is oversized.** Exact color, direction, and childhood-church match approach paranormal confirmation after the cue control. Reduce it to one concrete detail or supply one stronger ordinary route.
3. **The ambiguity paragraph explains itself.** “She might have seen … might know … might have followed …” enumerates alternatives after the action. Trust the blue window, church context, and her shoulder movement.
4. **Miriam is introduced twice in effect.** Her observer-badge entrance is clean, but the later room-key handoff makes her part of Mara’s acceptance logistics without relational setup. This is minor and does help the S02 seam.
5. **Jonah’s implication is quieter than B’s.** The repaired sensor and signed log are good evidence, but the scene offers less pressure that his restraint may also be avoidance. S02 can supply this, so it is not a continuity failure.

### S02 seam

The seam is strong, though B is stronger.

- C establishes recorded pulse and pressure, Jonah’s thumb arc, a repaired sensor bearing his signature, and his return to the workbench. S02’s raw wrist stream, crooked repair, and later disclosure of technical responsibility extend these facts cleanly.
- Mara takes a logged room key and opens the bakery box. Four nights later requires no missing decision.
- Livia waits for Mara’s nod before taking a bun, admits method details, feels hurt when Mara withholds the substituted state, and asks for another test. Her S02 care-as-access maneuver feels like a believable escalation.
- Jonah’s final gesture can function as either recognition or warning, preserving the later revelation that private restraint did not make him innocent.
- C does not state that the raw stream is restricted for the first month. S02 introduces that restriction without contradiction, but B plants it more elegantly.

## Final comparison

**B wins narrowly.** Its repeated-target control is exceptionally legible without becoming a lecture, its Jonah is more deeply implicated, and its sealed-stream/access bargain creates a nearly invisible seam into S02. C has the fresher sentence-level prose and the best explicit method audit, but the audit and blue-door remainder both show more scaffold pressure.

Recommended disposition: advance both to blind appraisal, with B seeded as the editorial favorite. If only one receives a bounded polish pass, choose B and repair only the `At the name, Livia turned` anomaly plus the two explanatory narrator sentences.
