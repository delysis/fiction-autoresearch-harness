Mara turned onto her right side, elbows and heels touching the cool floor. She knew the sound of his feet; it was the same as when he had gone through that door thirty minutes ago. Her eyes remained closed. If she opened them now, she would have to say something, and she wasn’t ready yet. She might never be ready.

“How’s that?” he said.

She exhaled. The sound of his voice was different when he was out here, but she still didn’t open her eyes. She was starting to feel a little dizzy. Her hands were beginning to ache from the weight of her body. “It’s fine.”

“Are you sure?” he said.

She took a breath and then exhaled again. Then she opened her eyes.
