Outside on the sidewalk, Jonah held the door open with his hand and waited. She felt his gaze, but he wasn't looking at her face. He stood still while she put on her coat, then slid the chain across the door. The air outside was cold and still. She noticed how slowly her breath came now.

Jonah stepped beside her onto the sidewalk. "I'll walk you home," he said, not asking. The streetlights threw long shadows across the empty pavement. The buildings rose dark and massive along the silent street.

"Did you see it?" she asked suddenly, turning toward him. "When I—" She broke off. There was no need to explain further. His eyes met hers briefly before dropping back to the sidewalk.

"You mean when you started talking about your ex-husband? And then stopped." He didn't ask for details. Instead, he said, "I saw."

She nodded, thinking of his expression as her words dried up. The way his face had changed, from relaxed to something sharp and careful.
