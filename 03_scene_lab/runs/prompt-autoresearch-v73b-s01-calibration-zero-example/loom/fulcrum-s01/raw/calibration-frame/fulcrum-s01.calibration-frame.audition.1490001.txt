At the kitchen entrance a second later, a male catering worker opened the door for his elder. He stood back to let her into the kitchen; he waited to close the door behind her; and he left the door closed after she entered, so that nobody else could wander in.

The other two men in the common room seemed unaware that this exchange had occurred, and Mara wasn’t going to tell them, but she still felt a kind of mild surprise. The catering worker must have seen Jonah set his wine on the table; she could hardly miss him. So why did this old woman bother with the drink?

If nobody is coming here for dinner tonight, why would the woman pick up an unemptied glass before it could be drunk and bring it back to the kitchen?
