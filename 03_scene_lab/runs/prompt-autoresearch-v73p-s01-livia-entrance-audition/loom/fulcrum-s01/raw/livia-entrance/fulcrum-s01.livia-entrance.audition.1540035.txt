 He glanced down at her hand for only a moment, still smiling, and reached around to lift a curtain that covered a large rectangular object against the wall. It was an ordinary door, clearly not the door to this room. He opened it. Jonah was right beside him, holding the plastic cup; Mara followed him closely and peered over his shoulder to see what was through the door.
A plain white room, long, about ten feet wide, and completely bare of furniture or decoration, with another identical door on the far end.
“This is our sensory deprivation room,” Jonah said beside her. “I didn’t think there were any here, actually.”
