He smiled. “Just a little more, my dear.” He lifted her hand away from his and held up her chosen object, a flat white stone; she recognized it from the previous round of examination, a chunk of smooth limestone about two inches square and one eighth of an inch thick.

He turned his gaze from Mara to Jonah, held up the stone for him to see, and said in a whisper, “It is not quite so simple. Look closely. What do you see?”
Jonah peered down at it with no sign of comprehension.
Adrian leaned in closer and lowered his voice further. “What kind of stone do you see here?”

And that was when a new arrival walked into the room.
